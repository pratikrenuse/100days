Watch each diagram draw itself every time you open your Mac.

The live wallpaper picks one of the 100 at random, draws the diagram in, then settles the quote. Close the lid, open it again, and a new one draws in.

  1. Install Plash, a free app from the Mac App Store that turns any web page into your wallpaper.
  2. In Plash, choose Add Website and paste the link below.
  3. Leave it running. A new wallpaper draws in on every wake, and every 30 minutes.

Get a new one every time you open your Mac
  1. Unzip the download and keep the folder somewhere permanent, like Pictures.
  2. Open System Settings, Wallpaper, then Add Folder and pick that folder.
  3. Set it to change when waking from sleep and turn on random order.
