For organisers: speaker bio

Pratik Renuse is a growth marketing leader, TEDx speaker and startup mentor based in Pune, India. He has been on the founding team of three startups, including Infento, where he led the $848K Kickstarter that became the most-funded STEM project globally in 2018. He mentors founders at Techstars, EBRD Star Venture and Seedstars, teaches as visiting faculty at Masters' Union and Tetr College of Business, and has delivered 50+ keynotes in 14 countries, including three TEDx talks. His focus today is how AI changes marketing and growth.
