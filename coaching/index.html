How I coach

Most career advice starts with job boards. I start with you: what drives you, what drains you and what success means in your own words.

Then we look outward at roles that fit, and build a plan you can act on the same week.

  • Find what drives you
  • Define your own success
  • Execute, step by step

That is the map I coach from. Not theory, but mistakes I already made so you do not have to.
