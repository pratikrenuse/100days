# Image Style (v2) -- cinematic photographic

Scrapped the coded "engraved gold" look. Every post now gets ONE realistic, cinematic, photographic image, generated (Midjourney / DALL-E / Higgsfield / any text-to-image model) from the prompts below. No wordmark, no name on the image, no stats, no charts.

---

## The one principle (unchanged): one honest idea, shown simply
The image shows ONE true, concrete thing from the post, made real and cinematic. Almost always: something small, precise, and human, shot beautifully, so the future the post describes feels like it is already here. The power comes from realism and a single strong subject, not from graphic tricks.

Do NOT: put text on the image, add data, fuse two objects into a gimmick, or make it look like clip art or a stock template.

---

## The house look (keep this consistent across every post, so the feed feels like one brand)
Paste this style block into the END of every prompt:

> cinematic editorial photograph, hyperrealistic, shot on 85mm, shallow depth of field, dramatic low-key lighting with a warm rim light, deep near-black background, fine detail, subtle film grain, moody and premium, muted palette with a single warm gold-amber glow as the only bright accent, 4:5 vertical, no text, no words, no watermark

- **Format:** 4:5 vertical (1080 x 1350) for LinkedIn.
- **Palette:** dark, moody, cinematic. One warm gold-amber light source as the only strong colour, so it still ties loosely to the brand without being a gold graphic.
- **Subject:** always ONE clear hero, centered or rule-of-thirds, lots of negative space, legible in a glance (squint test).
- **No text baked in.** The caption/hook lives in the LinkedIn copy, not on the image.

## Per-piece prompts (subject + the house look block)

**Biology -- "The End of Discovery by Luck":**
> Extreme close-up of a single glowing translucent DNA double helix forming in mid-air above an open human palm in a darkened lab, delicate strands of amber light, precise and deliberate, a sense of something being designed rather than found. [+ house look block]

Alt subject: a lone petri dish on a dark bench with a perfect luminous helix of light rising out of it.

**Robots -- "The Robot in Your Kitchen":**
> A humanoid robot hand and a human hand almost touching, fingertips lit by a single warm light, in a quiet home kitchen at night, tender and cinematic, the machine hand precise and matte, the human hand soft. [+ house look block]

**Energy -- the turbine / power ceiling:**
> A single industrial gas turbine standing alone in a vast dark hall, one warm shaft of light on it, a faint glowing city skyline reflected behind, dwarfed by the machine. [+ house look block]

**Stack / interconnect (Astera, cables):**
> A macro shot of one thin fiber-optic strand carrying a bead of amber light between two dark server racks, everything else in shadow. [+ house look block]

**Rare earths / geopolitics (MP Materials):**
> A single small rare-earth magnet on dark stone holding up a suspended glowing globe of light, quiet and powerful. [+ house look block]

**Orbital compute (Starcloud):**
> A lone satellite with solar wings catching warm sunlight against the black of space, Earth a soft glow below. [+ house look block]

Each post gets a fresh subject; never reuse the exact composition.

## How to produce it (until an image-gen tool is connected here)
Right now this session has no connected realistic image generator, so I hand over the finished PROMPT and Pratik runs it in his tool of choice (Midjourney, DALL-E, Higgsfield, Krea, etc.), or connects an image MCP so I can generate directly. When generating: render 3 to 4 variations, pick the one with the cleanest single subject and best light, upscale, done.
