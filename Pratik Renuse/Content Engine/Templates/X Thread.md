# X Thread -- adapter

STATUS: spec stub. To be built out next, from the GE and Astera stories, then locked with two worked examples the way the LinkedIn template was.

Purpose: the same story as a Twitter/X thread. Same voice, reshaped into short numbered posts where each one earns the tap to the next.

## What makes a thread different (shape changes, not voice)
- **Post 1 is everything.** It must work alone and make people open the thread. Strongest single line of the whole story, no setup.
- **~280 characters per post.** Each post is one beat. No post is filler.
- **Each post ends slightly open** so the reader taps "show more" (the slippery slide, at the post level).
- **Numbers get their own post** when they're strong enough to stop a scroll.
- **Last post is the shift close** plus a soft follow line.
- Typically 7 to 9 posts (matches the eight beats, sometimes splitting the data beat).

## Intended mapping (to finalise from GE + Astera)
1. Hook, standalone (the surprise)
2. The reaction + "so I went digging"
3. The plain explanation
4. The hidden player, named
5. The strongest number
6. The second number / kicker
7. The realization
8. The concrete forecast
9. The shift close + follow

## Same hard rules as every channel
Write for a normal person, no jargon, no em dashes, no emojis, real reactions, say each point once. If it doesn't sound like the GE post, it's wrong.

## Output format
Numbered posts (1/, 2/, ...), each under 280 characters, blank line between. Note the character count of post 1.
