# Eaton - Fifteen Years of Waiting -- HeyGen script

America already has fifteen years of data centre construction on order. Fifteen years.

Not planned. Ordered. By people who've spent enough money that somebody quoted them the electrical gear.

That number comes from a company called Eaton. They make the boring boxes between the power station and the server rack. Switchgear, transformers, backup power. Nothing turns on without them.

And because they have to quote all that before a building even exists, their order book is basically a thermometer for the entire AI build. Last Friday, their chief executive put the American backlog at three hundred and seven gigawatts. That's about fifteen years of work at the rate the industry actually managed last year.

Three months ago? That number was twelve years.

Now, the honest bit. Only about a fifth of that converts any time soon. The rest is twenty twenty eight and beyond, and some of it never gets built at all.

But here's where it lands. Today, the scarce thing is a chip. Tomorrow, it's a place in the line.
