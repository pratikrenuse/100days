# Etched - The Chip That Can Only Do One Thing -- HeyGen script

A chip company was just valued at ten point three billion dollars for making something that can only do one thing.

The company is Etched. And its chip runs exactly one kind of AI, the kind underneath ChatGPT. Build a model any other way and the chip just sits there.

Sounds like a terrible idea. Except that is the entire point. Every other AI chip has to keep space free for whatever comes next, because nobody knows what the next big model looks like. That space is insurance, and you pay for it every single day in speed. Etched cancelled the insurance. They say the result runs about twenty times faster than Nvidia's H one hundred.

Computers have done this before. Graphics got its own chip. Video got its own chip. Bitcoin mining got its own chip. Once a job stops changing, the money leaves the flexible machine.

Oh, and SK Hynix, the company that supplies Nvidia's memory, just invested in it.

Today the winner is the chip that can do anything, because we are still guessing what comes next. Tomorrow it is the chip that stopped guessing first.
