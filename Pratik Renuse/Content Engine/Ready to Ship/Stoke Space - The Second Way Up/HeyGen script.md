# Stoke Space - The Second Way Up -- HeyGen script

Every plan to put data centres in space depends on one assumption nobody says out loud. That a single company keeps making launch cheaper.

And the space idea isn't as daft as it sounds. Up there, a solar panel is up to eight times more productive, because there's no night and no weather. And cooling is free. Space does it for you.

The paperwork is already filed. SpaceX has applied for up to a million satellites. A six person startup filed for a hundred thousand. Nvidia is designing the computer that flies on the first one.

But all of it hangs on one number. What it costs to lift a kilo off this planet. And that number has basically been one company's plan.

Until last week. Stoke Space raised a billion dollars to build a rocket where both stages come back, not just the first one. First flight, early twenty twenty seven.

One thing to be clear about. Stoke hasn't reached orbit yet. First launches fail all the time.

Today, leaving Earth costs whatever one firm decides. Tomorrow, it's a market price. And once it's a price, somebody quotes it for a data centre.
