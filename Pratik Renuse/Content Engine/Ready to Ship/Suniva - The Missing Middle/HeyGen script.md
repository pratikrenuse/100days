# Suniva - The Missing Middle -- HeyGen script

America puts up a lot of solar panels. It barely makes the part inside that turns light into electricity.

Because a panel isn't one thing. Sand becomes silicon, the silicon becomes a block, the block gets sliced into wafers, the wafers become cells, and the cells get laminated into the panel on the roof. The cell is the bit that does the actual physics. Everything after it is packaging.

And for years, America funded the last step. Assembly lines are cheap and quick to build. So the country built panel capacity out of cells it bought from somewhere else.

Now a company called Suniva has closed eight hundred and thirty five million dollars to change that. One plant in South Carolina takes it from one gigawatt of cells to five and a half.

It doesn't open until twenty twenty eight, mind you. So it does nothing for the shortage happening right now.

But it's the same lesson as rare earths. We had the mines and not the refining. We had the assembly and not the cells.

Today, a country counts the end of the line it can see. Tomorrow, it counts the middle.
