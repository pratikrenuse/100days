# Agility Robotics - Labour Gets a Ticker -- HeyGen script

America's first humanoid robot stock is coming. Ticker, A G L T. And the CEO's boldest promise? That she's promising you nothing. No robot butlers. No android in your kitchen. Peggy Johnson of Agility Robotics says her robot Digit does warehouse work, gets paid, and that's the pitch.

The deal values Agility at about two and a half billion dollars and raises over six hundred and twenty million, the biggest pot of money in humanoid robotics ever. Foxconn, the iPhone maker, put in two hundred million. Digit already has over three hundred million dollars in signed orders, and there's a factory in Oregon ready to build ten thousand robots a year.

Meanwhile China just approved Unitree, the world's volume leader, for its own listing. Two robot stocks, two continents, a few weeks apart.

And that flips how we'll judge robots. A public company can't hide behind demo videos. It reports costs and breakdowns every quarter. So today, robots are highlight reels. Tomorrow, they're earnings reports. The hype era just got an accountant.
