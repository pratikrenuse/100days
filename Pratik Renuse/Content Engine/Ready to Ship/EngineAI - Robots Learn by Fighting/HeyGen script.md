# EngineAI - Robots Learn by Fighting -- HeyGen script

Last week in Shenzhen, a robot got kicked in the head, and kept fighting with its head hanging by a cable. That clip went everywhere. The story behind it is better.

It was opening night of the world's first fighting league for full-size humanoid robots. Thirty two teams, a prize pool of about one point four million dollars, and every team fights on the same machine, built by a company called EngineAI. Barely anyone knows the name.

Why make robots fight? Because factory demos are choreographed. A fight isn't. Another machine is trying to knock yours down, so balance, toughness, and fast repairs get tested for real, in public. And the audience pays for it. The league is built as a sport whose ticket money funds the engineering.

Cars did the same thing. A century of racing hardened the brakes and engines in your driveway.

So here's where I land. Today, robots prove themselves with perfect demos. Tomorrow, with the hits they survive. The arena is the new laboratory.
