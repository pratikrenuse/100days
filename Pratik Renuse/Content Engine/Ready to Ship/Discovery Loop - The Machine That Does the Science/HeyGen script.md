# Discovery Loop - The Machine That Does the Science -- HeyGen script

A company started this week, and its product isn't an app, or a model, or a chip. Its product is the scientific method. Running on its own, with nobody watching.

It's called Discovery Loop. And the loop is the one every scientist has run since the sixteen hundreds. Have an idea worth testing. Test it. Look at what came back. Pick the next one.

Machines already do the testing. What's never been handed over is the choosing. There's always somebody sitting there deciding what's worth trying next, and everything else waits on them.

That's the seat they're emptying.

But here's the part that got me. Look at where they aim it first. Not cancer. Not batteries. They're pointing it at AI research itself, with the company as its own first customer.

Because that's the one field where an experiment costs almost nothing, the answer lands in hours, and what comes out is a better version of the thing that asked.

Now, nothing here has been shown to anyone yet. A machine that only marks its own homework is just an expensive hall of mirrors.

Today, how fast we discover things depends on how many brilliant people are alive. Tomorrow, on how many lab benches we can build.
