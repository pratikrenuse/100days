Roughly half the AI hardware on earth is idle at any given moment. Powered up, paid for, waiting for data to show up. That is the industry's own estimate of itself.

..

An AI model does not run on one chip. It runs on thousands, and they have to keep agreeing with each other.

Each chip works out a piece of the answer, sends it off, then sits there until the combined total comes back. Do that millions of times a day and the waiting becomes most of the day.

The network in between carries thousands of separate little answers, untouched, and the expensive chips do the adding up at both ends.

..

Cornelis Networks raised 205 million dollars on 14 September to rearrange that. Put a small amount of computing power inside the network, and let it add the numbers up while they are already in motion.

It does more than add. It can squeeze data down before it travels, and work out which chips need to see which part of a question.

One finished answer arrives instead of thousands of fragments. Their marketing chief said it better than I can: the payload does not arrive the way it left.

They are the team that built Intel's Omni-Path and walked out in 2020. Qualcomm signed on the same day the money was announced.

..

The number that stopped me is the cost split. By their reckoning the network is about 15 percent of what an AI system costs, and it decides what you get out of the other 85.

It is not only that chips are hard to make. The ones already bought spend half their life waiting for something to arrive.

Hold all of that loosely. The idle figure and the cost split are Cornelis's own framing, and their headline claim of up to 50 percent less traffic comes from their own simulations, on parts that reach customers later this year.

..

For three years the answer to needing more computing has been to buy more computing.

The cheapest computer on earth is the one somebody already paid for and never used.

Today AI is sold by the chip. Ten years out it gets sold by the hours those chips actually work, and whoever recovers those hours collects on every one of them.
