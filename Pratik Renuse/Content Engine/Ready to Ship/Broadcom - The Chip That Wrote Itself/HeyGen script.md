# Broadcom - The Chip That Wrote Itself -- HeyGen script

An AI just helped design the chip it will run on next.

In late June, OpenAI revealed its first ever chip of its own, called Jalapeño. Designing a chip like this normally takes years. This one was ready for the factory in nine months, because OpenAI's own AI did part of the engineering.

But why? OpenAI already buys the best chips on Earth. Well, those chips are wasteful at the one job that pays the bills: answering you. This new one does only that, at roughly half the cost. At a billion answers a day, that's found money.

And the partner behind it is the company nobody names. Broadcom. It has quietly co-designed chips for Google and the other giants for years. Now OpenAI too. Every big lab is starting to mint its own chips, and this one quiet firm helps nearly all of them.

So here's what I think happens. Today, everyone queues outside one chip company's door. Tomorrow, every lab designs its own, half by machine. And the quiet company helping them all? It collects rent on every single one.
