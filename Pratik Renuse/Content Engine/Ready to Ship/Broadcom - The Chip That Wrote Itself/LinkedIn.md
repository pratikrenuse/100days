An AI just helped design the chip it will run on next.

In late June, OpenAI unveiled Jalapeño, its first ever chip of its own, built with a partner most people could not name: Broadcom.

A project like this normally takes years. This one went from blank page to ready-for-the-factory in nine months, and OpenAI says its own AI models did part of the engineering. The companies call it possibly the fastest chip project of its kind, ever.

Nine months. To design the machine that runs the machine.

..

Why would OpenAI even bother? It already buys the best chips on Earth.

Because the best all-purpose chip is wasteful at the one job that pays the bills: answering. Every single ChatGPT reply burns electricity and money. Jalapeño is built for that job alone, and Broadcom's CEO claims it cuts the cost roughly in half. First units arrive late this year, full scale by 2028.

Cut the price of an answer in half, at a billion answers a day, and you have found money on an absurd scale.

..

And the partner standing next to them is the better story.

Broadcom has quietly co-designed in-house chips for the biggest tech companies for years. Google's, among others. Now OpenAI's. One of the most valuable firms on the planet, and still the least famous one in the room.

Google has its own chips. Amazon has its own. Meta too, and now OpenAI. When the famous layer starts building its own boring layer, the message is simple: that is where the margin went. And the money does not vanish, it slides one floor down, to whoever does the designing.

..

The loop is the real story. AI helped design Jalapeño. Jalapeño will run the AI that designs the next one, faster. Chips took three years to create when humans did it alone. Nine months with AI in the loop. The next one, less.

Today, every AI lab queues outside one company's door for chips. Tomorrow, each lab mints its own, designed half by machine, and the quiet firm helping them all collects rent on every single one.
