# Humanoid - They Took the Legs Off -- HeyGen script

A robot company in London just raised a hundred and fifty two million dollars. And its robot has no legs.

I had to know why. Turns out the answer is sitting in the investor list.

Two of the names are Bosch and Schaeffler. Car parts companies. And neither of them is just writing a cheque. Schaeffler is the customer, it is putting thousands of these robots into its own factories. Schaeffler is also the parts supplier, it makes over half the robot's joints. And Bosch is the factory that actually builds the thing.

The investor, the buyer and the parts maker. All the same people.

So, no legs. Why? Because walking on two legs eats up most of a robot's brainpower just keeping it from falling over. Put it on wheels and all of that goes into the hands instead.

Meanwhile Tesla told investors last week it has still built exactly zero of its walking robots.

Today we are all waiting for a machine that moves like a person. Tomorrow the ones actually doing the work roll straight past us, and nobody looks up.
