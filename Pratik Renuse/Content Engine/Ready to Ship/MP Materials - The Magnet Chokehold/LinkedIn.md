We keep hearing China might choke off our computer chips.

But this week I found the thing they could really squeeze. And it's tiny.

It's a magnet. The kind that fits in your palm.

Sounds harmless, right? It isn't.

..

That little magnet is the strongest kind ever made, and it's the muscle inside almost everything that moves.

The motor in an electric car. The spin of a wind turbine. The joints of a robot. Even the fins that steer a missile. None of them work without it.

And here's the catch. These magnets are made from rare earth metals, and one country refines almost all of them. China makes roughly 90% of the world's supply.

So whoever controls the magnet controls the machine.

..

Which is why one American company suddenly matters so much: MP Materials.

It digs up rare earths at the only big mine in the country, a place called Mountain Pass. Barely anyone had heard of it. Then the phone started ringing.

- Apple signed a 500 million dollar deal to buy its magnets
- The Pentagon put in 400 million and guaranteed a price, so America never runs dry
- Its stock went from a 2 billion dollar company to around 13 billion

Not bad for a company that digs up dirt.

..

So the race everyone's watching, the one for smarter chips, has a quiet twin. A race for the stuff those machines are physically made of.

And it only gets tighter. Every robot and electric car we're promised needs pounds of these magnets, and a new mine takes a decade to build. This year, MP starts making the magnets itself, on US soil, at scale.

..

Today, the company that matters isn't a chipmaker. It's a miner most people can't name.

Because the future doesn't run on software alone. It runs on stuff you can hold. And right now, one country makes almost all of it.

FACT-CHECK NOTES (sources, mid-2026):
- China refines ~85 to 90% of rare earths and makes ~90%+ of the world's rare-earth magnets (IEA: 91% separation/refining, 94% sintered magnets, 2024).
- Neodymium (rare-earth) magnets are the strongest permanent magnets; used in EV motors, wind turbines, robotics, defense (missiles, jets).
- MP Materials: FY2025 revenue $275.6M (+35%); market cap risen from ~$2B to ~$13B; runs Mountain Pass, the largest US rare-earth mine.
- Apple $500M magnet deal; DoD $400M package with a ~$110/kg NdPr price floor (about double Chinese prices), 10-year purchase.
- MP's Independence (Texas) magnet plant begins production H2 2026.
- Sources: Fortune, MINING.com, SEC filings, IEA critical minerals.
