# MP Materials -- HeyGen script

We keep hearing China might cut off our computer chips. But this week I found the thing they could really squeeze, and it's tiny. It's a magnet, the kind that fits in your palm.

Don't laugh. That little magnet is the strongest ever made, and it's the muscle inside almost everything that moves: electric cars, wind turbines, robots, even the fins that steer a missile.

And here's the catch. These magnets are made from rare earth metals, and China refines about 90% of them. So whoever controls the magnet controls the machine.

Which is why one American company suddenly matters: MP Materials. It runs the only big rare earth mine in the country. Then the phone rang. Apple signed a 500 million dollar deal, the Pentagon put in 400 million and guaranteed a price, and its stock jumped from a 2 billion dollar company to around 13 billion.

So the real race isn't just for smarter chips. It's for the stuff those machines are actually made of. And every robot we're promised needs pounds of these magnets.

Today, the company that matters isn't a chipmaker. It's a miner most people can't name. Because the future doesn't run on software alone. It runs on stuff you can hold, and right now, one country makes almost all of it.
