# Rocket Lab -- HeyGen script

Name a space company. You said SpaceX, didn't you? Almost everyone does. But there's a second one, launching rockets nearly every two weeks, and hardly anyone knows its name.

It's called Rocket Lab. While the world watched Elon, it quietly became the second busiest rocket company in America. Most companies chase giant rockets. Rocket Lab started with a small one, called Electron, and got very, very good at flying it. It's now the second most launched rocket in the country, behind only SpaceX.

And demand is exploding, because suddenly everyone needs to be in space. Phone companies want satellites for signal. Armies want them for eyes. So the money is pouring in. Last year Rocket Lab flew 21 missions, every one a success. Its sales just jumped 64% in a quarter. And it's sitting on 2.2 billion dollars of orders it hasn't even filled.

And the big leap is coming. It's building a much bigger rocket, called Neutron, aimed to fly by the end of this year, to go after the launches SpaceX now wins by default.

So today, the quiet winner builds small rockets, fast and often. Tomorrow, it wants the big ones too. Because the next great industry isn't being built on Earth. It's being built a few hundred miles above it, one launch at a time.
