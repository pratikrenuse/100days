Name a space company. Go on.

You said SpaceX, didn't you? Almost everyone does.

But there's a second one, launching rockets nearly every two weeks, and hardly anyone knows its name.

..

It's called Rocket Lab. And while the world watched Elon, it quietly became the second busiest rocket company in America.

Here's how. Most companies chase giant rockets. Rocket Lab started with a small one, called Electron, and got very, very good at flying it. It's now the second most launched rocket in the country, behind only SpaceX.

And demand is exploding, because suddenly everyone needs to be in space. Phone companies want satellites for signal. Armies want them for eyes. Governments want their own, so they don't depend on anyone else.

..

Which is why the money is pouring in.

- Last year Rocket Lab flew 21 missions, every single one a success
- Its sales just jumped 64% in a single quarter
- It's sitting on 2.2 billion dollars of orders it hasn't even filled yet

For a company most people have never heard of.

..

So space isn't a one-company world anymore. It's turning into an industry, with a clear number two.

And the big leap is coming. Rocket Lab is building a much bigger rocket, called Neutron, aimed to fly by the end of this year. If it works, it can carry the heavy stuff, and go after the launches SpaceX now wins almost by default.

..

Today, the quiet winner in space builds small rockets, fast and often. Tomorrow, it wants the big ones too.

Because the next great industry isn't being built on Earth. It's being built a few hundred miles above it, one launch at a time.
