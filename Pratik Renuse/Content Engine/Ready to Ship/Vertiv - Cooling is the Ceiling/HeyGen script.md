# Vertiv -- HeyGen script

Everyone talks about how smart AI is getting. But nobody asks where all that heat goes.

So this week, I followed the heat. And it led somewhere weird. Not to a chip, but to a company that basically air-conditions computers.

See, an AI chip runs so hot it would cook itself in seconds. A whole rack throws off as much heat as a hundred microwaves at once. Fans used to handle it, but these machines got so hot that air just gave up. You'd basically need a hurricane. So now they run water, real water, right against the chips.

And almost nobody builds that plumbing like a company called Vertiv. You've probably never heard of it. But get this. Its backlog just hit 15 billion dollars, up 80% in a year. And its stock jumped nearly 30 times since 2020.

And it only gets hotter. By 2027, these chips run four times hotter, and water won't be enough. So they'll dunk them straight into liquid.

So here's what stuck with me. Today, the AI race is won with water. Tomorrow, by whoever can drown a chip and keep it alive. Because building a genius is easy. Keeping it from melting? That's the real race.
