And here's what makes that interesting. Two weeks ago, Nvidia was reportedly backing a different power project with its own credit, because OpenAI couldn't borrow that much alone. That was lending. This is owning. Lending, you can walk away from. Owning, you can't.

See, chips get manufactured. Buildings get poured. What you can't make on demand is land that's already plugged into the grid. The wait for that runs years now.
