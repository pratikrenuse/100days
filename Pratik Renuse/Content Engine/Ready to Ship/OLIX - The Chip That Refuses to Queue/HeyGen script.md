# OLIX - The Chip That Refuses to Queue -- HeyGen script

Almost everyone building AI right now is stuck in the same two queues. And this week, somebody walked out of both.

So, the AI processors everyone wants need a special kind of memory sitting right beside them. That memory is sold out. Not scarce. Sold out through next year, with buyers sending money before there's a contract. And the step that glues the memory onto the processor? Also full.

Then a two year old London company raised three hundred and twelve million dollars for a chip that needs neither one. On purpose.

They say it keeps the model inside the processor itself, so no special memory. No gluing step. And the chips talk to each other using light instead of copper wire.

The company is OLIX. Arm backed it. So did the guy who co-founded Netflix.

Now, nothing's shipped. Customers don't get chips until late twenty twenty seven, and nobody outside has tested any of it.

But look at what they're saying. For three years, the money went to whoever owned the part you couldn't get. Hardly anyone asked whether you needed the part.

Today, you win by owning the scarce thing. Tomorrow, by designing it out.
