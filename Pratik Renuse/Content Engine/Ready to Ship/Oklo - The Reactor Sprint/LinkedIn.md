Sometime this month, a brand-new kind of nuclear reactor in Texas is scheduled to come alive for the first time.

Not in the 2030s. This month.

The company is called Oklo. On July 1, the US Department of Energy approved the final safety analysis for its Groves test reactor. One readiness review left, then fuel goes in.

..

For my whole life, American nuclear ran on one law: a new reactor takes ten to twenty years of paperwork before anyone pours concrete. That is why everyone quit trying.

Then AI arrived, hungry for power that does not exist.

Data centres are on track to nearly double their electricity use by 2030, toward 945 terawatt hours a year. The gas turbines that were supposed to cover the gap? Sold out until 2029, with buyers paying 10 to 20% more each quarter just to hold a place in line.

So Washington built a fast lane. A federal pilot program with one blunt goal: get at least three new-style reactors switched on around July 4, 2026. Approvals in months, not decades.

Oklo is at the front of that sprint.

..

The company itself is a strange and clever thing. Sam Altman took it public in 2024. It does not sell reactors at all. It sells electricity: build a small plant next to a data centre, sign a long-term power deal, keep the machine.

Its first commercial unit broke ground in Idaho last September, aiming for first power around 2027 to 2028. Behind it, about 22 gigawatts of small-reactor projects are in development across the US. Amazon alone wants 5 gigawatts of them by 2039.

..

The realization, for me, is that the scarce thing was never uranium or engineering. It was permission. And permission just got a speed limit raised.

These are bets, not certainties. A test reactor is not a power plant, and this one has not split its first atom yet.

..

But the direction is set. Today, the AI boom runs on gas turbines you cannot buy before 2029. Tomorrow, a data centre orders a reactor the way it orders servers, delivery in two years, power for forty.

We spent half a century treating the atom as a last resort. AI just made it the schedule's favourite.
