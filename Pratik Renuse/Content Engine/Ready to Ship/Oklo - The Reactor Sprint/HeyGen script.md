# Oklo - The Reactor Sprint -- HeyGen script

Sometime this month, a brand-new kind of nuclear reactor in Texas is scheduled to switch on for the first time. Yeah, this month. Not the 2030s.

The company is called Oklo. On July first, the US government approved its final safety review. One check left, then the fuel goes in.

For decades, a new American reactor meant ten to twenty years of paperwork. Everyone gave up. Then AI showed up, needing almost double the electricity by 2030, and the gas turbines meant to cover the gap are sold out until 2029.

So the government built a fast lane. Three new reactors, switched on by this summer. Approvals in months.

And Oklo is a strange one. Sam Altman took it public, and it doesn't sell reactors. It sells the electricity, a small plant built right next to your data centre. Amazon alone wants five gigawatts of these by 2039.

So here's where I land. Today, AI runs on turbines you can't buy. Tomorrow, a data centre orders its own reactor like it orders servers. The atom was our last resort. It's about to become the default.
