# Fab2 - The Factory That Makes Factories -- HeyGen script

A company that has never sold a single chip just raised five hundred million dollars. To sell factories.

Because the hard part of making a new chip was never the design. Plenty of people can design one. The hard part is finding somewhere to build it. Only a handful of places on Earth can make advanced chips, and all of them are full. So a small team with a weird idea doesn't get a slot, and the weird idea never gets built.

Fab2 goes at that sideways. Instead of one enormous factory, it builds small ones, and then mass-produces them. A factory that makes factories. And it builds every machine inside, itself. Pumps, valves, gas lines, the tool that draws the circuit, all of it.

One of the founders made working chips in his parents' garage as a teenager. That's the whole argument, really. A factory can be small.

It's slow, though. Today this suits prototypes, not volume.

But there has only ever been one answer to a chip shortage. Build more factories. Today, you queue for somebody else's. Tomorrow, one turns up on a truck.
