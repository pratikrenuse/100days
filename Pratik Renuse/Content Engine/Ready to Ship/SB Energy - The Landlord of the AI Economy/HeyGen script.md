# SB Energy - The Landlord of the AI Economy -- HeyGen script

The largest data centre ever announced is going up on an old uranium site in Ohio. And the owner is a company you have probably never heard of.

It is called SB Energy. And this week the Wall Street Journal reported something strange about how it gets paid for. Reported, mind you. Nothing is signed.

The plan is ten gigawatts of computers, and right beside them, their own power station burning gas. Nine point two gigawatts of it. Why build your own? Because a big electrical transformer in America is now quoted four to five years out. Four years. So waiting for the grid is not an option.

Then it gets stranger. OpenAI wants to rent this place, but it may not have a strong enough credit rating to borrow that kind of money. So Nvidia is in talks to guarantee it. Around two hundred and fifty billion dollars.

The chip company, co-signing the loan.

The whole build could pass five hundred billion.

Today, the AI race goes to whoever trains the smartest model. Tomorrow, it goes to whoever the bank says yes to.
