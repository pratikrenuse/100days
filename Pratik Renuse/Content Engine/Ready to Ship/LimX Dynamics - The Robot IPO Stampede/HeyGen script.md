# LimX Dynamics - The Robot IPO Stampede -- HeyGen script

A robot startup in China just raised two hundred million dollars. For the second time this year. And it hasn't even gone public yet.

The company is called LimX Dynamics, now worth about two point two billion. Why the hurry? Because China's whole robot industry has reached the same conclusion. In their own words, listing is a must.

The starting gun is Unitree, the world's biggest humanoid maker. Its stock market debut is expected any day now, and it will hand the sector its first real price, maybe fourteen billion dollars. Everyone behind it in the queue wants in before that number gets tested.

Sound familiar? Electric cars ran this exact play. One hot listing, then dozens of makers raising billions. Most of them died. But the money built the battery and motor factories that made cheap electric cars real.

Now robots are loading the same mechanism, with shipments up about seven hundred percent this year.

So here's what I think happens. The brands fade. The parts bin they paid for runs the century.
