About

Over the last decade working across the EU, I learned where I do my best work: the "0 to 1" environment — building a startup from ground zero into something real.

I built Infento's web shop from €0 to €2M in Amsterdam, grew the Swiss brand VU to €275K+ and onto the Olympic stage, and scaled the fintech Bueno from €0 to €300K+ ARR across 28 countries at a 24× LTV/CAC.

To share what I've learned, I mentor founders across the USA, EU, Asia, and Africa, and coach graduates from Harvard, LSE, HEC Paris, and beyond. Transforming someone's perspective so they believe in themselves is the most fulfilling part of the job.

If we could create value together, I'd love to meet — I'm always happy to connect with like-minded people.
