A note

Over the last decade working across Europe, I realised I thrive in the “0 to 1” environment — building a startup from nothing into something real.

That pattern repeats. Infento in Amsterdam, from €0 to €2M+ in D2C sales. VU in Switzerland, from €0 to €275K+ and onto the Olympics. Bueno in Spain, from €0 to €300K+ ARR across 28 countries at 24× LTV/CAC. And before them, Bayer's €250K global executive event and Airbus's EU-wide branding.

To share what I've learned, I advise and mentor founders across the USA, EU, Asia, and Africa — through Techstars, EBRD, Seedstars, Founder Institute, and StartupIndia–NITI Aayog. I also coach graduates from Harvard, LSE, IMD, HEC Paris, Oxford, and the IIMs. Transforming someone's perspective so they believe in themselves is immensely fulfilling to me.

And occasionally, I get on a stage — TEDx, the Asia Pacific Digital Marketing Conclave, the Russian Stream Conference. If we could create value together, I'm always happy to meet likeminded folks.

— Pratik
