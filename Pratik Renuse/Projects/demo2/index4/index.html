A note

Over the last ten years across Europe, I learned where I do my best work: at the very beginning, when there is nothing yet — no brand, no customers, no playbook. That blank space is where I feel most at home.

Three times I started from zero and watched something real take shape. Infento in Amsterdam. VU in Switzerland. Bueno in Spain. The numbers came later. The beginning is the part I love.

— Pratik
