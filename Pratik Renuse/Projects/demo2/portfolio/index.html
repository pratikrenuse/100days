About

I thrive in the “0 to 1” — turning something from nothing.

A few experiences taught me where I do my best work: building a startup from ground zero into something real. Infento in Amsterdam, from €0 to €2M+ in D2C sales. VU in Switzerland, from €0 to €275K+ and onto the Olympics. Bueno in Spain, from €0 to €300K+ ARR across 28 countries.

To share what I’ve learned, I advise and mentor founders across the USA, EU, Asia, and Africa through Techstars, EBRD, Seedstars, and Founder Institute. I also coach graduates from Harvard, LSE, HEC Paris, Oxford, and IMD — transforming someone’s perspective so they believe in themselves is immensely fulfilling.

If we could create value together, reach out. I’m always happy to meet new, likeminded folks.
