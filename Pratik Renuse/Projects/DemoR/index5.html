A Note

Over the last decade of working across Europe, I realised something simple about myself: I come alive in the "0 to 1" — the messy, uncertain work of building a company from the ground up, turning something out of nothing.

I built a children's STEM brand from €0 to €2M in Amsterdam, helped take a Swiss sportswear label to the Olympics, and grew a fintech from zero to €300K ARR across 28 countries. Different products, same thrill: the early, formless beginning.

Somewhere along the way, I found a second love — sharing what I'd learned. Mentoring founders across five continents, and watching a graduate begin to believe in themselves, turned out to be just as fulfilling as any launch.

So if you build, teach, or simply make things from nothing — I'd love to meet you.

— Pratik
