# 023_187_video_4 · notes
Who: Pratik giving a 15 minute talk to developers, designers and marketers at the Deployed AI event hosted by Coditas, followed by audience Q&A.
Summary: Pratik shows one external and three internal AI tools he built at Bueno with Claude: interactive Spanish property tax calculators that replaced static PDF guides and doubled lead conversion from about 2.5% to 5%, a news agent that drafts the weekly newsletter's property news section, a Meta ads dashboard with an executive summary for the CEO, and UX insights through the Mouseflow MCP. He closes on build plus sell as the two founder skills, the Mario power up meme, and matching a tool's wow factor to what the customer values.
Topics: AI tools for marketing, lead generation calculators, interactive lead magnets, newsletter automation, Meta ads dashboard with Claude MCP, Mouseflow MCP, build and sell, product simplicity, Bueno
