# 037_216_Session_7_24_April · notes
Who: Pratik teaching the final session of his growth metrics course to a TETR / Master's Union student founder cohort
Summary: The last class of the course. Pratik teaches product channel fit (pick one channel per niche and go deep), tells the full Inventor story of reaching a million in revenue on Meta ads in Europe and then validating a US Kickstarter launch with 25,000 to 30,000 pre-launch leads, walks through a channel prioritisation table and a monthly investor update template, runs a quiz, and has student teams present their funnels and north star metrics.
Topics: product channel fit, channel prioritisation by niche, influencers and trust, marketplaces (Amazon, Blinkit, Zepto), Kickstarter pre-launch lead generation, investor monthly updates, board meetings, north star metric, funnel building at month zero, awareness campaigns
Publishable: partly (Pratik's teaching and the Inventor Kickstarter story are strong clip material; student presentations, names and the after-class chat should stay private)
