Summary: A cohort workshop on fine tuning an MVP design. Breakout rooms follow each section.
Topics: MVP design, MVP channels, call to action, landing pages, pre-selling, B2B say-yes actions, cold outreach, MVP design tree, Wizard of Oz MVP, pivoting, accelerator introductions
