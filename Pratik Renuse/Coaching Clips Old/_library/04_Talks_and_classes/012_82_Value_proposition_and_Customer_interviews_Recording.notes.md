# 012_82_Value_proposition_and_Customer_interviews_Recording · notes
Who: Daria (Seedstars facilitator) teaching a cohort of climate and impact founders in the Seedstars x CTCN programme; participants include Emma, Shani, Mohammed, Joe, Kaif and others. Pratik is not clearly heard speaking in this recording.
Summary: A 1 hour 43 minute workshop on the value proposition canvas, writing a one sentence value proposition, and running problem validation customer interviews. Uses a vertical farming company and an air purifying facade as worked examples, with short breakout exercises and Q&A.
Topics: value proposition canvas, jobs to be done, pains and gains, value proposition formula, stakeholder mix, problem hypothesis, customer interviews, five whys, outreach on LinkedIn, sample size
Publishable: partly (useful teaching content, but it is delivered by Daria, not Pratik, so clips are not his to use as his own; good source for site ideas only)

## Pratik's best moments (clip and site candidates)
None from Pratik. Strongest session moments (Daria) for reference only:
- [42:49 to 43:36] The value proposition formula: our product helps [customer segment] who want [job to be done] by reducing [pain] and increasing [gain].
- [55:39 to 56:25] How to choose which gain or pain to put in the value proposition: how well you can deliver it and how much it matters to the customer.
- [01:08:51 to 01:09:39] Cold calling shipping companies: out of 20 calls, 5 agreed to talk.
- [01:14:21 to 01:15:54] Finding interviewees: LinkedIn connection note trick, ask about 50 people to get about 20 interviews.
- [01:23:19 to 01:25:47] Why "would you buy" and "would you pay" are bad interview questions, the airline leg room example, and asking about past behaviour instead.
- [01:26:40 to 01:28:15] Five whys and asking for examples and feelings to dig into the real problem.
- [01:33:56 to 01:35:28] Users suggest acoustic panels for echoing meeting rooms, the better fix was small call booths: listen to user ideas but solve the underlying problem.
