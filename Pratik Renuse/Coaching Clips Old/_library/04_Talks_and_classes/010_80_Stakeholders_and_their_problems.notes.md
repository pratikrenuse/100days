Who: First session of the Seedstars x CTCN Youth Climate Innovation Academy on Zoom. Led by Daria (Seedstars trainer) with Ada (program coordinator), plus Karina and Valentin from CTCN on climate finance and technical assistance. Founders from the youth climate teams take part. Homework is a stakeholder map and personas in a shared team sheet.
Topics: climate finance, CTCN technical assistance, lean startup feedback loop, stakeholder mapping, customer vs user vs beneficiary, B2B vs B2C, decision making unit, jobs to be done, pains and gains
