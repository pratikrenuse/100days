# 032_211_Session_2_6_March · notes
Who: Pratik teaching an in person growth metrics class to a TETR College of Business cohort, with student startup teams (clothing, silverware, pickleball, card game, supplement, stationery and others) working through a live workshop.
Summary: Day 2 of Pratik's growth metrics course. He explains the North Star metric with Amazon repeat purchases, the Sean Ellis and Superhuman "how disappointed would you be" story and Headspace mindful minutes, gives a three step way to find one, then coaches teams table by table on their North Star and key drivers. He closes with rate limiting steps, one metric that matters, marketing attribution models, and short term versus long term bets using Airbnb stories.
Topics: North Star metric, key drivers, product market fit, Sean Ellis test, painkiller versus vitamin, repeat purchase rate, referral metrics, brand building for luxury products, finding problems in competitor reviews, rate limiting step, OMTM, marketing attribution, short term versus long term bets, Airbnb
