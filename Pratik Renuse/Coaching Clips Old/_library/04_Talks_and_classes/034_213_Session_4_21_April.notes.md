# 034_213_Session_4_21_April · notes
Who: Pratik teaching a TETR / Master's Union student cohort (founders running their own e-commerce stores)
Summary: Session 4 of Pratik's growth metrics course. He recaps vanity vs actionable metrics, the north star metric, aha moments and growth experiment prioritisation, shares D2C funnel benchmarks, then runs a Tony's Chocolonely style case where students diagnose a broken funnel (CAC 15 vs AOV 8), find the bottom of funnel bottlenecks and design experiments for a board update.
Topics: vanity vs actionable metrics, north star metric, aha moment, growth experiments, effort vs impact prioritisation, D2C funnel benchmarks, internal traffic filtering, CAC vs AOV, checkout optimisation, payment methods, repeat purchase rate, MECE problem trees, segment-level funnels, board presentation
