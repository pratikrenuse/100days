# 065_34_Introductory_Coaching_Call_with_Krishna · notes
Who: Pratik coaching Krishna, an aspiring screenwriter and filmmaker in his mid twenties (intro call via the Evisors platform)
Summary: An intro mindset coaching call. Pratik has Krishna rate the main areas of his life, uses the "life is a house" analogy to argue that mindset is the foundation, runs an ideal day visualisation, and walks through his seven step, three month programme (self assessment, values, life blueprint, right vs wrong, changes, environment, network). The call ends by enrolling Krishna, with payment deferred until he sees results.
Topics: mindset, push vs pull motivation, life wheel ratings, life is a house analogy, ideal day visualisation, values, life blueprint, habit building, coaching programme structure, pay it forward pricing

## Pratik's best moments (clip and site candidates)
- [15:40 to 16:05] Two kinds of energy: one pulls you towards work you know is your calling, the other is you pushing and fighting yourself. Pushing alone does not last.
- [32:31 to 37:12] Life is a house: mindset is the foundation, health the walls, wealth the roof, and relationships the doors and windows. Build in that order and people can come and go while the house stays standing.
- [39:08 to 45:00] The nine out of ten exercise: close your eyes, picture your life with a nine out of ten mindset, then write your ideal day from waking up onwards. Writing it down gives you a north star.
- [45:00 to 45:50] Steering the ship: if you change direction every hour you never arrive, and our minds are wired for the negative, so you need a written direction to navigate by.
- [54:23 to 55:48] Lasting change comes from rewiring, not motivational videos: change the stimuli around you (Pratik's office posters) and you change how you think about the future.
- [1:11:51 to 1:14:36] Step one, an honest and shameless self assessment of what is holding you back; step two, find your values, because you only fight for what you value ("if this was your last day, how would you have lived?").
- [1:16:15 to 1:17:06] We write 20 year plans for companies and countries but never a blueprint for our own lives.
- [1:28:49 to 1:29:30, 1:34:07 to 1:34:40] Pay it forward pricing: the client pays after seeing results, for the next student, and once they change they help others too.
