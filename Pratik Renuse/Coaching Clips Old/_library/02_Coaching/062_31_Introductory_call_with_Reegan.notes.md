## Pratik's best moments (clip and site candidates)
- [12:56 to 13:44] A job search is a sales funnel: lift 500 applications from 60 first round calls to 150, and every later stage and your odds of an offer rise with it.
- [14:55 to 16:22] What an ATS does: of 500 resumes, software passes maybe 50 to 100 to HR, who shortlist 10 to 20. Your first goal is to get past the ATS.
- [18:42 to 20:12] Keep the CV to one page and one single column. Two column layouts look nice but come out scrambled in an ATS. Order: summary, experience, education, skills.
- [22:42 to 24:14] Make the recruiter's decision instant: show location, years of experience and every must have requirement so they can see the match in seconds.
- [28:44 to 31:50] Do not leave a gap. Add freelance or Upwork projects as recent experience; European recruiters value side projects, and it answers "what did you do since October?"
- [34:13 to 36:09] The situation, action, result format, with Pratik's own example: no digital strategy, he built the process, and within a year it added over a million in sales.
- [36:58 to 37:44] Generic bullets like "front end development of websites" appear on 20 other CVs; describe the problem you solved and the result so you stand out.
- [39:07 to 42:37] Prove your skills with certifications, from the best (accredited bodies) down to Udemy or LinkedIn; spend an hour a day for a week rather than leaving skills unproven.
