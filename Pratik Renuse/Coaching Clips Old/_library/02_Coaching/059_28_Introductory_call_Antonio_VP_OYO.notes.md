## Pratik's best moments (clip and site candidates)
- [09:33 to 10:19] An early hiring signal: ask "what do you do on the weekends?" An early stage startup needs people who want to build, not only people who switch off.
- [10:19 to 13:56] The value fit mistake: a highly skilled senior marketing hire who did not share the company's family first values caused about six months of slower growth. Skills are not enough; check the value match.
- [15:32 to 16:21] Offer equity, or at least a basic ESOP, to hires beyond the founding team, so new people are fully in rather than "half foot in, half foot out".
- [17:55 to 21:17] Add a 16 Personalities and a Gallup StrengthsFinder test to hiring: a brilliant operator with terrible leadership cost the team, and a simple strengths test would have shown it.
- [29:55 to 31:31] Put live revenue and customer reviews on big screens in the production area, so the person packing boxes sees the smiling kid at the other end.
- [32:16 to 33:02] At peak season everyone, co-founders included, packed boxes together with music and pizza: small rituals that build "we are in this together".
- [35:54 to 38:16] Founders should write down their individual and company values (for example radical transparency, inspired by Bridgewater), then use value questions such as "if you had all the money in the world, what would you do?" at the end of interviews.
- [46:31 to 51:25] Eating together builds a tribe: one big lunch table and an open kitchen for office and production, plus a monthly growth talk followed by Friday drinks, where anyone can teach the rest of the team.
