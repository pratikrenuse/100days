## Pratik's best moments (clip and site candidates)
- [07:35 to 08:46] You have already done the homework (STAR, stories, resources). In a late round the job is to read the hiring manager: are they data driven or instinctive, and steer your answers that way.
- [10:12 to 11:20] If the interviewer values honesty, do not try to be "too nice", because overly nice is not honest. Stay direct.
- [11:20 to 12:39] Senior people want clarity: lead with the point, skip the long stories, and structure answers with McKinsey's Pyramid Principle.
- [12:39 to 14:20] Use a personality tool (Crystal Knows on LinkedIn) to see how the interviewer likes to communicate, short or long, direct or detailed, then tailor the conversation.
- [14:28 to 15:40] The two circles: you and the interviewer each have traits. Do not mould yourself to all of theirs; find the overlap and build the conversation there, which makes the whole round easier.
- [17:47 to 18:31] In a results oriented, high workload marketing team, expect prioritisation questions and prepare examples for them.
