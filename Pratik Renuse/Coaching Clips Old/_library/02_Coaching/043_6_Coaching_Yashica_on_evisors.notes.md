## Pratik's best moments (clip and site candidates)
- [09:16 to 10:06] The energise or de-energise exercise: for each past role, list what gave you energy while doing it, ignoring long term benefits
- [11:37 to 12:37] Testing many careers only helps if you pull out the pattern of what energises you, then compound it
- [12:37 to 13:51] Interviews are a two way choice: go in with your own criteria and a stronger mindset
- [13:51 to 14:50] His own pattern: he always enjoyed new projects at Airbus and Bayer, which is why he moved into startups and VC
- [21:29 to 23:23] From the hiring manager's side: every hire fills a value gap, so show it with a small demo project or presentation, not just a CV and a message
- [24:19 to 25:45] Focus 90 to 95 percent on perceived value, not real value: say you did not have their data, and "imagine what she can do when we give her all the inputs"
- [25:45 to 27:27] He sent a full marketing plan during the interview stage for a VC role; do this only for your top tier roles
- [28:19 to 29:07] Out of 100 applications, 10 get reviewed, 4 or 5 get interviews and 1 is hired, so you are competing with the other applicants
