## Pratik's best moments (clip and site candidates)
- [07:43 to 09:10] Backtrace your past: creativity, people, owning the result keep showing up. When the same elements appear again and again, build your next role around them so work stops feeling like a burden.
- [09:14 to 10:44] Pratik's own story: from Airbus strategy work to Toastmasters, local events, a TEDx talk and paid speaking within about four years, all done alongside his main work.
- [10:44 to 12:20] Side career streams compound: a niche you do once every two weeks can go from x to 10x to 100x over the years, and no one can replace the way only you do it. We are no longer in an era of one career path.
- [13:09 to 15:08] Clarity creates confidence: when you know exactly what environment you do your best work in, you choose companies instead of being chosen, and interviewers feel that certainty.
- [15:29 to 16:30, 19:40 to 21:41] The reverse exercise: list what you hated at each stage, and turn it into non negotiables. Pratik knew he could not do a single focus job again, so he built advising, coaching and speaking around his main work.
- [26:22 to 28:42] How to start a side practice: ask "if I already knew how, what would I do?", build a simple website and portfolio, give free sessions for reviews, then let people name a price, and grow through community groups with admins' permission.
- [30:35 to 32:31] Do not quit the day job to go all in on one passion. A real holiday is having several streams you enjoy, each one filling your bucket.
- [33:28 to 35:08] It does not take 20 years: advising to UN level mentoring in about two and a half years, speaking to TEDx in about three and a half. Study what the best in a field do; you may not reach the top 1%, but the top 10% is reachable.
