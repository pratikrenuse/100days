## Pratik's best moments (clip and site candidates)
- [07:21 to 08:33] How to find ATS keywords: read the job description top to bottom, highlight one or two important words per sentence, remove duplicates, and narrow 20 words down to the three to five that matter most.
- [09:51 to 11:45] CV rules for Europe: one page, two or three bullets per role (two is better), and tailor every section to the job, education included, because 300 to 500 people apply for each role.
- [11:45 to 12:28] Ask "is everyone else doing this?" Everyone lists skills; instead list the certification or proof that shows the skill.
- [17:31 to 21:33] Do not let an algorithm or connection limits become an excuse. Pick the companies and people you want, start reaching out, and solve small blockers during execution rather than overthinking strategy.
- [29:37 to 33:17] Certificates help at lesser known companies, but at top companies they carry maybe 20 to 30 percent of the weight; experience and projects are the bedrock.
- [34:23 to 38:44] Launch a micro product, such as a curated list of design resources, on Product Hunt. A top 10 launch on your CV beats any certificate because it is transferable, proven experience, and almost no one does it.
- [44:10 to 48:08] Leaders with long term vision want people who think long term: study where your industry is heading (for designers, augmented reality) and build that skill early for a first mover advantage.
- [1:05:20 to 1:06:54] Advising founders is not telling them what to do: give a bird's eye view and keep asking questions until they find the answer, whether it is a pitch, a budget choice, or how to stretch three months of runway.
