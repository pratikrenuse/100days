# 040_3_coaching_meghana · notes
Who: Pratik coaching Meghana, a graduate student working in biotech who wants to move into business (consulting, finance or marketing)
Summary: A career coaching call (the recording starts mid sentence and ends before the resume part). Pratik sets three goals for the call, then focuses on choosing the right career path before chasing a job: get clear on ideal work hours, reason through what you enjoyed in past roles and why, and build relationships through genuine value rather than years of tenure. He shares how reflecting on what he enjoyed at university led him to public speaking, Toastmasters and later TEDx and conference talks.
Topics: career planning, choosing the right path, work hours and work life balance, self reflection exercises, professional relationships, transferable skills, public speaking origin story

## Pratik's best moments (clip and site candidates)
- [02:26 to 03:50] Climbing the mountain is not the problem for smart people; the risk is realising a year or two later it was the wrong mountain, so identify the right path early
- [08:11 to 09:49] Homework: block one or two hours in your calendar to think only about how many hours you want to work and the balance you want, because it defines which career fits
- [15:42 to 17:17] Build your own reasoning loop: take everyone's input, then ask why it does or does not make sense for you
- [17:17 to 19:39] Professional relationships come from genuine value and rapport, not years at a company; his still active contacts from Bayer and Airbus
- [20:27 to 21:13] Use the consultant's why loop on your own life: list what you enjoyed and did not enjoy in each experience and why
- [21:13 to 22:45] His own story: in 2016 to 2017 something felt missing, he noticed he always loved presenting, joined Toastmasters, spoke for free, then got paid and invited to TEDx and conferences
