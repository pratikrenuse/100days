How to read this as Google Docs

Because Google Docs has a hard limit of ~1.02M characters per document, the full book (1.43M characters) is split into two volumes. Both live as HTML files in your Google Drive folder My Drive » Mochary Method Consolidated:

  1. Open Google Drive in your browser.
  2. Navigate to My Drive » Mochary Method Consolidated.
  3. Right-click volume_1.html » Open with » Google Docs. Drive converts the HTML into a native Google Doc with proper headings, lists, and links. Save the converted doc.
  4. Repeat for volume_2.html.
