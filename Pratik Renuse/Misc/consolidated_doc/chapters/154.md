##### **Company Basics**

Please share with me some basics about your company:

  - Current revenue and % growth from 12 months ago. Write here
  - Current burn, cash in the bank, and therefore runway (months of burn remaining). Write here
  - Valuation and date of last round. Amount raised. Lead investor. Write here
  - Current key board members and/or primary investors. Write here
  - # of employees. Projected # of employees in 12 months. write here
  - A copy of your pitch deck, if you have one. Link here 
  - A copy of your most recent board packet, if you have one. Link here 
      - (I ask for the pitch deck and the board packet because that is a way for me to ingest a lot of information about your company without you having to spend hours describing it to me.)

#### **Optional**

The following items are optional but highly recommended. We will be doing them at some point. The sooner, the better. So, if you have time and energy, please do them now. If not, we will do them later ...

It is critical to have a North Star for your company (Mission, Vision, Values). If you have already created these, please link them here. If you have not, or want to see my take on how to create these, please create them using [Mission, Vision, Values: How to create them](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1L6ks72TkBIlgPDh6_n-NBBE7e_7nQ2YZ6B4BF12IQEs/edit), and then:

  - Paste (or link) the results here.

It is also critical to define your org chart (who reports to whom) and each person's roles and responsibilities. To that end, please link your org chart here. And create an [AORs](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1v72Mozz6Zr-3Knzcd9yNcs1E8-QKk5NkTVmR6pIQj98/edit?usp=drive_web&ouid=102503928790226855337) list. Please only fill it out for yourself (what you do) for now. Link the resulting sheet here.

Next, please define your company's OKRs for the upcoming quarter (these are not the same as your individual goals from above) and create a Green/Yellow/Red tracker to report on them month-by-month using [OKR Creation](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1GoiH4pwsph5rwSM_wMMiMJkX6-qFVwO0SVYkodNZhwg/edit). If you have already done so, no need to re-create. If this is your first Time creating company OKRs, no need to include the team on this first pass. Just create your version first. Please:

  - Link the results here.

Good board reporting and investor pitches transfer a lot of information about the business quickly. The more that I know about your business, the more helpful I can be. Please, therefore:

  - Most recent board packet. Link here
  - Prior board packet. Link here
  - Most recent investor pitch deck. Link here

It is essential to have a central repository of information where your team can access important company information (what you created above, plus decisions made, etc.)—a wiki. If you have one, please connect me to it, and link here. Please create a Notion account, connect me, and link it here if you do not yet have one.

One of the most valuable exercises that I do myself is an [**Energy Audit**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1HmaRdtk8R5GQtMj72Y6XhHTru5SQAiiqDfDP3qy73k4/edit?usp=drive_web&ouid=102503928790226855337). This audit has led to massive productivity gains for me and those that I have recommended it to. Please do one, and 

  - Paste (or link) the results here.

Later, once we have built trust, I will also ask for access to your internal tools to ensure they are operating correctly. This step includes a Product Roadmap (3- and 12-months), Sales Pipeline, Recruiting Pipeline, and monthly Financials. If you already feel this trust, please link each here:

  - Product Roadmap (3- and 12-months). Link here
