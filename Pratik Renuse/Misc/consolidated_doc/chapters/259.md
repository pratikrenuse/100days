##### The coaching process

One of the fundamental tenets of my coaching is that our brains work best when they are in a state of joy/excitement and worst when they are in a state of fear/anger. For more detail, please read [Fear (and Anger) give bad advice](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1sDWHUm6zwEGiPBQH2Ig6DDvVL-IEQSlfNeQnR2mhjmE/edit). It takes about 3 minutes to read.

##### Personal

For me, coaching is primarily about creating a deep personal connection, i.e., becoming close friends. To that end, I want you to know about me as a human. And I want to know about you as a human. 

I have written up the primary superficial personal information about myself. Please read [Matt](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1p-0dzdOgupxhhYFHSKGoHCY9ugUwVbAUCdFmlcBCOQw/edit). It takes about 5 minutes to read. 

Now, please take no more than 5 minutes to write some personal information about yourself. Here are suggestions:

- Your phone number (so that I can text you if I think I might be late for our meeting). write here
- Your age and birthday. write here
- Where you live and with whom. write here
- The name of your partner, if you have one. write here
- The name, ages, and genders of your kids, if you have them, and their primary interests. write here
- The name and type of your pets, if you have them. write here
- Who are your closest 2-3 friends (other than your partner and family). write here
- What do you like to do for fun now. write here
- A topic that you like to talk about. write here
- What you liked to do for fun when you were a kid (and actually had real time to play!). write here
- Where you grew up. write here
- The names of your parents and siblings. write here
- What is a personal dream that you have? write here
- What system (e.g., GTD) and/or tool (e.g., Omnifocus) do you use for remembering to-dos? write here
