1\. **Buy the equipment you need**:

**Video Essentials**:

· [Camera + lens](https://www.amazon.com/Sony-Full-Frame-Mirrorless-Interchangeable-Lens-ILCE7M3/dp/B07B45D8WV) (includes the lens in this package)

· [Capture Board](https://www.amazon.com/Elgato-Cam-Link-Broadcast-Camcorder/dp/B07K3FN5MR) (it reduces latency by processing the HDMI output of the Camera through dedicated hardware)

· [Power supply](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B07H3FXLC6) (allows you to plug the Camera in a power outlet and avoid relying on its battery)

· [HDMI cable](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B014I8TZXW) (connects the Camera to the capture board)

· [Memory Card](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/sandisk-extreme-pro-128gb-sdxc-uhs-i-memory-card/6293605.p?skuId=6293605) (you may buy two of these since the Camera has two slots)

**Audio Essentials**:

· [Microphone](https://www.amazon.com/Shure-SM7B-Cardioid-Dynamic-Microphone/dp/B0002E4Z8M) (one of the best vocal microphones you can buy)

· [Mic Arm](http://amazon.com/Gator-Frameworks-Desk-Mounted-Microphone-GFWBCBM3000/dp/B082YHPC3Z) (desk mounted with built-in XLR cable that connects to your mic)

· [preamp](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07QR6Z1JB/) (used to power your mic and connects it to your computer)

**Extra items you may also want to buy:**

· [Mic Activator](https://www.amazon.com/Cloud-Microphones-CL-1-Cloudlifter-1-channel/dp/B004MQSV04) (significantly improves mic quality, connects preamp to the microphone and [requires this cable](https://www.amazon.com/AmazonBasics-Male-Female-Microphone-Cable/dp/B01JNLTTKS))

· [Camera cage](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B07SX2SCQJ) (helps you install addons such as light and clamp/tripod)

· [Camera Light](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/digipower-the-streamer-video-light-silver/6403889.p?skuId=6403889)

· [Clamp](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B077QDKRC7) (can hold the camera to a monitor or edge of a table)

· [Small tripod](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/sunpak-grippod-11-tripod-black-blue/6359089.p?skuId=6359089) (alternative to the clamp)

2\. **Set up the Camera to (a) output a clean HDMI signal and (b) bypass the memory card** (that way, you can keep streaming without being capped to the memory card capacity).

3\. **Open the Zoom app** on your computer and **click on the Settings button** (the little gear button).

4\. **Click on "Video" and select your camera feed** in the drop-down menu.

A similar option is to go with [what Gary Tan recommends](https://kit.co/garrytan/badass-telework-setup).

Justin Kan took it to the next level by adding a [teleprompter](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B019AJOLEM/) paired with an iPad and the Duet app. That way, you appear to be looking forward the whole time.
