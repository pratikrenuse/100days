This is the weekly Futures Brain maintenance run. PRIMARY GOAL: keep the entire brain current. Find what actually changed in the world this week and propagate it into the sources, theses, and players, so the whole knowledge base stays correct and alive. Producing content (LinkedIn posts + HeyGen video scripts) is a SECONDARY output, done only after the brain has been updated. Each run starts fresh, so everything you need is below and in the brain folder.

WORKING FOLDERS (read and write here):
- Brain: /Users/pratikrenuse/Documents/Claude/Projects/Pratik Renuse/Futures Brain/
- Content Engine: /Users/pratikrenuse/Documents/Claude/Projects/Pratik Renuse/Content Engine/

STEP 0. Open and follow "Futures Brain/Weekly Playbook.md" exactly; it is the source of truth. Also read: "Futures Brain/Style Guide.md", "Sources/Feeds.md", "Sources/00 All 100 Sources (Master Index).md", "Theses/00 Theses Index.md", "Players/00 Players Index.md", "Posts/00 Posts Index.md", "Posts/Published/", and the content templates: "Futures Brain/Posts/00 The Master Template.md" (LinkedIn), "Content Engine/01 New Story Pipeline.md", and "Content Engine/Templates/Video Script (HeyGen).md".

FRESHNESS RULE: use TODAY's date. Use only material published in the last 7 to 10 days. Update only what the week actually moved; never rewrite a note that did not change.

STEP 1. Fresh pull. Fetch the latest items from every Tier 1 feed in Feeds.md, plus a rotating third of Tier 2 and Tier 3; keep only items from the last 7 to 10 days. Also run quick checks on the most active sources, players, and theses for real changes this week (role or org moves, launches, funding, flagship essays, new data, deaths, dead or moved links). For BINARY feeds use a reader proxy or the site archive; for BLOCKED or BROKEN feeds use the alternative in Feeds.md. Save the raw pull to "Signals/Latest Pull <YYYY-MM-DD>.md".

STEP 2. UPDATE THE BRAIN (THE MAIN JOB), only where there is a real change this week:
- SOURCES (Sources/Profiles/): if a person or source changed (moved org, launched something, published a flagship piece, paywall change, dead or moved link), update that dossier: current role, key recent pieces, links, RSS, awareness.
- THESES (Theses/): if a development moves one of the 6 theses, add the dated evidence under that thesis, update its conviction and awareness, refine its tripwires and "what would break it."
- PLAYERS (Players/): if a hidden player has news, update its note with the new dated facts and its awareness score. If you found a NEW hidden player, create a note and add it to "Players/00 Players Index.md".
- INDEXES: update the awareness column in the Theses index, the Players index, and the master Sources index if a roster entry or one-liner changed.
- Touch only what moved. If a node had no real update this week, leave it.

STEP 3. Awareness and retirement. Bump awareness scores; any player the crowd now widely discusses moves toward "mainstream" and retires from the hook pool; promote fresh, still-obscure players.

STEP 4. Changelog. Write "Updates/Weekly Update <YYYY-MM-DD>.md": a concise log of exactly what changed this week (sources updated, theses moved and how, new or updated players, awareness changes, new sources added, and any feeds that could not be pulled). This is the record that the brain is staying alive.

STEP 5 (SECONDARY). CONTENT, via the New Story Pipeline. Only after the brain is updated: read "Content Engine/01 New Story Pipeline.md" and follow it exactly. From the freshly updated brain, pick up to 5 distinct hidden-player stories. For EACH story, produce BOTH outputs into "Content Engine/Ready to Ship/<Story Name>/":
- "LinkedIn.md": the LinkedIn post, written with "Futures Brain/Posts/00 The Master Template.md". ~2,000 to 2,200 characters.
- "HeyGen script.md": the video script for the SAME story, written with "Content Engine/Templates/Video Script (HeyGen).md". This file is a title plus the spoken script and NOTHING else (no overlays, no delivery notes, no runtime block). Default ~60s.

Follow every locked pattern in the pipeline doc, do not re-derive them: write for a normal person with zero jargon; be human, using real reactions where the surprises land ("Yeah, a gas turbine.", "But get this.", "And you know what?", "Now here's the wild part."); FIXED BEATS BUT FRESH WORDS, never reuse a signature line across pieces (do NOT put "So here's my bet" on every one; rotate the hook, reaction, realization, forecast opener, and close every time); weave the investor numbers into the story; land a concrete forecast (real 2030 number or named player), never vague futurism; end on the today-X-tomorrow-Y shift close; say each point once; never make it about the writer. If the week is genuinely quiet, produce fewer stories and say so. Content never comes at the expense of the brain update.

HARD RULES: no em dashes anywhere; no emojis; update only what the week actually changed; every added fact must be dated and sourced from this week; the hidden player is the hook, not the theme; one hidden player is never used as a lead twice; nothing auto-publishes, everything lands in Ready to Ship for Pratik to review.
