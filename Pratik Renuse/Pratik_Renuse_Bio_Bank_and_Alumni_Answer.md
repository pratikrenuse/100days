## Speaker bio

Pratik Renuse is an international consultant, investor and entrepreneur who helped build startups in Amsterdam go from 0 to over €2 million in revenue. One of the startups he is part of creates the world's first constructible rides and has won the prestigious Red Dot Design Award in Germany.

A former Airbus and Bayer marketer, Pratik has travelled to over 20 countries and rode on the world's highest motorable road. He credits his ability to network with the right people and prioritise the right goals as the reason he has been able to create value at this scale.

### Speaking topics

**Entrepreneurship.** How a former homeless college student helped create multi-million euro companies in Europe, and what the audience can learn from those mistakes.

**Personal growth.** There are not 2 but 13 dimensions to a life worth living, including health, intellect, emotional life, environment, family and parenting. Insights on living beyond just career and finances.

**Networking.** Pratik has connected with the VP of Amazon, the former water minister of the Netherlands, Jordan Belfort's team and several major European investors. He shares his methodology for gaining access to high-value individuals.

**Social dynamics.** How to meet a stranger in a foreign country and build a solid friendship in hours, and why travel is the fastest form of education.
