# Atoms over Algorithms

#thesis
**Awareness:** Building (the smartest investors get it, the public doesn't)

This is the overarching lens. Most other theses are children of it.

## Claim
As intelligence itself races toward being free, the durable value of the AI era leaves the software layer (models, apps) and pools in the physical things intelligence needs to act through: power, chips, memory, robots, minerals, molecules.

## Where it leads (2035 to 2045)
The list of the most strategically important companies on earth fills up with industrial and physical-layer names, many of which most people can't pick out of a lineup today. "Owning the smartest model" and "owning the AI economy" turn out to be two different things, won by two different kinds of company.

## Mechanism
The cost of a unit of intelligence fell roughly 1000x in three years. When the visible, glamorous layer commoditises, scarcity and pricing power slide to whatever physics will not let you scale on a software timeline. Money and intelligence both become cheap; the rare thing is physical capacity that takes a decade to build.

## Evidence for (dated)
- Inference cost down ~1000x in ~3 years; equivalent quality near $0.40 per million tokens (early 2026, Epoch AI).
- Capital is abundant: ~$725B hyperscaler AI capex in 2026; physical chokepoints (turbines, HBM) sold out years ahead regardless.
- The physical layer is moving onto financial front pages: a memory maker (SK Hynix) filed for a ~$29B US Nasdaq listing while the turbine maker (GE Vernova) stayed sold out to 2029. (June 24 and 26, 2026) The strategically central names are increasingly industrial, not software.
- Exponential View's "State of the AI Economy" (June 25, 2026) hardens the "intelligence -> near-zero" premise: blended price per million tokens fell ~$17 to ~$2, and the cost to use a given level of AI falls ~10x every 12 months. Crucially, demand is elastic (~1.2-1.8): every 10% price cut drives 12-18% more tokens, so cheaper intelligence grows the market rather than shrinking it, and the durable value pools in the physical layers that serve it (energy 66% of a data center's running cost; memory now ~18% of build cost). Measured revenue also understates value (consumer surplus ~+30%). See [[Signals/EV State of the AI Economy (2026-06-25)]].

- **The price spread got wide enough to change behaviour, not just budgets.** As of the August 10, 2026 semis brief: OpenAI's GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.4 run as low as $30 and $15 per million tokens while DeepSeek V4 is under $1. That gap is now large enough that routing routine work to a cheap model and reserving the frontier for high-value requests is a profit lever rather than a procurement detail. Anthropic's annualised revenue run rate per megawatt rose to **four times** its previous level within nine months, from algorithms, routing and model adaptation rather than new hardware. Meta released Muse Glimmer, a 30-billion-parameter open-weight model under Apache 2.0 that runs on a single consumer GPU. Intelligence per dollar and per watt is compounding on three independent axes at once, which is exactly what this thesis predicts. (404K SEMI-AI brief and TechStartups, August 10, 2026)
- **And the demand it unlocked is no longer human.** Per the same brief, citing Cloudflare Q2 2026 data: machine traffic passed human traffic on the web in the first half of 2026, with the crossover estimated at May, roughly 18 months earlier than expected. A single shopping agent may visit around 5,000 websites where a human visits around 5. The elastic-demand point in the Exponential View data now has a physical expression: cheap intelligence does not shrink the market, it fills the internet with machines. The second-order casualty is the advertising model, because agents do not click ads. (August 10, 2026)
- **Meanwhile the money kept moving to atoms.** BofA estimates Amazon, Microsoft, Google, Meta and Oracle will spend roughly $730B of aggregate capex in 2026, up about 100% year over year, potentially exceeding $1 trillion across 2027 to 2028, with some firms briefly running negative free cash flow. Amazon's Trainium annualised revenue run rate has passed $25B, and Google signed an external compute agreement at $920M per month through mid-2029 for roughly 110,000 GPUs while developing in-house Frozen v2 for 2028 at a target of six to ten times current tokens per watt. Software margins are being converted into concrete, power and silicon at close to a trillion dollars a year. (August 10, 2026)

## What would break it
- A breakthrough that makes the software layer defensible again (a durable model moat).
- Physical capacity scaling far faster than expected, erasing the scarcity premium.

## Outlook (10/20/30 curve, sourced)
Full deep-research curve (the intelligence cost curve + where value pools + bear case): [[Signals/Atoms over Algorithms - Long-Horizon Curve (2026-07-02)]]. Headline: the price of intelligence is collapsing ~10x/yr (a16z: 1,000x in three years; Epoch: median ~50x/yr, accelerating to ~200x post-2024; Stanford HAI: GPT-3.5 tier down 280x). Demand is elastic (Google went 9.7T -> 3.2 quadrillion tokens/mo), so total AI spend still rises. The contested part: Altman's bull case says intelligence converges to "near the cost of electricity" (value pools in the physical layers), while SemiAnalysis shows value pooling UP into the frontier labs today (Anthropic ~$9B -> ~$44B ARR, margins 38% -> 70%+). Reconciliation: the commodity tier races to zero, the frontier tier keeps pricing power; value pools wherever scarcity stays, physical compute, energy, and the frontier, not the middle.
