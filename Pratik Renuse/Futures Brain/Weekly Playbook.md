# Weekly Run Playbook

PRIMARY GOAL: keep the entire brain current. Once a week, find what actually changed in the world and propagate it into the sources, the theses, and the players, so the whole knowledge base stays correct and alive. Producing content drafts is a SECONDARY output, done only after the brain has been updated.

Always use TODAY's date. Only use material from the last 7 to 10 days. Update only what the week actually moved; do not rewrite notes that did not change.

## Read first
- Sources/Feeds.md (feed list and pull tiers)
- Sources/00 All 100 Sources (Master Index).md (the full roster)
- Style Guide.md (voice and format, no em dashes)
- Theses/00 Theses Index.md (the 6 arcs and their awareness scores)
- Players/00 Players Index.md (hidden players and awareness)
- Posts/00 Posts Index.md and Posts/Published/ (so any later drafts do not repeat a hidden player)

## Step 1: Fresh pull (the week's developments)
- Pull the latest items from every Tier 1 feed in Feeds.md, plus a rotating third of Tier 2 and Tier 3. Keep only items from the last 7 to 10 days.
- Also run quick checks on the most active sources, players, and theses for real changes this week: role or org moves, launches, funding rounds, flagship essays, new datasets, deaths, dead or moved links.
- For BINARY feeds use a reader proxy or the site archive; for BLOCKED or BROKEN feeds use the alternative in Feeds.md.
- Save the raw pull to Signals/Latest Pull <YYYY-MM-DD>.md.

## Step 2: Update the brain (THE MAIN JOB), only where there is a real change
Walk the week's developments and propagate each into the right place:
- SOURCES (Sources/Profiles/): if a person or source changed (moved org, launched something, published a flagship piece, changed paywall, dead or moved link), update that dossier: current role, key recent pieces, links, RSS, awareness.
- THESES (Theses/): if a development moves one of the 6 theses, add the dated evidence under that thesis, update its conviction and awareness, and refine its tripwires and "what would break it."
- PLAYERS (Players/): if a hidden player has news, update its note with the new dated facts and its awareness score. If you found a NEW hidden player this week, create a note and add it to the Players index.
- INDEXES: update the awareness column in the Theses index, the Players index, and the master Sources index if a roster entry or one-liner changed.
- Rule: touch only what moved. If a node had no real update this week, leave it untouched.

## Step 3: Awareness and retirement
- Bump awareness scores. Any player the crowd now widely discusses moves toward "mainstream" and retires from the hook pool. Promote fresh, still-obscure players.

## Step 4: Changelog (the record the brain is alive)
- Write Updates/Weekly Update <YYYY-MM-DD>.md: a concise log of exactly what changed this week. Sources updated, theses moved and how, new or updated players, awareness changes, new sources added, and any feeds that could not be pulled.

## Step 5 (SECONDARY): draft the week's content
- Only after the brain is updated. From the freshly updated brain, pick up to 7 distinct hidden-player angles and write full drafts plus a 7-day plan into Posts/Weekly/<YYYY-MM-DD>/ in the locked voice (personal hook on the unknown player, grounded middle with dated specifics, a futuristic take-home, no em dashes, about 250 to 320 words, chunked LinkedIn layout with `..` breaks). If the week is genuinely quiet, produce fewer drafts and say so. Drafts never come at the expense of the brain update.

## Guardrails
- No em dashes anywhere.
- Update only what the week actually changed; never rewrite an unchanged note.
- Every fact added must be dated and sourced from this week.
- The hidden player is the hook, not the theme.
- One hidden player is never used as a lead twice.
