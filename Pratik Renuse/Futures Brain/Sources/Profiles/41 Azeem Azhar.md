## Who they are
Azeem Azhar is a technology analyst, investor, and writer who has produced Exponential View for over a decade, now with 159,000-plus subscribers. He focuses on "exponential technologies" (those whose cost falls and capability rises steeply for decades) and on the gap between how fast they move and how slowly society adapts.

## Their future thesis (one line)
We live in an "exponential age" where AI and related technologies compound faster than institutions can adapt (the "exponential gap"), and AI's compute demands have now broken the 50-year Moore's Law trend, reshaping the economy, energy systems, and the balance of power.

## Hooks for the brain
- The "exponential gap": technology compounds, institutions do not, and the widening space between them is where disruption and policy failure live. A core organizing frame for a futures brain.
