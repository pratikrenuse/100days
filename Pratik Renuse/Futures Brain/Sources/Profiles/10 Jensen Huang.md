## Who they are
Co-founder, President, and CEO of NVIDIA, the company whose GPUs power essentially all frontier AI training and inference. As the supplier of the compute, he is arguably the single most pivotal industrial figure in the AI build-out.

## Their future thesis (one line)
We are entering the age of "physical AI": after perception and generative AI, the next wave is reasoning agents and robots that act in the real world, an opportunity he sizes at roughly $50 trillion, powered by "AI factories" that manufacture intelligence like electricity.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- GTC 2026 keynote, "the age of physical AI" (March 2026), https://www.nvidia.com/gtc/keynote/
  Reframes AI from chatbots to three things at once: AI factories churning out tokens, billions of autonomous agents running the world, and physical robots operating among us. Predicts tens to hundreds of billions of agentic systems globally and positions robotics, autonomous vehicles, and smart infrastructure as nodes in one distributed AI network. Unveils the Vera Rubin compute platform as the engine for this. The clearest single statement of his decade-out vision.

- "Robotics is a $50 trillion industry" / physical AI as a $50T market (GTC 2026), https://gamesbeat.com/nvidia-believes-physical-ai-systems-are-a-50-trillion-market-opportunity/
  Argues that physical AI and robotics applied to manufacturing and labor is a roughly $50 trillion opportunity, with factories that are themselves robots, operated and managed by robots. Names partners across autos (BYD, Hyundai, Nissan, Geely, Uber robotaxis) and industrial robotics (ABB, Universal Robots, KUKA). The strongest articulation of AI moving off screens and into the physical economy.

- Davos 2026 remarks on the future of AI (January 2026), https://www.weforum.org/stories/2026/01/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-on-the-future-of-ai/
  His macro framing for a policy and business audience: AI as a new industrial infrastructure layer, with reasoning AI and agents as the next leg. Addresses jobs by pointing to the scale of new investment and AI-native industries across healthcare, manufacturing, finance, and science. Useful as his answer to the disruption question.

- "Decade of AV, robotics, autonomous machines" (VivaTech, June 2026), https://www.fortune.com/2026/05/06/jensen-huang-servicenow-bill-mcdermott-agentic-ai-robos/
  Declares the coming ten years the decade of autonomous vehicles, robotics, and autonomous machines, with agentic AI rewiring a $50 trillion economy. Frames the factory of the future as fully robotic end to end. Good for the "what the 2030s physically look like" angle. Confirm exact URL.

## Hooks for the brain
- He is the "hidden player" in plain sight: he does not build the models, he builds what every model runs on, so his roadmap effectively sets the ceiling for everyone else.
- "AI factories" (manufacturing intelligence like a utility) is a powerful mental model for the economy of the 2030s, reusable across the knowledge base.
- The $50 trillion robotics/physical-AI number is the boldest economic claim on the roster, use it as the bull case to stress-test.
- Angle: his vision is relentlessly optimistic and compute-centric, the perfect foil to Ilya Sutskever ("scaling is over") and the safety-first voices (Hinton, Bengio).
