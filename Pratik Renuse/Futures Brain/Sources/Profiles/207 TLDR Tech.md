## What it is
The flagship daily newsletter (weekdays) from TLDR, summarizing the most important technology news, big-tech moves, startups, science, and engineering links in short blurbs for a technical audience. Broader in scope than TLDR AI, covering hardware, platforms, IPOs, and developer tooling alongside AI.

## What it is good for (future signal)
Use it to keep AI in its wider tech context: chip supply, energy and data centers, platform shifts (Apple, Google, Meta, SpaceX), and the labor and business-model effects of automation. The feed is plain XML and dependable. Especially good for the "AI reshapes work and infrastructure" cluster, since it regularly surfaces stories on repricing software engineers, energy, and capital flows that pure-AI feeds skip.
