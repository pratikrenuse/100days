## Who they are
Chief Scientist of OpenAI since May 2024, succeeding Ilya Sutskever, where he sets the company's long-term research direction. A Polish computer scientist with a PhD in theoretical computer science from Carnegie Mellon, he led key work on GPT-4, OpenAI Five, and the first reasoning models.

## Their future thesis (one line)
We are close to AI systems that can work coherently and indefinitely like a person, so the next few years are about building an automated AI researcher: a "research lab in a data center" that accelerates science.

  In this exclusive interview, Pachocki names the "AI researcher" as OpenAI's North Star: an autonomous research intern that can handle multi-day tasks by September 2026, scaling to a full multi-agent automated researcher by 2028.
