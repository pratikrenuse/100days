## Who they are
Co-founder and CEO of Hugging Face, the open and collaborative AI platform (valued around $4.5B, hosting over a million repositories and serving 15,000+ companies). The most prominent industry advocate for open-source AI, now expanding into open-source robotics via the Pollen Robotics acquisition and the Reachy Mini robot.

## Their future thesis (one line)
The future is not a handful of API giants but thousands of companies running their own specialized in-house models, tens of millions of AI builders, open-source robotics, and a world where AI agents may outnumber human users by the end of 2026.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "Hugging Face's Clem Delangue on Open Source AI and the LLM Bubble" (a16z MTS Live, 2025/2026), https://a16z.com/podcast/hugging-faces-clem-delangue-on-open-source-ai-and-the-llm-bubble-mts-live/
  Delangue argues we are in an "LLM bubble," not an AI bubble, and that the durable future is many specialized open models rather than a few dominant foundation-model APIs. He expects AI builders to grow from a few million to tens of millions. His sharpest contrarian framing of the market's shape.

- "Six predictions for AI" annual posts (e.g. 2025 edition), https://huggingface.co/posts/clem/703679306559358
  His recurring year-ahead prediction posts covering open models, specialized in-house AI, on-device models, and robotics. Read across years they form a running, scoreable forecast. Best concise primary source for his concrete bets.

- Open-source robotics: Pollen Robotics acquisition and Reachy Mini (2025-2026), https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/18/hugging-face-ceo-says-were-in-an-llm-bubble-not-an-ai-bubble/
  Hugging Face's move into open hardware, acquiring Pollen Robotics and shipping the open desktop robot Reachy Mini (reportedly ~10,000 units), to "make AI robotics open source." It extends the open-model thesis into the physical world. Signals where he thinks the open ecosystem expands next.

- "Agents may outnumber humans" / platform-for-agents framing (2026), https://www.turingpost.com/p/clem-delangue-hugging-face-ai-builders
  Delangue's claim that Hugging Face is positioning for a world where AI agents could become a larger user base than humans by end of 2026, and that tens of millions of builders will train and fine-tune their own models. It reframes the platform's users from people to autonomous agents. A striking 10-year-direction hook.
