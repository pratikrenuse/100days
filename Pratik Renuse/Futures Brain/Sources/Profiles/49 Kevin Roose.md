## Who they are
Award-winning technology columnist (at The New York Times through August 2026) and co-host of Hard Fork. Author of the forthcoming book The AGI Chronicles. With Casey Newton he is starting a new AI-focused media venture built to "take AI progress seriously." Flag: the original venue note (NYT) is changing in 2026.

## Their future thesis (one line)
What is happening in AI right now is bigger than most people understand, AGI should be taken seriously even without a firm date, and society, institutions, and individuals are systematically underestimating how fast and how far it will go.
