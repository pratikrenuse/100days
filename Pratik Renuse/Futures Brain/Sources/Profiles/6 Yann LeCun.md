## Who they are
Co-founder and Executive Chair (and chief scientist figure) of AMI Labs (Advanced Machine Intelligence Labs), a Paris-based world-models startup he launched in December 2025 after leaving Meta, where he was Chief AI Scientist for twelve years. A Turing Award winner and one of the three "godfathers" of deep learning.

CORRECTION TO TENTATIVE ENTRY: He is no longer Meta's chief AI scientist. He left Meta in November 2025 and now leads AMI Labs (raised about $1.03B in March 2026). Update any reference that still lists him at Meta.

## Their future thesis (one line)
Autoregressive LLMs are a dead end for real intelligence: the path to human-level AI runs through "world models" (systems like JEPA that learn how the physical world works in latent space, plan, remember, and pursue objectives), not bigger text predictors.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "A Path Towards Autonomous Machine Intelligence" (2022, still his core blueprint), https://openreview.net/forum?id=BZ5a1r-kVsf
  His foundational position paper proposing an architecture for machines that perceive, predict, plan, and act with persistent memory and configurable objectives, built around a "world model" and the JEPA (Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture) idea. Argues prediction in abstract representation space, not pixel or token reconstruction, is the route to common sense. This is the intellectual root of everything he is now building.

- AMI Labs launch and world-models thesis (January to March 2026), https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/01/22/1131661/yann-lecuns-new-venture-ami-labs/
  Coverage of why he left Meta and what AMI is: a contrarian bet that the industry's LLM obsession is wrong-headed and will not deliver systems that understand the physical world, plan, or remember. AMI focuses on world models that are controllable and safe, with the long-term aim of human-level machine intelligence. Frames the next decade as an architecture battle, not just a scaling race.

- V-JEPA 2 and zero-shot robot control (2025), https://blog.pebblous.ai/blog/yann-lecun-jepa-world-models/en/
  Explains the JEPA line of work: V-JEPA 2 was pre-trained on over a million hours of video and enables zero-shot robot control in new environments with only tens of hours of robot data. Evidence for his claim that learning a predictive model of the world generalizes far better than text-only training. Relevant to robotics and embodied AI futures. Treat as secondary explainer, the primary artifacts are Meta/FAIR papers.

- Ongoing X commentary arguing LLMs alone will not reach human-level AI, https://x.com/ylecun
  His running public argument (since around 2022) that scaling text models hits a ceiling because most of the world is not in text and most of what matters is hard to predict at the pixel or token level. The clearest contrarian voice against the "just scale LLMs" consensus. Best read as a stream rather than a single piece.
