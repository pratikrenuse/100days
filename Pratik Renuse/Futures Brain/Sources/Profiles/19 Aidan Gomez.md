## Who they are
Co-founder and CEO of Cohere, the enterprise-focused frontier-AI company (ARR reportedly grew from ~$35M in early 2025 to ~$240M by year-end, valuation around $6.8-7B, widely expected to IPO in 2026). As a 20-year-old Google Brain intern he was one of eight co-authors of "Attention Is All You Need," the 2017 transformer paper.

## Their future thesis (one line)
The real frontier is enterprise: large organizations will not hand their most valuable data to public model APIs, so the future is secure, private, in-house agentic AI deployed inside the enterprise.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "Introducing North: A secure AI workspace to get more done" (Cohere blog, 2025), https://cohere.com/blog/north-eap
  Launch of North, Cohere's agentic platform combining LLMs, search, and automation into one secure workspace, pitched as deployable in private/regulated environments. The argument is that enterprises in banking, healthcare, and telecom need agents that never expose data to third-party APIs. This is the productized form of Gomez's enterprise-first thesis.

- "Cohere CEO Aidan Gomez Says New Agents Product Expands Enterprise Adoption of AI" (Upstarts, 2025/2026), https://www.upstartsmedia.com/p/cohere-ceo-exclusive-north-agents
  Interview where Gomez frames North as Cohere's premier end-to-end product and explains the 18-month build and enterprise pilots (RBC, Dell, Bell, Ensemble Health). He argues most valuable enterprise data will never go to OpenAI, so private agents are the unlock. Clearest statement of his "why enterprise wins" position.

- "Attention Is All You Need" (2017, foundational), https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762
  The paper introducing the transformer, the architecture underlying essentially all modern large models, which Gomez co-authored as an intern. Relevant as the origin of the current paradigm and his standing in the field. Context for why his read on the next phase carries weight.

- Cohere 2026 direction: Europe expansion and agent platform (CNBC investor memo coverage, February 2026), https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/13/ai-startup-cohere-revenue-ipo.html
  Reporting that Cohere will keep expanding in Europe and building out North toward an anticipated IPO. It signals a bet on sovereign and regulated-industry demand for private AI. Useful for tracking enterprise-AI adoption and the open-versus-closed enterprise split.

## Hooks for the brain
- Enterprise-first, data-stays-private is a distinct thesis from the consumer-AGI race, a useful "where the money actually flows" angle.
- Gomez is a transformer co-author now betting against the API-centric future its descendants enabled, a clean narrative tension.
- Hidden-player angle: regulated industries (banks, hospitals, telecom) as the quiet center of gravity for serious AI deployment.
- Overlaps with Mensch on sovereignty/private deployment but from a commercial rather than geopolitical motive, good for a compare-and-contrast.
