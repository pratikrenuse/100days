## Who they are
Macro investment strategist and founder of Lyn Alden Investment Strategy, widely read for accessible analysis of fiscal dominance, monetary history, energy, and Bitcoin. As of 2026 she is also a general partner at Ego Death Capital (a Bitcoin-focused venture firm) and author of the bestselling book "Broken Money."

## Their future thesis (one line)
The world has left the post-WWII era of "linear progress" and entered a fragmented "Wild West" of fiscal dominance, permanent large deficits, currency debasement, and AI-driven information disorder, so investors should favor scarce, productive, self-custodiable assets (hard money, commodities, quality equities) over long-duration fiat claims.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "June 2026 Newsletter: The Wild West" (Jun 4, 2026), https://www.lynalden.com/june-2026-newsletter/
  Her clearest big-picture framing of the next decade: the stable, US-led, disinflationary order that shaped modern investing is breaking into a fragmented world of self-reliance, industrial policy, trade wars, and broken global rule of law. Includes a striking argument that AI-generated fakes and bots are reversing the 20th-century gain in shared global knowledge ("inverting Plato's Cave"). Prescribes a portfolio of scarce, productive, low-political-dependence assets.

- "Broken Money" (book, 2023, evergreen), https://www.lynalden.com/broken-money/
  Her flagship work tracing money through the lens of technology, past, present, and future. Argues the current fiat system is structurally broken by the speed mismatch between money and information, and that open monetary networks (Bitcoin) are a likely part of the future. Free sample chapters (13 and 19) are downloadable. The foundation for all her macro views.

- "A Century of Fiscal and Monetary Policy" (2020, foundational), https://www.lynalden.com/fiscal-and-monetary-policy/
  The long-arc piece she repeatedly cites, showing how decades of financial imbalances produce rising populism and outlier political outcomes. Sets up her "long-term debt cycle" framework: private debt rotates onto sovereign balance sheets, then resolves through inflation and political resets. Essential context for her "deficits run hot forever" call.

- "Implications of Open Monetary and Information Networks" (evergreen), https://www.lynalden.com/open-networks/
  Connects Bitcoin and open information networks as parallel forces decentralizing control away from incumbents. Frames the long-term shift toward permissionless, open systems for both money and information. Bridges her monetary and her tech-future thinking.

- "Bitcoin's Energy Usage Isn't a Problem. Here's Why." (evergreen), https://www.lynalden.com/bitcoin-energy/
  Her widely cited rebuttal to the Bitcoin-energy critique, arguing mining can monetize stranded and intermittent energy and support grid economics. Relevant to any future-of-energy plus crypto thesis. A standard reference in the debate.
