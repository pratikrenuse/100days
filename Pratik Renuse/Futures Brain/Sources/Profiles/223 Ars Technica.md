## What it is
Technology, science, and tech-policy news site founded in 1998, owned by Conde Nast (sits in the same Wired Digital group as Wired). Known for deep, original reporting and analysis rather than churn, with writers who are often postgraduates or come from research institutions.

## What it is good for (future signal)
Best read as a sanity check on hype: Ars tends to wait, test, and explain, so it is good for the technical "is this real" view on AI models, chips, energy, security, and tech regulation. Use it to pressure-test claims that show up first on social or vendor blogs, and to track the infrastructure layer (silicon, datacenters, networking) that constrains where AI can actually go in 10 to 20 years.
