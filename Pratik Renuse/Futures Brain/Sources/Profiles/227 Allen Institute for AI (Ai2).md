## What it is
A nonprofit AI research institute founded by Paul Allen, focused on "fully open" AI: open models, open data, and open training recipes (the Olmo, Tulu, Molmo families, and the Asta science-agent ecosystem). It is a counterweight to closed frontier labs.

## What it is good for (future signal)
Ai2 is the best window into the open-source frontier: if you want to know how close open models are to closed ones (and on what timeline open AI infrastructure becomes a public good), this is the primary source. Their work also tracks AI applied to hard, durable problems: science, robotics, and the planet (climate, oceans, wildlife). Use it to forecast the open-versus-closed balance of power and the spread of AI into research and the physical world.
