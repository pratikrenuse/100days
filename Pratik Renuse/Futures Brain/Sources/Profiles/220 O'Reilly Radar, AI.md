## What it is
The AI section of O'Reilly Radar, the publisher's analysis blog. It mixes a monthly "Radar Trends to Watch" link digest with longer essays on where software development, agents, and AI tooling are heading, written by O'Reilly editors and practitioners.

## Access
- URL: https://www.oreilly.com/radar/topics/ai/
- RSS feed: https://www.oreilly.com/radar/topics/ai/feed/index.xml
- Paywall: no (Radar blog posts are free; the broader learning platform is subscription)

## What it is good for (future signal)
A practitioner's-eye view of the AI build stack as it actually changes month to month: which protocols, models, agent patterns, and developer workflows are winning. The monthly "Radar Trends to Watch" is one of the densest single round-ups of weak signals across models, infrastructure, security, and policy, with each item linked to a primary source. Good for seeing the near-term shape of how AI gets built and operated.

  A start-of-year piece collecting the trends the Radar team expects to matter most across AI and software during 2026.
