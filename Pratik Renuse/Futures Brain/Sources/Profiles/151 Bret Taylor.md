## Who they are
Bret Taylor is co-founder and CEO of Sierra, the enterprise AI agent company for customer experience he started in 2023 with Clay Bavor, and he is Chairman of the board of OpenAI (both roles confirmed current as of 2026). Earlier he co-created Google Maps, was CTO of Facebook, co-CEO of Salesforce alongside Marc Benioff, and the last chairman of Twitter's board before the Musk acquisition.

## Their future thesis (one line)
AI agents are the next atomic unit of software, as foundational as the website and the mobile app were, and within years a company's agent will become its primary digital experience, replacing menus, forms, and clicks with stated outcomes that agents carry out.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "Agents as a Service" (March 25, 2026), https://sierra.ai/blog/agents-as-a-service
  Taylor and Clay Bavor argue that as clicks turn into prompts, the traditional web app with its menus, forms, and tables starts to look like a "horseless carriage," a design built on assumptions that no longer hold. They introduce Ghostwriter, an agent that builds and continuously improves other agents from plain-English direction and source material, and frame the future of software as "prompts, not clicks," where you define an outcome and agents deliver it.

- "The Guide to AI Agents" (May 23, 2024), https://sierra.ai/blog/ai-agents-guide
  This is Sierra's foundational explainer defining AI agents as autonomous software systems that can reason, make decisions, and pursue goals with flexibility, not just answer questions. It lays out the thesis that agents will soon be everywhere, serving consumers, employees, and businesses, and sets up Sierra's view that every company will need an agent the way it once needed a website.

- "An Interview with Sierra Founder and CEO Bret Taylor About AI Agents and Tech History Lessons" (June 26, 2025), https://stratechery.com/2025/an-interview-with-sierra-founder-and-ceo-bret-taylor-about-ai-agents-and-tech-history-lessons/
  In this long-form interview with Ben Thompson, Taylor connects lessons from past platform shifts (web, mobile) to the emerging agent era and explains why he believes agents will reshape how software is built and used. He discusses Sierra's strategy, outcome-based pricing, and his read on where enterprise AI is heading. Note: full transcript is paywalled behind Stratechery Plus.

- "OpenAI chair Bret Taylor says AI is 'probably' a bubble, expects correction in coming years" (January 22, 2026), https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/22/openai-chair-bret-taylor-ai-bubble-correction.html
  Speaking at Davos, Taylor said AI is "probably" a financial bubble that is drawing both "smart money" and "dumb money" into competitors at every layer of the stack, and he expects a market correction in the coming years. At the same time he described himself as an AI optimist, arguing the free market will sort out which players have the best products and that the long-term importance of the technology is real even if near-term valuations overshoot.

- "OpenAI chairman Bret Taylor lays out the bull case for AI agents" (March 4, 2025), https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/04/openai-chairman-bret-taylor-lays-out-the-bull-case-for-ai-agents/
  Taylor frames AI agents as the defining software category of this era and makes the case that for most companies their agent will eventually handle a large share of customer interactions, much like their website or app does today. He argues conversational agents that understand intent in plain language will displace the need to learn complex software interfaces.
