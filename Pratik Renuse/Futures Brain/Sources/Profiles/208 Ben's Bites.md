## What it is
An AI newsletter written by Ben Tossell (founder, formerly of Makerland and Earlynode), published on Substack and read by builders and operators. It mixes AI news with hands-on tips, tool reviews, and a builder-community angle, leaning practical ("here is what I actually built or used this week") rather than pure reporting.

## What it is good for (future signal)
Use it as a builder's-eye view of where AI is genuinely useful right now. Ben tests tools, agents, and workflows and reports what works, which is a good leading indicator of which AI capabilities are crossing from demo to daily practice. Strong for the "agents and AI-native software replace SaaS" thesis and for spotting the practical tooling (skills, MCP, coding agents) that will compound over the next decade.
