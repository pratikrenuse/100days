## Who they are
Creator of Keras and author of "On the Measure of Intelligence" (2019). Co-founder (with Zapier's Mike Knoop) of Ndea, an AGI research lab launched out of Y Combinator (W2026), and co-founder of the ARC Prize Foundation. Left Google in November 2024 after ~9 years.

## Their future thesis (one line)
The path to AGI is not bigger transformers but program synthesis fused with deep learning, intelligence is skill-acquisition efficiency on novel tasks, and we should measure progress by tests like ARC-AGI that resist memorization.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "On the Measure of Intelligence" (2019, foundational), https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.01547
  Chollet's defining paper. It argues that skill at a fixed task is not intelligence because skill is bought with prior knowledge and data, and instead defines intelligence as how efficiently a system acquires new skills on unfamiliar problems. It introduces the ARC-AGI benchmark as a concrete instrument for this definition. This is the intellectual root of everything he is now building.

- "Ndea: a new AGI lab" / About Ndea (2026), https://ndea.com/about.html
  Announcement and thesis page for his $43M YC-backed lab betting that deep-learning-guided program synthesis, not scaling, is the route to general intelligence. The lab states it has no product, around fifteen people, and three-to-five years of pure-research runway. Clearest statement of his contrarian "scale is not AGI" position as an operating bet.

- "The ARC Prize & How We Get to AGI" (YC Startup Library talk, 2025/2026), https://www.ycombinator.com/library/Md-fran-ois-chollet-the-arc-prize-how-we-get-to-agi
  A talk-format walkthrough of why current models fail at novel reasoning and how the ARC Prize is structured to push the field toward genuine generalization. He connects benchmark design to a research agenda. Good for the "how he thinks we get there" narrative.

- ARC-AGI-2 and the 2026 ARC Prize (2025-2026), https://arcprize.org/
  The benchmark and competition (now a non-profit foundation, with a 2026 prize pool reported around $2,000,000) designed so that brute-force scaling does not win. Tracks whether frontier systems are improving on fluid reasoning versus memorized skill. The live scoreboard is itself a forward indicator.
