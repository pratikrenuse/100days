## Who they are
Host of the Lex Fridman Podcast, one of the largest long-form interview shows, known for multi-hour conversations with AI researchers, founders, scientists, and political figures. Research scientist associated with MIT (LIDS), working on human-AI interaction and machine learning.

## Their future thesis (one line)
Fridman is a platform and interlocutor more than a thesis-holder: broadly optimistic and curiosity-driven about AI, robotics, and human-machine collaboration, he surfaces the strongest first-party arguments from the people actually building the future.
