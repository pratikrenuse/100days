## Who they are
Pioneering geneticist at Harvard and the Wyss Institute who helped launch the Human Genome Project, developed early genome sequencing methods, and has co-founded around 50 biotech companies. He co-founded Colossal Biosciences with Ben Lamm to pursue de-extinction of the woolly mammoth and other species.

## Their future thesis (one line)
Reading and writing genomes at scale will let us reverse aging, eradicate genetic disease, and even revive extinct species, with longevity escape velocity plausibly reachable around 2035 if therapies are fast-tracked.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "The End of Aging and Extinction: George Church on Longevity, Dire Wolves, and Woolly Mammoths" (22 July 2025), https://wyss.harvard.edu/media-post/the-end-of-aging-and-extinction-george-church-ph-d-on-longevity-dire-wolves-and-woolly-mammoths/
  In this Wyss Ignition Sequence interview, Church explores gene therapy, de-extinction, and the future of human health. He argues we cannot rule out reaching longevity escape velocity by 2035, the point where new therapies extend life faster than time passes. He links the same genome-engineering toolkit to reviving species like the dire wolf and the woolly mammoth.

- "George Church: Learn from COVID and fast-track therapies that reverse aging" (Longevity.Technology), https://longevity.technology/news/george-church-learn-from-covid-and-fast-track-therapies-that-reverse-aging/
  Church argues that the rapid COVID vaccine effort shows how aging therapies could be accelerated if regulators and funders treated aging as urgent. He advocates combination gene therapies that target multiple age-related pathways at once. The piece captures his conviction that aging is an engineering problem we can fast-track.

- Colossal Biosciences dire wolf announcement (April 2025), https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colossal_Biosciences
  Colossal, which Church co-founded, announced gene-edited wolf pups carrying dire-wolf traits, reconstructed using ancient DNA from a 13,000-year-old tooth and a 72,000-year-old skull. The company frames de-extinction as a tool for conservation and ecosystem restoration. It is the first high-profile demonstration of Church's de-extinction vision.

- Colossal woolly mammoth program, on track for 2028, https://www.denofgeek.com/culture/colossal-woolly-mammoth-on-track-2028-de-extinction-evolves/
  Colossal aims to produce woolly mammoth hybrid calves by 2028 by editing mammoth-like traits into Asian elephants, with reintroduction to Arctic tundra as a goal. A 2025 milestone produced gene-edited "woolly mice" carrying cold-adaptation traits. This is the flagship long-horizon project of Church's de-extinction agenda.
