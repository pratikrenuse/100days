## Who they are
Senior Research Scientist at NVIDIA and Lead of its AI Agents initiative, co-leader of the GEAR Lab (Generalist Embodied Agent Research) and a co-lead of Project GR00T, NVIDIA's humanoid-robot foundation model effort. Stanford PhD under Fei-Fei Li, and OpenAI's first intern (2016).

## Their future thesis (one line)
We are building the "Foundation Agent," a single generally capable model that learns to act skillfully across many worlds (real robots and virtual simulation), and within a few years every machine that moves will be autonomous.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "Nvidia's Jim Fan on Robots Thinking Fast and Slow" (Sequoia Training Data, 2024/2025), https://sequoiacap.com/podcast/training-data-jim-fan/
  Fan describes a "System 1 / System 2" architecture for robots: a fast reactive policy paired with slower deliberate reasoning, and explains why simulation plus foundation models can scale embodied intelligence. He argues the same agent should generalize across skills, embodiments, and realities. The clearest articulation of his Foundation Agent thesis.

- GEAR Lab mission and program (ongoing), https://research.nvidia.com/labs/gear/
  The lab page sets out the goal of building foundation models for embodied agents spanning humanoid robots and virtual-world NPCs. It frames data, simulation, and scale as the bottlenecks to a "robotics explosion." Useful as the institutional roadmap behind his public claims.

- Project GR00T (humanoid foundation models, 2024 onward), referenced via https://jimfan.me/ and NVIDIA research
  NVIDIA's initiative to build a general foundation model for humanoid robots, which Fan co-leads. It treats humanoids as a general platform that can inherit internet-scale knowledge and learn dexterous skills in simulation. Central to his "every machine that moves will be autonomous" forecast.

- Voyager / Eureka / MineDojo line of work (2022-2024), https://voyager.minedojo.org and https://eureka-research.github.io/
  Earlier agent research where LLMs drive open-ended skill learning in Minecraft (Voyager) and write reward functions to teach dexterous robot hands (Eureka). These demonstrate his core method: use foundation models to bootstrap agents that keep acquiring new skills. They are the proof-of-concept lineage for the Foundation Agent vision.

## Hooks for the brain
- "Every machine that moves will be autonomous" is a vivid, quotable 10-year framing for the embodied-AI thesis.
- Simulation-first scaling (train in sim, deploy in reality) is a distinct mechanism from the data-scaling story dominating LLMs, good for a contrasting post.
- Hidden-player angle: NVIDIA is positioning as the picks-and-shovels supplier for the robot foundation-model era, not just the chip vendor.
- Pairs naturally with Sergey Levine (Physical Intelligence) and Pieter Abbeel for a "who is building the robot brain" cluster, with genuinely different bets.
