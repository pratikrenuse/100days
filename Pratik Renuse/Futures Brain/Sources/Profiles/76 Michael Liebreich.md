## Who they are
Founder of Bloomberg New Energy Finance (BNEF), which Bloomberg acquired in 2009, and still a Senior Contributor there. Co-Managing Partner of EcoPragma Capital and CEO of Liebreich Associates. A leading "pragmatic optimist" on the energy transition: bullish on electrification and clean power, skeptical of hype around hydrogen and some other silver bullets. Former adviser to the UN on Sustainable Energy for All and former Transport for London board member.

## Their future thesis (one line)
The clean energy transition is real, accelerating, and ultimately unstoppable on economics, but it must be navigated pragmatically: electrify everything you can, use clean electrons directly, and be ruthless about which technologies (and which hydrogen dreams) actually pencil out.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "The Great Clean Energy Acceleration 2.0" (27 May 2026), https://about.bnef.com/insights/clean-energy/liebreich-the-great-clean-energy-acceleration-2-0/
  His latest big BNEF essay arguing the transition has re-accelerated despite political headwinds, revisiting the post-2022 energy shock and where momentum stands now. A current state-of-the-transition read from one of its most data-literate observers.

- "The Pragmatic Climate Reset" (Dec 2025), https://about.bnef.com/insights/clean-energy/liebreich-the-pragmatic-climate-reset-part-i/
  A multi-part essay resetting climate strategy around what is actually working: cheap clean electricity and electrification, with a hard look at where targets and narratives have drifted from reality. Captures his "pragma" brand and his critique of both doomers and over-promisers.

- "Make Electricity Cheap Again" (21 Jun 2026), https://mliebreich.substack.com/p/make-electricity-cheap-again
  His Substack 8-point plan arguing electricity must be cheap, clean, abundant, and resilient, and that cheap clean power is the master key to the whole transition. A concise, prescriptive statement of his policy worldview.

- The Clean Energy Ladder / hydrogen "ladder" framework (Cleaning Up and BNEF essays), https://www.cleaningup.live/
  His widely cited "hydrogen ladder" ranks end uses by how sensible hydrogen is versus direct electrification. It has become a standard reference for separating credible decarbonization from hype, and underpins much of his forward analysis.

## Hooks for the brain
- Liebreich is the pragmatic-optimist anchor: more bullish than Smil and Doomberg, more disciplined and skeptical than Seba and Naam. He occupies the credible center of the energy debate.
- His "Cleaning Up" podcast is a long-running, high-signal interview library with transition leaders, a rich secondary source for surfacing other players.
