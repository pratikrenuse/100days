## Who they are
Founder, CEO, and CIO of ARK Invest, the disruptive-innovation asset manager. As of 2026 she is one of the most prominent and polarizing public bulls on AI, robotics, autonomous vehicles, multiomics, and Bitcoin, best known for ARK's annual "Big Ideas" report and aggressive long-range price targets.

## Their future thesis (one line)
We are entering "The Great Acceleration," a period where converging exponential technologies (AI, robotics, energy storage, blockchain, multiomics) compound on each other to add multiple percentage points to global GDP growth and create tens of trillions in new value by 2030, with Bitcoin maturing into a major store-of-value asset.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "Big Ideas 2026: The Great Acceleration" (Jan 2026), https://www.ark-invest.com/big-ideas-2026
  ARK's 10th annual flagship report and the single best summary of her worldview. Argues AI plus other converging platforms (robotics, energy storage, public blockchains, multiomics) are co-accelerating economic growth, potentially adding ~1.9 points to annualized real US GDP growth this decade. Spans AI agents, robotaxis, next-gen data centers, and digital assets. Free, data-dense, and the canonical artifact to mine.

- ARK Bitcoin 2030 outlook (Big Ideas 2026 / commentary, 2026), https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/05/01/institutional-demand-to-drive-bitcoin-market-cap-to-usd16-trillion-by-2030-ark-invest
  ARK projects Bitcoin reaching roughly a $16 trillion market cap by 2030 (about $750,000 per coin base case, up to ~$1.25M bull case), with the broader crypto market near $28 trillion. Notably, ARK raised its "digital gold" estimate after gold's 2025 run but cut Bitcoin's emerging-market safe-haven role due to fast stablecoin adoption. A concrete, falsifiable long-range crypto forecast. (Reported via CoinDesk; primary numbers are from the ARK report.)

- "Cathie Wood's 2026 Outlook: The US Economy Is A Coiled Spring" (early 2026), https://www.ark-invest.com/articles/market-commentary/cathie-woods-2026-outlook
  Her macro framing for 2026: deflationary pressure from AI and innovation, a US economy poised to accelerate as rates ease and productivity gains land. Argues innovation-driven deflation is the dominant force, not the inflation most investors fear. Useful for understanding her contrarian read on the macro setup.

- "In The Know with Cathie Wood" (monthly video series, ongoing), https://www.ark-invest.com/videos/market-commentary/may-2026-in-the-know-cathie-wood
  Her recurring monthly commentary connecting fiscal/monetary policy, market signals, and ARK's innovation theses. The best running source for how her views update month to month. Video format, so not text-fetchable, but ARK often posts summaries.
