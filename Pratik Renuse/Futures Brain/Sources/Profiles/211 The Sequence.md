## What it is
A Substack newsletter by Jesus Rodriguez (CEO of IntoTheBlock) covering machine learning engineering and research, with explainers on core ML concepts and breakdowns of frontier papers and model architectures.

## Access
- URL: https://thesequence.substack.com/

## What it is good for (future signal)
Use it to track where model architecture is heading beyond transformers (state space models, text diffusion, recursion as a scaling law) and to read weekly "Last Week in AI" recaps that flag capital, lab, and IPO shifts. Good early read on the techniques that become mainstream 2 to 5 years out.
