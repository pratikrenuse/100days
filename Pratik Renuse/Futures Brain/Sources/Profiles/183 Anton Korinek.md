## Who they are
Professor in the Department of Economics and Darden School of Business at the University of Virginia and Faculty Director of the Economics of Transformative AI (EconTAI) Initiative, plus nonresident senior fellow at Brookings and the Peterson Institute and a member of Anthropic's Economic Advisory Council. Named to the 2025 TIME100 AI list, he is a leading economist modeling the macroeconomics of a possible transition to artificial general intelligence.

## Their future thesis (one line)
Economists and policymakers should plan now for scenarios in which AI approaches or reaches human-level capability, because transformative AI could drive explosive growth while making human labor economically redundant, demanding new approaches to taxation, distribution, and the social contract.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "Scenarios for the Transition to AGI" (March 2024), https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/viob7f5yv13zy0ziezlcg/AGI_Scenarios.pdf?rlkey=8hxq9rm82kksocw1zjilcxf8v&dl=0
  With Donghyun Suh, models how output and wages evolve as AI automates an expanding share of human tasks. The key result: wages can rise for a while but may collapse once AI can do essentially everything humans can, a scenario economists should take seriously rather than rule out.

- "Scenario Planning for an A(G)I Future" (December 2023, IMF Finance & Development), https://www.imf.org/en/Publications/fandd/issues/2023/12/Scenario-Planning-for-an-AGI-future-Anton-korinek
  Makes the case that policymakers must prepare for the possibility of human-level AI even amid deep uncertainty, using scenario planning rather than single point forecasts. It is an accessible statement of why "this might really happen" deserves a place in mainstream economic policy.

- "Economic Growth under Transformative AI" (September 2025), https://www.korinek.com/research
  With Phil Trammell, surveys the wide range of possibilities for growth, wages, and the labor share under transformative AI, from modest acceleration to explosive takeoff. It is a guide to the parameter choices that determine which future we get, mapping the debate's intellectual terrain.

- "When Does Automating AI Research Produce Explosive Growth?" (January 2026), https://www.econtai.org/research/AutomatingAIResearch.pdf
  With co-authors, examines recursive self-improvement, where AI accelerates AI research itself, and finds the threshold for a growth take-off may be within reach. This connects the abstract idea of an intelligence explosion to concrete economic growth dynamics.

- "Public Finance in the Age of AI: A Primer" (January 2026, Brookings / NBER volume), https://www.brookings.edu/articles/public-finance-age-ai-primer/
  With Lee Lockwood, asks what governments should tax once AGI erodes labor income and consumption, the traditional tax bases. It is an early, practical treatment of how to fund the state and redistribute in a post-labor economy.
