## Who they are
Entrepreneur and investor, former CTO of Coinbase and former general partner at a16z. Author of "The Network State" and founder of the Network School, a residential community for builders. One of the most-followed tech-futurist voices on X.

## Their future thesis (one line)
The nation-state is being disrupted by the internet: cloud-first communities that share values, crowdfund territory, and gain diplomatic recognition (network states) will compete with legacy governments, while fiat systems risk debt crises and capital controls.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "The Network State: How to Start a New Country" (2022), https://thenetworkstate.com/
  His core book argues you can build a new country the way you build a startup: start with an online community aligned on one moral premise, grow membership and a crypto-backed economy, crowdfund physical territory, and eventually win recognition from existing states. It reframes citizenship, governance, and even law as opt-in software-like systems. The full text is free to read online.
- The Network State Podcast (ongoing, episodes through 2026), https://balajis.com/podcast
  Long interviews with founders and policymakers about running money and people at scale and what it takes to start a new kind of state. A January 28, 2026 episode with a16z cofounder Ben Horowitz explores how internet-native institutions are starting to mirror and challenge traditional states. Functions as the running R&D log for the network-state idea.
- Sovereign-debt and capital-controls warnings (2026), https://x.com/balajis
  Across X and his newsletter in early 2026 he argued the US and UK are heading toward sovereign-debt stress and that a "Berlin Wall" of capital controls could trap wealth inside borders. He uses this to push exit, hard assets, and crypto as hedges against legacy finance. Primarily delivered as threads and posts, so best read directly on X.
