## Who they are
Cardiologist and executive vice president of Scripps Research, founder and director of the Scripps Research Translational Institute, and one of medicine's leading voices on AI and digital health. He authored Deep Medicine (2019) and Super Agers (2025), and in March 2026 joined Flagship Pioneering as academic advisor on preemptive health.

## Their future thesis (one line)
Medicine is shifting from reactive sick care to preemptive, data-rich, AI-augmented health, where multimodal AI, omics, and continuous sensors let us predict and prevent disease and extend healthspan years before symptoms appear.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "Super Agers: An Evidence-Based Approach to Longevity" (2025), https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/Super-Agers/Eric-Topol/9781668067666
  Topol's book argues healthspan extension will come from five converging forces: lifestyle, cellular science, omics, AI, and new drugs and vaccines. He documents how AI is transforming cancer and cardiovascular screening and treats GLP-1 drugs as a turning point whose benefits reach far beyond weight and diabetes. The evidence-first framing positions him as the credible, data-driven counterpart to hype-heavy longevity figures. The core claim: prevention guided by AI and omics is the realistic path to more healthy years.
- "Agentic AI Comes to Medicine" (Ground Truths, 2026), https://erictopol.substack.com/p/agentic-ai-comes-to-medicine
  Topol analyzes a shift from AI that assists with narrow tasks to autonomous agentic systems (citing Nature work on MIRA and AMIE) that can carry an end-to-end care plan. He argues this is the most consequential near-term change in clinical medicine and weighs both the promise and the risks of handing more of the workflow to AI. The piece is a clear marker of where he thinks frontline care is heading this decade.
- "Sid Mukherjee: On AI, Longevity and Being a Digital Human" (Ground Truths podcast, 2026), https://erictopol.substack.com/p/sid-mukherjee-on-ai-longevity-and
  A conversation with oncologist-author Siddhartha Mukherjee on how AI will reshape drug discovery, longevity, and the idea of a continuously monitored "digital" patient. The two map where biomedicine is heading as AI compresses discovery timelines and data becomes the substrate of care. Useful as a two-expert synthesis of the AI-plus-medicine future, and it links directly to person #90 in this list.
