## Who they are
Co-founder and CEO of Commonwealth Fusion Systems, an MIT spinout founded in 2018 that is the best-funded private fusion company, having raised over $3 billion from investors including Nvidia, Google, Mitsubishi, and Bill Gates. He is building SPARC, a demonstration tokamak, as the proof that high-field magnet fusion can produce net energy.

## Their future thesis (one line)
Fusion is no longer a science project but an engineering and manufacturing race; high-temperature superconducting magnets make compact, net-energy tokamaks buildable now, and the first commercial fusion plants can be on the grid in the early 2030s.

  Coverage of Mumgaard's CES 2026 keynote, where he declares "fusion is no longer a science project; it's actually the next big thing in tech." - CFS publishes five peer-reviewed papers validating ARC physics (Jun 4, 2026), https://cfs.energy/news-and-media/commonwealth-fusion-systems-builds-on-learnings-from-sparc-to-publish-five-peer-reviewed-papers-validating-the-physics-of-the-arc-fusion-power-plant/
  CFS published five physics-basis papers in the Journal of Plasma Physics (Cambridge University Press) confirming the physics for ARC to deliver 400 MW of net electricity continuously. Mumgaard says the papers "validate and de-risk" the path to grid electricity in the early 2030s. This moves the commercial-plant claim from marketing into peer-reviewed science.

- CFS installs first SPARC magnet and lands Nvidia deal (TechCrunch, Jan 6, 2026), https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/06/commonwealth-fusion-systems-installs-reactor-magnet-lands-deal-with-nvidia/
  Reports installation of the first of 18 high-temperature superconducting D-shaped magnets, the core technology that distinguishes CFS, plus a partnership with Nvidia and Siemens to build an AI digital twin of SPARC. Mumgaard notes the magnets are theoretically strong enough to lift an aircraft carrier. The digital twin is positioned as a way to accelerate ARC's design.

- "Fusion power's next step: tackling the biggest challenges" / SPARC commercialization framing via CFS, https://cfs.energy/
  The CFS site lays out the SPARC-to-ARC pathway: prove net energy with SPARC, then build ARC as the first plant feeding steady power to the grid. Mumgaard's consistent message is that the main remaining risk is "making it work," which SPARC is meant to retire. Useful as the canonical statement of the company's roadmap.
