## What it is
Community-ranked link aggregator run by Y Combinator, where founders, engineers, and researchers post and vote on technology stories. The front page is a real-time read on what the technical community thinks matters right now.

## Access
- URL: https://news.ycombinator.com
- RSS feed: https://hnrss.org/frontpage (working; returns the current front page as XML. The official API is at https://github.com/HackerNews/API)
- Paywall: no

## What it is good for (future signal)
The front page is an early-warning system for shifts in developer behavior: which tools, models, and platforms people actually adopt, and where they are revolting against incumbents. Comment threads often contain sharper analysis than the linked article. Use it to spot weak signals (a niche project, an unusual benchmark, a policy fight) months before they reach mainstream coverage, and to gauge sentiment on AI, privacy, and platform power.
