## Who they are
Founder of Thrive Capital and the lead venture investor in OpenAI, having backed it from a roughly 29 billion dollar valuation through rounds valuing it in the hundreds of billions. In 2026 Thrive raised its largest fund (over 10 billion dollars) and OpenAI took an ownership stake in Thrive Holdings, deepening an unusually entangled investor relationship.

## Their future thesis (one line)
Frontier AI is the defining technology and investment of the era, and the winning move is to concentrate capital and operating ownership around the leading lab while using its models to transform whole categories of traditional enterprise work.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "OpenAI takes an ownership stake in Thrive Holdings" partnership post (December 2025), https://x.com/JoshuaKushner/status/1995484976951767350
  Kushner announces a strategic partnership in which OpenAI becomes an equity owner in Thrive Holdings to deliver frontier technology to customers. Notably the deal involves no cash, with OpenAI contributing engineers, models, and deep integration into companies Holdings acquires. A concrete bet that AI gets deployed by buying and re-tooling real operating businesses, starting with accounting and IT.

  OpenAI's own writeup of the partnership, framing the first targets as high-volume, rules-driven, workflow-heavy functions where its platform delivers immediate gains. It is a template for how frontier models reach the enterprise: not as a tool sold in, but as an owner-operator transforming a company from inside. Useful as a primary-source signal of the AI-rolls-up-services thesis.
