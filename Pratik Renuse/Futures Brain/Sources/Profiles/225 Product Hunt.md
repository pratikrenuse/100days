# Product Hunt

#source
Domain: News, feeds and aggregators
Verified: June 2026

## What it is
A daily launch board where makers post new tech products and the community upvotes them. It is the closest thing to a live ticker of what indie builders and startups are shipping, especially in software and AI.

## Access
- URL: https://www.producthunt.com
- RSS feed: https://www.producthunt.com/feed (working; returns Atom XML of recent launches. Human-readable leaderboards live at /leaderboard/daily, /weekly, /monthly. There is also an llms.txt at https://www.producthunt.com/llms.txt)
- Paywall: no

## What it is good for (future signal)
Product Hunt shows the direction of travel before it consolidates: the same idea appearing in five launches a week is a trend forming. In 2026 the board is dominated by AI agents that take actions for you (email, to-do, sales, e-commerce, publishing), which is a strong signal about where consumer and SMB software is heading. Use it to track product categories that are becoming "table stakes," and to read taglines as a window into how builders are positioning AI to buyers.

Captured from the "Best of Product Hunt: June 2026" monthly leaderboard (https://www.producthunt.com/leaderboard/monthly/2026/6):
- "Fundraisly" (June 2026), https://www.producthunt.com/products/fundraisly

- "Upstream" (June 2026), https://www.producthunt.com/products/upstream-3
  An inbox "designed for humans and agents," reframing email as a shared workspace where AI agents act alongside people. Points to the agent-native rebuild of everyday tools.
- "Bond" (June 2026), https://www.producthunt.com/products/bond-12
  A to-do list that "does itself," using an agent to complete tasks rather than just track them. Captures the shift from productivity software that organizes work to software that performs it.
- "Publora" (June 2026), https://www.producthunt.com/products/publora
  A publishing API that lets agents post across roughly ten social platforms. Infrastructure for a world where agents, not humans, operate the distribution layer.
- "Framer 3.0" (June 2026), https://www.producthunt.com/products/framer
  A major update putting AI agents directly on the design canvas, plus team branching and a creator community, with the option to connect external coding agents. Shows agents being embedded into mainstream creative tools rather than bolted on.
