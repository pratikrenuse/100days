## Who they are
Co-founder and CEO of World Labs (the spatial-intelligence startup behind Marble), professor at Stanford, and co-director of the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI (HAI). Known as the "Godmother of AI" for creating ImageNet, which catalyzed the deep learning era.

## Their future thesis (one line)
Language is not enough: the next frontier is "spatial intelligence," world models that understand, generate, and reason about 3D physical space, which will unlock creativity, robotics, science, and embodied AI over the coming decade.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "From Words to Worlds: Spatial Intelligence is AI's Next Frontier" (November 2025), https://drfeifei.substack.com/p/from-words-to-worlds-spatial-intelligence
  Her landmark manifesto arguing that today's language models are "wordsmiths in the dark," eloquent but ungrounded in the physical world. Lays out three capabilities truly spatially intelligent world models must have: generating worlds with a storyteller's imagination, navigating with a first responder's fluency, and reasoning about space with scientific precision. Positions spatial intelligence as the foundation for the next era of AI, with impact across creativity, embodied intelligence, and science.

- Marble launch (World Labs' first product) (November 2025), https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/12/fei-fei-lis-world-labs-speeds-up-the-world-model-race-with-marble-its-first-commercial-product/
  Coverage of Marble, a model that generates physically coherent 3D worlds from text or image prompts, framed as the first concrete step toward a spatially intelligent world model. Evidence that her thesis is now shipping, not just theory. Relevant to the futures of gaming, robotics, design, and simulation.

- "After LLMs: Spatial Intelligence and World Models" (talk, 2025), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=60iW8FZ7MJU
  A talk with co-founder Justin Johnson explaining why world models are the necessary next step beyond LLMs and how spatial understanding generalizes to robotics and the physical economy. Good narrative overview of the World Labs bet. Useful for understanding the technical case, not just the vision.

- Her human-centered AI advocacy via Stanford HAI, https://hai.stanford.edu
  Her ongoing institutional work arguing AI must be developed to augment humanity, with attention to policy, ethics, and broad benefit. Balances the startup-builder side with a governance and public-interest voice. Relevant to the "how should society steer this" question.
