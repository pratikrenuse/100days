## Who they are
Founder of Platformer, an independent newsletter on the intersection of tech platforms, AI, and democracy, and former senior editor at The Verge. Co-hosts the Hard Fork podcast with Kevin Roose. Note: the old "platformer.substack.com" venue is outdated; Platformer now publishes on its own site (Ghost) at platformer.news.

## Their future thesis (one line)
AI is genuinely transformative and genuinely dangerous, so the responsible posture is to take it seriously rather than dismiss it, and the near future means AI reshaping software jobs, the media business, and human relationships faster than institutions can adapt.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "11 predictions for 2026" (late 2025), https://www.platformer.news/2026-tech-predictions-ai-bubble-openai-meta-google/
  His annual forecast piece. Predicts AI's dramatic impact on software engineering (coding benchmarks near saturation, slower hiring, possible large layoffs), AI companions becoming a cultural and political flashpoint, and an LLM-powered cyberattack giving safety advocates concrete leverage. A concise, datable set of near-term bets to track. Partial paywall.
- "The phony comforts of AI skepticism" (December 2024), https://www.platformer.news/ (search "phony comforts")
  Argues the "AI is fake and sucks" crowd takes false comfort in dismissing AI, and that the technology is more real and more dangerous than skeptics admit. A defining statement of his "take it seriously" stance; drew sharp pushback from critics like Gary Marcus, so read it as one side of a live debate. Partial paywall.
- "How we're shaking up Platformer for the AI era" (2026), https://www.platformer.news/platformer-schedule-changes-ai-automation/
  Explains his strategic pivot toward original reporting and scoops and away from aggregation, on the logic that AI is commoditizing link roundups and analysis. A useful real-world data point on how AI is already reshaping the economics of media and knowledge work. Free.
- "Why I'm having trouble covering AI" (2025), https://www.platformer.news/why-im-having-trouble-covering-ai/
  Reflects on how the huge spread of credible expert views on existential risk makes it genuinely hard to calibrate AI coverage. Honest and useful for the futures brain because it models the uncertainty rather than papering over it. Free.
