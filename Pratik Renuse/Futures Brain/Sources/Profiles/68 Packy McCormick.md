## Who they are
Writer and venture investor behind Not Boring, a widely read Substack (around 264,000 subscribers in 2026) that blends technology strategy, optimism, and storytelling. As of 2026 he is a leading popularizer of the "technology will eat every industry" thesis and runs an associated early-stage fund.

## Their future thesis (one line)
Over the next 20 years startups will displace incumbents across energy, healthcare, defense, manufacturing, education, and more by building products so much better that the shift is inevitable, and the next leap in AI comes less from language models than from "world models" that understand physical reality.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "Everything is Technology" (May 13, 2025), https://www.notboring.co/p/everything-is-technology
  His most succinct manifesto: a wave of billion-dollar exits and multi-billion-dollar raises is just the appetizer, and the next two decades will see trillions in new value as technology companies displace incumbents across energy, healthcare, defense, manufacturing, education, housing, finance, and aerospace. He uses numbers and history to argue the displacement is inevitable once products get decisively better, and that even fragmented sectors like law, accounting, and consulting become addressable markets. A core optimism-with-receipts piece.

- "World Models: Computing the Uncomputable" (Mar 2026, with Pim de Witte), https://www.notboring.co/p/world-models
  A roughly 19,000-word deep dive arguing that "world models," AI systems that internally simulate physical reality, are a more promising path to general intelligence than language models alone. It covers the philosophy, history, key papers, progress, competing approaches, and what controllable world models would mean for robotics and machines acting on our behalf. His clearest statement of where he thinks the real AI frontier is heading.

- "Us Against Spacetime" (2025), https://www.notboring.co/p/us-against-spacetime
  Frames the grand human project as a long fight to overcome the constraints of physics, energy, and distance, casting technological progress as humanity's tool against spacetime itself. It is his most philosophical statement of why building matters and where the species is headed. Useful as a high-altitude "why the future is worth building" frame.

- "Welcome to Not Boring World" (Jan 2026), https://www.notboring.co/p/welcome-to-not-boring-world
  Announces a new paid section where outside founders, researchers, and investors publish their best long-form ideas, positioning Not Boring as a platform rather than one writer's blog. It signals a bet that distributed expert writing about the future scales better than a single voice. Relevant as a media-of-the-future move and a directory of emerging thinkers.

## Hooks for the brain
- "Everything is Technology" is a ready-made TAM map of the future: nine-plus industries he expects startups to eat, useful scaffolding for any sector-by-sector futures post.
- McCormick is the optimist-builder voice aimed squarely at founders and operators, the most marketer-adjacent thinker on this list, and his essays model how to make dense tech legible and fun.
- "World Models" is an early, accessible flag that the next AI wave may be physical/spatial rather than text, a contrarian counter to the LLM-centric consensus the other profiles assume.
