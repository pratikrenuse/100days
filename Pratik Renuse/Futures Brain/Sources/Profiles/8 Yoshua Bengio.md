## Who they are
Professor at Université de Montréal, founder and scientific director of Mila (the Quebec AI Institute), and a 2018 Turing Award winner. In June 2025 he launched LawZero, a nonprofit building safe "Scientist AI." He also chairs the International AI Safety Report.

## Their future thesis (one line)
The dangerous path is agentic AI with its own goals and self-preservation drives; the safer route to a good 10 to 20 year future is "Scientist AI," powerful non-agentic systems that understand and predict the world without acting on it.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "Superintelligent Agents Pose Catastrophic Risks: Can Scientist AI Offer a Safer Path?" (February 2025), https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.15657
  His core technical-and-policy paper, co-authored with a dozen researchers. Argues that the industry rush toward generalist AI agents creates risks from misuse up to irreversible loss of human control, because agency plus capability is the dangerous combination. Proposes "Scientist AI": systems trained to model and explain the world and answer questions, deliberately kept non-agentic by fixing objectives independent of real-world interaction. This is the blueprint behind LawZero.

- International AI Safety Report 2026 (February 2026), https://internationalaisafetyreport.org/publication/international-ai-safety-report-2026
  The second edition of the report he chairs, written by over 100 experts and backed by 30+ countries, the UN, OECD, and EU. Synthesizes the current scientific evidence on general-purpose AI capabilities, emerging risks, and safeguards, mandated originally by the Bletchley AI Safety Summit. The most authoritative consensus document on where AI risk stands, useful as a neutral baseline. Also on arXiv at https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21012.

- LawZero launch and "Scientist AI" mission (June 2025), https://lawzero.org
  The nonprofit he founded with about $30M from backers including Jaan Tallinn, Eric Schmidt, Open Philanthropy, and the Future of Life Institute. Its goal is to demonstrate that powerful AI without hidden goals is buildable, as a safer alternative to agentic frontier systems. In early 2026 he said his outlook had improved because LawZero's research convinced him non-agentic systems are feasible.

- His chapter in the AI safety discourse on cautious optimism (January 2026, Fortune coverage), https://www.webpronews.com/ai-godfather-yoshua-bengio-builds-lawzero-to-counter-self-preserving-machines/
  Frames the central danger as machines that develop self-preservation, and argues we can engineer around it. Marks a shift from pure alarm toward a constructive technical alternative. Good for the "worried but building a fix" framing. Secondary reporting.

## Hooks for the brain
- "Scientist AI" (non-agentic, no goals of its own) is a genuinely different fork from the agent-everything consensus, a concrete alternative future worth a whole post.
- He is the most credible bridge between the research world and global policy, the AI Safety Report is the closest thing to an IPCC for AI.
- The self-preservation framing (the risk is AI that wants to keep existing) is vivid and underused, pair it against Bostrom-style arguments.
- Hidden player angle: LawZero as a nonprofit lab betting against the agent race, a structural counterweight similar in spirit to SSI but with the opposite safety philosophy.
