## Who they are
Co-founder and chief AGI scientist of Google DeepMind, credited with popularizing the term "AGI". He leads where DeepMind focuses, supervises recruitment, and steers its AI safety and alignment work, and is known for both bullish AGI timelines and long-standing concern about existential risk.

## Their future thesis (one line)
There is roughly a 50 percent chance of human-level AGI by around 2028, which makes aligning superhuman systems and preparing society for a post-AGI world the defining problems of the next few years.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- Dwarkesh Podcast: "2028 AGI, new architectures, aligning superhuman models" (October 2023), https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/shane-legg
  Legg lays out his AGI timeline as a log-normal distribution with a mean of 2028 and gives a 50 percent chance of AGI by then, absent a major disruption. He discusses what new architectures and alignment methods superhuman models will require. The single best source for his quantified forecast and reasoning.
- "The arrival of AGI" (Google DeepMind talk, recent), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l3u_FAv33G0
  A talk on what the arrival of AGI means and how to build a world where it helps humanity. It pairs his timeline optimism with his insistence that safety and societal preparation cannot lag the capability curve. Useful as his current framing of the transition, not just the prediction.
- AGI definition clarification post (January 2025), https://x.com/ShaneLegg/status/1877674960770007042
  Legg clarifies the criterion he has used inside DeepMind for a decade: AGI is matching regular people on cognitive tasks they can do, and by that bar we are not there yet but may be within a few years. It pins down what he actually means by AGI, which matters because the term is used loosely. A precise yardstick for judging whether later capability claims qualify.
- Post-AGI preparation, economist hire (2025), https://www.linkedin.com/posts/medickinson_the-arrival-of-agi-shane-legg-co-founder-activity-7420888674218840064-UmL7
  Reporting that Legg moved to bring economics expertise into thinking about a post-AGI world. The signal for futures work is that a frontier lab co-founder is now planning seriously for the societal and economic aftermath, not just the technical arrival. Marks a shift from "can we build it" to "what happens after".

## Hooks for the brain
- Feeds the "AGI is near and alignment is the bottleneck" thesis: a credible, quantified near-term timeline from someone who coined the term. Use as the central anchor for AGI-arrival scenarios.
- Use his cognitive-tasks-regular-people-can-do definition as the operational test for any "we have AGI" claim; it filters hype from substance.
- The post-AGI economist hire is a hook for second-order scenarios (labor, distribution, governance after AGI); pair his alignment focus with Kaplan and Sutton on the capability side to separate "will it arrive" from "will it be safe".
