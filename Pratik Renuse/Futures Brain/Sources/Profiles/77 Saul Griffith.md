## Who they are
Australian-American inventor, engineer, and electrification advocate. Founder of Rewiring America, Rewiring Australia, and Rewiring Aotearoa (nonprofits pushing to decarbonize by electrifying everything), founder and chief scientist at Otherlab, and co-founder of Makani Power and Instructables. MacArthur "genius" Fellow. The leading popularizer of the "electrify everything" household-and-grid thesis.

## Their future thesis (one line)
The fastest, cheapest route to decarbonization is to electrify every machine in daily life (cars, heating, cooking, water heating) and power them with cheap rooftop and grid solar plus batteries, which makes household energy dramatically cheaper and electricity eventually almost free.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "Pax Electricana" (19 Apr 2026), https://energyandstuff.substack.com/p/pax-electricana
  His most ambitious recent essay, subtitled a middle-power strategy for a decentralized electro-industrial order. He argues that cheap distributed electricity reorders geopolitics, letting middle powers and households escape dependence on imported fossil fuels and build a more decentralized industrial base. A genuine 10-to-20-year worldview piece.

- "Electricity 'too cheap to meter' is so... 1975. FREE ELECTRICITY is 2025!" (7 Nov 2025), https://energyandstuff.substack.com/p/electricity-too-cheap-to-meter-is
  Uses the Australian electricity market as a preview of where the world is heading: so much cheap rooftop solar that electricity trends toward free at times. Argues the US could get there too if it makes rooftop solar cheaper, and that this changes the economics of everything downstream.

- "The Iran war exposes our stupidity. Australia should stop buying oil and electrify everything." (4 May 2026), https://energyandstuff.substack.com/p/the-iran-war-exposes-our-stupidity
  An energy-security argument: Australia exports most of its crude and buys back refined fuel, and electrifying end uses would save vast sums daily while removing geopolitical exposure. A concrete national case study of his electrify-everything thesis.

- "Electrify: An Optimist's Playbook for Our Clean Energy Future" (2021, MIT Press), https://www.rewiringamerica.org/research/electrify-the-book-saul-griffith
  His foundational book and the manifesto behind Rewiring America. Lays out a detailed, machine-by-machine blueprint for decarbonizing the US by electrifying homes, vehicles, and industry, framed as a jobs-and-prosperity program, not a sacrifice. The reference text for the whole movement.

## Hooks for the brain
- Griffith is the electrify-everything anchor, the household-and-demand-side complement to the supply-side optimists (Seba, Naam, Handmer). His unit of analysis is the machine in your home, which makes the transition tangible.
- His Australia-as-leading-indicator framing is a distinctive lens: watch Australia to see the rooftop-solar future arriving first.
- Hidden players: the Rewiring network (America, Australia, Aotearoa) as a policy-and-advocacy engine; also the "free electricity" idea as a provocative futures frame worth its own post.
