## Who they are
Founder and former CEO of Scale AI, now Meta's chief AI officer leading Meta Superintelligence Labs after Meta's roughly 14 billion dollar investment in Scale in 2025. In 2026 he shipped Meta's first model from the new Muse series (Muse Spark), positioning Meta to compete with OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google.

## Their future thesis (one line)
Within roughly five years, superintelligent AI will run the frontiers of research and produce some of the biggest scientific discoveries in human history, with humans increasingly interpreting what the machines find rather than making the discoveries themselves.
