## Who they are
Neurosurgeon and engineer (MD Harvard, PhD MIT) who was on the eight-member founding team of Neuralink, left in 2018, and co-founded Precision Neuroscience in 2021, where he is Chief Science Officer. He champions a minimally invasive, surface-electrode approach to brain-computer interfaces designed for clinical scale and safety.

## Their future thesis (one line)
The core science of BCI is effectively solved; the next decade is about turning it into safe, scalable, reversible medical devices that restore digital and physical function to paralyzed patients, implanted through low-risk surgery.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "Building BCI For the Real World: A Chat With Dr. Benjamin Rapoport of Precision Neuroscience" (Jul 25, 2024), https://neurotechnology.substack.com/p/rapoport-precision-bci
  A detailed interview laying out his thesis that BCI must be safe, scalable, and minimally invasive rather than research-grade. He explains why Precision uses a thin surface array (Layer 7) that sits on the brain without penetrating it, enabling safe explantation and hardware upgrades over a patient's life. He frames the near-term goal as giving quadriplegic patients fluid, thought-based control of standard computers.

- Precision Neuroscience world record for electrodes placed on the human brain (May 28, 2024), https://www.globenewswire.com/en/news-release/2024/05/28/2889069/0/en/Precision-Neuroscience-Announces-World-Record-for-Number-of-Electrodes-Placed-On-Human-Brain.html
  Announces a record number of electrodes placed on a human brain at once, demonstrating the high-resolution recording his approach can achieve without penetrating tissue. It is a concrete milestone toward the data density needed to decode intention. Signals that surface arrays can compete with penetrating electrodes on signal quality.

- "Initial experience with the Precision Neuroscience Layer 7 micro-electrocorticography interface for real-time intraoperative neural decoding" (Neurosurgical Focus, 2026), https://thejns.org/focus/view/journals/neurosurg-focus/60/2/article-pE3.xml
  A peer-reviewed clinical report on using the Layer 7 micro-ECoG array for real-time neural decoding during surgery. It moves the company's claims from press release into the clinical literature. Marks the transition of surface-array BCI from prototype to documented clinical use.

- FDA implantable BCI Collaborative Community (iBCI-CC) clinical practice guidelines work, https://www.ibci-cc.org/
  Rapoport chairs the workgroup writing the first clinical practice guidelines for implantable BCI, covering the patient journey from candidacy through implantation, training, and eventual explantation. This is the regulatory and standards scaffolding that will shape how BCI scales clinically over the next 3 to 5 years. It signals that the field is professionalizing before mass deployment.
