## Who they are
Political scientist and entrepreneur; founder and president of Eurasia Group, the political-risk consultancy, and of GZERO Media. He popularized the "G-Zero" idea of a leaderless world and is among the most visible public interpreters of global political risk, best known for the firm's annual "Top Risks" forecast.

## Their future thesis (one line)
We live in a "G-Zero" world with no single power or coalition willing to lead, and the defining risk of the coming decade is that this leadership vacuum, combined with a fracturing US-led order and the disruptive force of AI, drives instability that existing institutions are unprepared to manage.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "Eurasia Group Top Risks 2026" (January 5, 2026), https://www.eurasiagroup.net/issues/top-risks-2026
  The firm's flagship annual forecast names the "US political revolution" as the top risk, arguing the US is dismantling the very global order it built. Other listed risks include China's position in the global energy transition, US intervention in the Western Hemisphere, a weakening European center, and pressure on AI business models, a concrete map of the year's geopolitical fault lines.

- "Ian Bremmer: US ending its own global order" (Axios interview, January 5, 2026), https://www.axios.com/2026/01/05/ian-bremmer-us-ending-own-global-order
  Summarizes his central 2026 argument that the biggest geopolitical shock is internal to the US, as its government steps back from the postwar liberal order it long underwrote. It frames the decade ahead as defined by American retrenchment and the resulting global power vacuum.

- "The Power of Crisis: How Three Threats and Our Response Will Change the World" (2022), https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/The-Power-of-Crisis/Ian-Bremmer/9781982167516
  Argues that three accelerating crises, pandemics, climate change, and disruptive technologies including AI, are global by nature and cannot be solved by any country alone. It makes the case that shared threats could force cooperation and new institutions, an optimistic counterpoint to his G-Zero diagnosis.

- "GZERO World with Ian Bremmer" (ongoing weekly show and podcast), https://www.gzeromedia.com/gzero-world
  His running interview and analysis program where he interprets live geopolitics, from US-China relations to AI governance and Middle East and Ukraine conflicts. This is the best place to track how his risk framework evolves week to week.

- "Eurasia Group Top Risks 2025: The G-Zero Wins" (January 2025), https://www.eurasiagroup.net/issues/top-risks-2025
  The prior year's forecast argued the leaderless G-Zero order had effectively triumphed, with no actor able or willing to set global rules. Useful as the immediate precursor that sets up the 2026 "US political revolution" thesis.
