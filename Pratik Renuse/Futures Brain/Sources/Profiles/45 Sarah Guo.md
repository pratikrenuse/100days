## Who they are
Founder and managing partner of Conviction, a venture firm she launched in 2022 (after nearly a decade at Greylock) purpose-built to back AI-native "Software 3.0" companies. Co-host of No Priors, a widely followed podcast interviewing leading AI founders, researchers, and engineers.

## Their future thesis (one line)
AI is creating "Software 3.0," a wave of net-new intelligent applications, and durable value will concentrate in the "untrainable" work that lives in a company's private reality, while anything benchmarkable rapidly commoditizes.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "The Untrainable" (2025), https://saranormous.substack.com/p/the-untrainable
  Her sharpest thesis essay: argues that anything that can be put on a benchmark is already commoditizing, so the valuable, defensible work is "illegible by construction." An application earns its place in the "untrainable corner" by arranging a company's private reality so a model can act on it, work won by teams that put domain-specialized engineers next to the customer. A strong frame for predicting where economic value in AI actually accrues over the next decade.
- No Priors with Jensen Huang on the "superintelligence century" (early 2026), https://www.youtube.com/@NoPriorsPodcast
  Guo and Elad Gil sit down with NVIDIA's CEO to take stock of AI at the start of 2026 and discuss what a "superintelligence century" could look like. High-signal for the long-horizon compute and infrastructure view from the company powering most frontier AI. Audio/video.
- No Priors on agents replacing tools (Notion, Simon Last) (2026), https://www.youtube.com/@NoPriorsPodcast
  A conversation on the shift in productivity software from "humans do the work" to "humans manage a swarm of agents." Concrete illustration of how the agentic transition reshapes everyday knowledge work. Audio/video.
- No Priors / Latent Space crossover at Microsoft Build on autonomous agents and software engineering (2026), https://www.youtube.com/@NoPriorsPodcast
  A crossover episode (with swyx) on how autonomous coding agents are reshaping the software engineer's role. Useful for the future-of-work and future-of-software-labor thread. Audio/video.
