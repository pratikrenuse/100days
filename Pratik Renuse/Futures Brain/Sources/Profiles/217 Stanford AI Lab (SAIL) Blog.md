#source

## What it is
The official blog of the Stanford Artificial Intelligence Laboratory, where SAIL students, faculty, and researchers share their work with the general public. It leans toward primary research: conference paper roundups and technical explainers of new methods.

## Access
- URL: https://ai.stanford.edu/blog/
- RSS feed: https://ai.stanford.edu/blog/feed.xml
- Paywall: no

## What it is good for (future signal)
A direct line into frontier academic AI before it becomes product. The conference roundups (ICLR, CVPR, NeurIPS) act as a map of where the best PhD work is concentrating, so they flag the research themes that tend to surface in industry one to three years later. Useful for spotting which capabilities (reasoning, agents, spatial intelligence, world models) the field is investing in next.
