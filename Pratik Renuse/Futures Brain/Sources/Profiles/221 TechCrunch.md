## What it is
A high-volume technology news site focused on startups, venture funding, and the major platform companies. Fast, deal-driven coverage: who raised what, who shipped what, and who acquired whom across the tech and AI industry.

## Access
- URL: https://techcrunch.com/
- RSS feed: https://techcrunch.com/feed/
- Paywall: no (most coverage free; some events and premium research are paid)

## What it is good for (future signal)
The clearest read on where capital is flowing, which is an early indicator of which technologies get built out at scale. Funding rounds, valuations, and acquisitions reveal the bets the market is making 3 to 7 years ahead. The AI category in particular tracks the concentration of money and talent (for example into OpenAI, Anthropic, and physical-AI startups) that will shape the competitive landscape.
