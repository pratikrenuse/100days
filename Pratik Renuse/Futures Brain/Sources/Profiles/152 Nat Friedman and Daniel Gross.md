## Who they are
As of June 2026, both are at Meta. Nat Friedman (former CEO of GitHub, 2018 to 2021, and longtime AI investor) is head of product at Meta Superintelligence Labs, working alongside Alexandr Wang. Daniel Gross (co-founder of Cue, which Apple bought in 2013, ex Y Combinator partner, and co-founder of Safe Superintelligence with Ilya Sutskever) left SSI in mid 2025 and now, in his own words, "runs compute for Meta." The pair co-invested for years through their NFDG partnership and the AI Grant program, and built the Andromeda GPU cluster to give their startups compute; both stepped back from NFDG when they joined Meta, and Andromeda has since spun out as an independent company.

## Their future thesis (one line)
AI capability is bottlenecked less by ideas than by compute and execution speed, so the winners will be whoever can marshal the most compute, build real products fast, and treat raising the ceiling of what is possible as close to a moral duty.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "An Interview with Daniel Gross and Nat Friedman About Models, Margins, and Moats" (January 23, 2025), https://stratechery.com/2025/an-interview-with-daniel-gross-and-nat-friedman-about-models-margins-and-moats/
  Their most recent joint Stratechery conversation, recorded just as the Stargate buildout was announced and the DeepSeek release rattled the market. They argue over where durable advantage in AI will actually sit once raw model quality commoditizes: in distribution, in compute access, or in product, rather than in the model weights themselves. A useful snapshot of how two of the field's most active investors were reasoning about margins and moats right before they joined Meta. (Paywalled.)

- "An Interview with Nat Friedman and Daniel Gross Reasoning About AI" (February 29, 2024), https://stratechery.com/2024/an-interview-with-nat-friedman-and-daniel-gross-reasoning-about-ai/
  A conversation covering Gemini, Groq, Sora, and Mistral, where they make the case that the next major leap would come from AI that can genuinely reason rather than just pattern match. This proved an early read on the reasoning model wave that followed. It captures their habit of forecasting the next capability frontier well before it is obvious. (Paywalled.)

- "The $1.5B Compute Startup Helping AI's Hottest Companies Find GPUs" (March 18, 2026), https://www.upstartsmedia.com/p/andromeda-ai-compute-startup-raises-60m
  A reported feature on Andromeda, the GPU cluster Friedman and Gross spun up through NFDG to feed their AI Grant startups (Cursor, Perplexity, Pika, ElevenLabs and others), which has since become an independent company chasing what its backers call a multi trillion dollar market in tradable compute. It confirms that both men left NFDG and joined Meta in 2025, and notes Gross publicly said in January 2026 he was building a compute desk for Meta. The piece is the clearest external evidence of their core bet: that access to compute is the scarce commodity that will shape AI's next decade. (Free.)

- "Some things I believe" on Nat Friedman's homepage (undated, current as of June 2026), https://nat.org/
  Friedman's personal worldview laid out as a list of convictions: that humans have a right, maybe a duty, to reshape the universe; that speed matters because "slow is fake" and you learn faster by contacting reality more often; that the efficient market hypothesis is a lie and the best opportunities live where it is wrong; and that small empowered teams beat large ones. It is the most direct statement of the optimist, build fast, raise the ceiling philosophy that drives both his investing and his work at Meta. (Free, undated.)

- Daniel Gross homepage bio (current as of June 2026), https://dcgross.com/
  Gross's single page bio, valuable mainly as primary source confirmation of his June 2026 status: it leads with "I run compute for Meta," then lists his investing track record with Friedman (ElevenLabs, Perplexity, Groq, CoreWeave, Figma and more), his SSI co-founding, his Cue and Apple history, and his Y Combinator AI program. Note: the page still lists him as a Safe Superintelligence co-founder without flagging his 2025 departure, so treat parts of it as not fully updated. (Free.)

## Hooks for the brain
- Feeds the "compute is the binding constraint" thesis: Andromeda, the AI Grant, and Gross now literally running compute for Meta all point to GPUs and energy, not algorithms, as the limiting factor for AI over the next 10 to 20 years. Use the Upstarts piece as the anchor source and as a candidate signal for a compute-as-commodity market emerging.
- Feeds the "talent and capital consolidate inside big labs" thesis: two independent investor-operators getting absorbed into Meta Superintelligence Labs (after Meta failed to buy SSI outright) is a concrete data point on frontier AI re-centralizing around a few well capitalized players. Pair with the SSI and Meta hiring story.
- Feeds the techno-optimist worldview thread: Nat's "raise the ceiling, go fast, EMH is a lie" beliefs are a clean primary source for the accelerationist mindset shaping who builds AGI and how. Use his homepage when characterizing the values of the people steering frontier AI.
