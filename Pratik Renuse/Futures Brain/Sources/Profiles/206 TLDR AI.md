A free daily newsletter (weekdays) from TLDR, the technical newsletter company, summarizing the most important AI research papers, model releases, tools, and industry news in short, link-out blurbs aimed at engineers and technical readers. Part of the broader TLDR family of newsletters.

## What it is good for (future signal)
Use it as a clean, dated daily index of frontier AI movement: model launches, research milestones, funding, and infrastructure deals. The feed is plain XML and loads reliably, so it is a dependable scraping target for tracking the pace and direction of AI progress week over week. Strong for spotting capability jumps (scaling laws, test-time compute, recursive self-improvement) and the business consolidation around a few labs.
