## What it is
A reported explainer newsletter by journalist Timothy B. Lee (previously Ars Technica, Vox, Washington Post; holds a master's in computer science), published on Substack with a small team including Kai Williams. It publishes roughly weekly, going deeper than headline aggregators to explain how AI systems actually work and what the most important trends and debates really mean.

## What it is good for (future signal)
Use it for grounded, technically literate analysis that sits between hype and dismissal. Lee tests systems himself (self-driving cars, image models, math reasoning) and reports what they can and cannot do, plus the policy and geopolitics around them. Strong for the compute-and-geopolitics thesis (US vs China, export controls), the realistic-capabilities thesis (where AI genuinely improves and where it stalls), and the governance thesis (de facto model licensing).
