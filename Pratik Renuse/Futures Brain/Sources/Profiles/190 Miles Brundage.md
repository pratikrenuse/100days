## Who they are
Executive Director of AVERI, the AI Verification and Evaluation Research Institute, a non-profit he launched in January 2026 to make third-party auditing of frontier AI effective and universal. He previously led Policy Research and the AGI Readiness team at OpenAI from 2018 to 2024.

## Their future thesis (one line)
Advanced AI is arriving faster than our ability to govern it, so the central near-term task is building a credible ecosystem of independent third-party auditors that can verify frontier labs' safety and security claims and prevent dangerous concentration of power.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "The Launch of AVERI" (16 January 2026), https://milesbrundage.substack.com/p/the-launch-of-averi
  Announces AVERI, a 501(c)(3) think tank focused on rigorous third-party verification of frontier AI developers' safety and security claims. It argues that voluntary self-assessment by labs is not enough and that external auditing must become standard practice. This sets the institutional agenda for his work going forward.

- "We're in Triage Mode for AI Policy" (22 February 2026), https://milesbrundage.substack.com/p/were-in-triage-mode-for-ai-policy
  Contends that AI capabilities have outrun governance, so policy must prioritize the highest-leverage interventions now rather than wait for comprehensive regulation. He highlights large-scale AI auditing and a growing ecosystem of independent assessors as urgent, and frames auditing as a check on extreme concentration of power. The piece is a strategic map of what to do under time pressure.

- "AI Auditing 2027" (16 July 2025), https://milesbrundage.substack.com/p/ai-auditing-2027
  A short scenario story set in a near future where AGI has effectively arrived, exploring how geopolitics and third-party assessment of AI systems play out. It dramatizes both the stakes of verification and the practical mechanics of auditing frontier systems. The narrative format makes the case for building auditing capacity before it is desperately needed.

- "Why I'm leaving OpenAI and what I'm doing next" (2024), https://milesbrundage.substack.com/p/why-im-leaving-openai-and-what-im
  His departure note explaining that he left OpenAI to pursue AI policy research with more independence, judging that the field needs credible outside voices. It signals his shift from inside an AGI lab to building external accountability infrastructure. This is the origin point for AVERI and his current agenda.
