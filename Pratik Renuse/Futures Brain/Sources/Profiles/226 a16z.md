#source
Domain: News, feeds and aggregators

## What it is
One of the largest venture capital firms (over $90B in assets under management), and a prolific publisher of essays, investment announcements, and podcasts. Its content doubles as a worldview: techno-optimist, pro-"American Dynamism," and heavily invested in where AI, crypto, bio, and defense tech are heading.

## Access
- URL: https://a16z.com
- RSS feed: a16z.com/feed returns 404. The live content hub is https://a16z.com/news-content/ (titled "News and Content"), which lists the latest articles, investment news, and podcast episodes with authors. Treat this page as the working source of truth; a clean RSS feed could not be confirmed.
- Paywall: no

## What it is good for (future signal)
a16z is a directional bet, not neutral journalism: read it to see where serious capital is flowing and what narrative the funders are building around it. Their "Big Ideas" and "Top 100 Gen AI Apps" pieces are useful structured forecasts, and the investment announcements reveal which categories (AI infra, defense, fintech rails, robotics) they think will define the next decade. Discount the optimism, but track the direction.
