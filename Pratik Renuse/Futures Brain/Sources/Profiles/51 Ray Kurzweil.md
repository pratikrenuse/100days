## Who they are
Inventor, author, and AI pioneer. Since 2012 a Principal Researcher and AI visionary at Google working on natural language and machine intelligence. Best known futurist for the "singularity" concept and exponential-technology forecasting.

## Their future thesis (one line)
Information technology improves exponentially, so by 2029 AI reaches human level, by 2045 we merge our biological brains with the cloud, and along the way medicine reaches longevity escape velocity.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "The Singularity Is Nearer: When We Merge with AI" (June 25, 2024), https://www.thesingularityisnearer.com/
  A sequel to his 2005 book that updates his timeline using the last decade of AI, machine learning, and quantum progress. He restates 2029 for human-level AI and 2045 for the singularity, when nanobots in our capillaries connect our neocortex to cloud computing and multiply human intelligence. The book also argues AI-driven biotech will compress drug discovery and push lifespans toward escape velocity. An instant New York Times bestseller.
- "The Singularity Is Near: When Humans Transcend Biology" (2005), https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Singularity_Is_Near
  His landmark statement of the Law of Accelerating Returns: technological change is exponential, not linear, so the 21st century will see thousands of times more progress than the 20th. It forecasts the merger of human and machine intelligence and frames the singularity as a near-inevitable phase transition for civilization. The reference text for most modern singularity discourse.
- Longevity escape velocity argument, updated in The Economist (2024), https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Longevity_escape_velocity
  Kurzweil argues medical progress will soon add more than a year of life expectancy per year lived, so people who stay healthy long enough may keep outrunning death. He revised his timeline to roughly 2029 to 2035 and tied it to AI simulating biology to accelerate drug discovery. (The Economist piece itself is paywalled.)

## Hooks for the brain
- The original "exponential vs linear" mental model that nearly every AI-timeline argument now borrows from, named and dated.
- Longevity escape velocity is the underrated marketer angle: a date by which "anti-aging" stops being lifestyle and becomes a countdown.
- Kurzweil sits inside Google, so his forecasts double as a read on what a frontier lab quietly believes about merge timelines.
- Contrast pairing: Kurzweil (merge, optimism) vs Bostrom or Tegmark (control, caution) on the exact same 2045 horizon.
