## Who they are
Professor of AI and Robotics at UC Berkeley, Director of the Berkeley Robot Learning Lab and Co-Director of BAIR. Co-founder of Covariant (robotic foundation models); in August 2024 Amazon licensed Covariant's models and hired its founders, and Abbeel is now also an Amazon Scholar. Host of The Robot Brains Podcast.

## Their future thesis (one line)
Robotics is following the foundation-model playbook: large pretrained "robot brains" plus reinforcement learning and simulation will make general-purpose robots practical, and embodied AI is the next platform after LLMs.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- The Robot Brains Podcast (ongoing series), https://shows.acast.com/the-robot-brains/episodes
  Abbeel's interview show with leading AI and robotics researchers and founders. As host he repeatedly probes guests on timelines for general-purpose robots, data and simulation bottlenecks, and the transfer of foundation-model methods to the physical world. The back catalog is effectively a curated map of the embodied-AI frontier and his own framing of it.

- Covariant robotic foundation models and the 2024 Amazon deal, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pieter_Abbeel
  Covariant built foundation models for robotic picking and manipulation in warehouses; in 2024 Amazon licensed the technology and hired the founders. This is the clearest commercial test of his thesis that a single pretrained model can generalize across many robotic tasks. It also signals where large logistics players expect embodied AI to land first.

- Berkeley Robot Learning Lab research (ongoing), https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~pabbeel/
  His academic group's work on deep reinforcement learning, imitation learning, and unsupervised/foundation methods for robots. The publications trace the methods (meta-learning, RL, generative models) he believes will compose into general robot intelligence. Best primary source for the technical substance behind his public claims.

- "Deep Unsupervised Learning" course materials (ongoing), https://x.com/pabbeel/status/1761107771326689322
  Open course covering autoregressive models, flows, VAEs, GANs, and diffusion. It reflects his bet that generative and unsupervised methods are foundational to scalable robot learning. Useful for understanding the building blocks he expects to define the next decade of embodied AI.
