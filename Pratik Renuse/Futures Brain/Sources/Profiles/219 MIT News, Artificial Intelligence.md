## What it is
The artificial intelligence topic feed from MIT News, the institute's official newsroom. It reports on research coming out of MIT labs (notably CSAIL) plus institute initiatives, education programs, and events, written for a general audience.

## Access
- URL: https://news.mit.edu/topic/artificial-intelligence2
- RSS feed: https://news.mit.edu/rss/topic/artificial-intelligence2
- Paywall: no

## What it is good for (future signal)
A primary-source view of academic AI advances at the point they are announced, often months before they show up in products or other coverage. Strong for early signal on efficiency techniques (smaller, faster, cheaper models), privacy-preserving methods, agentic systems, and the societal-impact research that tends to shape later policy debates.
