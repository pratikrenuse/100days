## Who they are
Co-founder and CEO of Helion Energy, founded in 2013 out of plasma and space-propulsion research at MSNW, and backed by Sam Altman and SoftBank. He leads a fast-iteration approach to fusion using a field-reversed configuration and direct electricity recovery rather than the conventional tokamak-plus-steam-turbine path.

## Their future thesis (one line)
The fastest route to commercial fusion is to build, test, and iterate prototypes rapidly; Helion's pulsed, magneto-inertial machines that convert fusion directly to electricity can deliver power to the grid by the late 2020s, years ahead of conventional approaches.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "Helion Achieves New Industry-First Fusion Energy Milestones" (Feb 13, 2026), https://www.helionenergy.com/newsroom/helion-achieves-new-fusion-energy-milestones
  Announces that Helion's 7th-generation Polaris prototype became the first privately developed fusion machine to demonstrate measurable deuterium-tritium fusion and to reach 150 million degrees Celsius. Kirtley frames it as validation of the build-learn-iterate strategy after seven prototypes. It is the company's strongest technical proof point to date.

- "The fusion pivot: Helion CEO David Kirtley's journey from starships to sustainable star power" (GeekWire, 2026), https://www.geekwire.com/2026/the-fusion-pivot-helion-ceo-david-kirtleys-journey-from-starships-to-sustainable-star-power/
  A profile tracing Kirtley's path from space-propulsion research to fusion and his hands-on leadership style. It explains why Helion bet on field-reversed configuration and direct energy conversion instead of tokamaks. Useful for understanding the technical philosophy behind the company.

- Helion's fusion power purchase agreement with Microsoft (announced May 2023), https://www.helionenergy.com/newsroom/helion-announces-worlds-first-fusion-ppa-with-microsoft
  The world's first fusion power purchase agreement, committing Helion to deliver electricity to Microsoft by 2028 with real financial penalties if it fails. Kirtley calls 50 MW "a big first step of commercial-scale fusion." This deadline is the boldest near-term commitment in the industry and defines Helion's aggressive timeline.

- Helion begins building Orion, its first commercial machine (Jul 2025), https://www.helionenergy.com/newsroom/helion-secures-land-and-begins-building-site-of-worlds-first-fusion-power-plant
  Helion broke ground in Malaga, Washington on Orion, the machine intended to deliver fusion electricity to Microsoft. It is the physical commitment behind the 2028 promise. Signals that Helion is moving from prototypes to a grid-connected commercial build.
