# Isomorphic Labs
#source
Domain: Biotech and longevity
Verified: June 2026
## What it is
Isomorphic Labs is a Google DeepMind spinout building an AI drug-design engine on top of AlphaFold, aiming to reimagine the entire drug discovery process and, in its own framing, eventually "solve all diseases." It both partners with large pharma and develops its own internal pipeline.
## Access
- URL: https://www.isomorphiclabs.com/
- RSS / feeds / newsletter / API: News and announcements at https://www.isomorphiclabs.com/articles ; no public API (proprietary platform), no public newsletter. Follow via LinkedIn and press coverage.
- Paywall: no (corporate site and press releases are free; the platform itself is not public)
## What it does for the future
Isomorphic is the clearest test of whether AI moves from predicting protein structure to actually designing approved medicines. If its in silico design engine produces molecules that clear human trials faster and cheaper than traditional discovery, it reshapes the cost curve and timeline of pharma over the next 10 to 20 years, with knock-on effects for longevity, oncology, and immunology.
## Notable recent work (2025 to 2026)
- "Isomorphic Labs raises $600M to advance AI drug design" (March 2025), https://www.isomorphiclabs.com/articles
  In its first external raise, Isomorphic took on roughly $600 million led by Thrive Capital with Alphabet and GV participating, capital earmarked to push programs toward the clinic. The round signaled that investors were willing to fund AI-native discovery as a standalone bet rather than a DeepMind side project.
- "Isomorphic Labs gears up for first human trials of AI-designed drugs" (July 6, 2025), https://fortune.com/2025/07/06/deepmind-isomorphic-labs-cure-all-diseases-ai-now-first-human-trials/
  Fortune reported the company was staffing up and positioning oncology candidates to be the first AI-designed drugs to enter first-in-human testing. Leadership described trials as "very close," though the timeline later slipped toward end of 2026.
- "Isomorphic Labs enters cross-modality research collaboration with Johnson & Johnson" (January 20, 2026), https://www.isomorphiclabs.com/articles/isomorphic-labs-enters-into-a-research-collaboration-with-johnson-johnson
  J&J became Isomorphic's third major pharma partner after Eli Lilly and Novartis, but this deal notably extended beyond small molecules to large molecules and biologics. Isomorphic supplies in silico predictions while J&J runs the wet-lab assays and advances selected programs.
- "Isomorphic Labs secures $2.1 billion Series B to scale its AI drug design engine" (May 12, 2026), https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/isomorphic-labs-secures-2-1-billion-funding-to-scale-its-ai-drug-design-engine-302769674.html
  Led by Thrive Capital with Alphabet, GV, MGX, Temasek, CapitalG, and the UK Sovereign AI Fund participating, the raise dwarfed the 2025 round and signaled confidence ahead of the clinic. The company reiterated it expects to enter human trials by the end of 2026, with internal programs concentrated in oncology and immunology.
## Hooks for the brain
