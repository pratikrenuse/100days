## Who they are
Independent energy and climate analyst, writer, and adviser. Full name Nathaniel Bullard. Former Chief Content Officer and lead analyst at BloombergNEF, now writes independently and advises climate-tech ventures (including Halcyon). Best known for his sweeping annual decarbonization presentation, a 200-plus-slide read on the state of the energy transition.

## Their future thesis (one line)
Decarbonization is now a deep, measurable, mostly economic phenomenon driven by exponential technology cost declines, and the job is to track the real-world stocks, flows, and growth curves carefully rather than to forecast with false precision.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "Decarbonization: Parameters, Dollars and Sense, Electrons Photons Molecules" (15 Jan 2026), https://www.nathanielbullard.com/presentations
  His latest annual deck, the flagship artifact. It is a once-a-year synthesis of how we got to now, where energy and climate are going, and what is new, organized around measurement, economics, and the shift from electrons to molecules. The single best snapshot of his current worldview.

- "Why everyone missed solar's exponential growth" / The Forecaster's Gap (20 Nov 2024), https://www.exponentialview.co/p/the-forecasters-gap
  An essay for Exponential View arguing that mainstream forecasters systematically underestimated solar because linear and even exponential models fail on real technology adoption. Central to his case that the future arrives faster than institutional projections expect.

- "Sun Machines" (contributor, The Economist cover essay, 20 Jun 2024), https://www.economist.com/interactive/essay/2024/06/20/solar-power-is-going-to-be-huge
  He was quoted in and helped shape The Economist cover story on solar's coming dominance. Captures his view that solar is on track to become the largest source of electricity, with profound system-wide consequences.

- "Growth Measurements" (Decarbonization no. 16, Jun 2025), https://www.nathanielbullard.com/blog/decarbonization-no-16-growth-measurements
  A representative newsletter issue showing his method: reading present-day growth signals (Waymo ride growth, global solar generation, European gas import shifts) to infer where energy transitions are heading. Good example of his data-first, chart-led style.
