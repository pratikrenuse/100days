## What it is
A long-running technology magazine published by MIT, covering AI, computing, biotech, climate, and policy with a reported, analytical voice. Strong on framing where a technology is heading and who it shifts power toward.

## Access
- URL: https://www.technologyreview.com/
- RSS feed: https://www.technologyreview.com/feed/
- Paywall: partial (a monthly free-article allowance, then a metered paywall; "The Download" newsletter items are generally accessible)

## What it is good for (future signal)
One of the better sources for the "what does this mean in five to ten years" question. Its annual franchises ("10 Breakthrough Technologies" and, new in 2026, "10 Things That Matter in AI Right Now") are explicit attempts to call durable shifts rather than news of the day. The "Download" daily brief is a fast way to scan emerging stories with editorial judgement attached.

## Hooks for the brain
