## Who they are
Co-founder of GiveWell and Open Philanthropy who, as of 2025, joined Anthropic as a member of technical staff working on its Responsible Scaling Policy and AI safety planning. His blog Cold Takes hosts the "most important century" thesis, one of the most cited frameworks for why advanced AI makes this century pivotal.

## Their future thesis (one line)
This could be the most important century in history because AI capable of automating science and technology (PASTA) would trigger explosive growth and lock in a galaxy-scale future, so we are dangerously unprepared and should respond with vigilance rather than hype or denial.

## Key future-relevant pieces
- "The Most Important Century" series (2021, summary updated 2022), https://www.cold-takes.com/most-important-century/
  The flagship series argues that if AI can fully automate innovation, it would restart an old economic feedback loop and drive productivity toward a singularity this century, getting us to a radically unfamiliar future faster than intuition allows. He introduces PASTA (Process for Automating Scientific and Technological Advancement) as the pivotal technology and contends it is more likely than not this century. The throughline is that all plausible long-run views are "wild," so we should take the stakes seriously now.

- "This Can't Go On" (2021), https://www.cold-takes.com/this-cant-go-on/
  Shows that the few-percent annual growth we treat as normal is historically bizarre and mathematically unsustainable, since at current rates the economy would soon exceed the atoms available in the galaxy. He uses this to argue we live in a uniquely special, accelerating sliver of time rather than a stable steady state. A compact, persuasive piece for resetting intuitions about "business as usual."

- "All Possible Views About Humanity's Future Are Wild" (2021), https://www.cold-takes.com/all-possible-views-about-humanitys-future-are-wild/
  Argues that whether transformative change comes this century or in 100,000 years, we still occupy an extraordinary position as among the first beings in a galaxy about to be populated. The point is to disarm the "this is too sci-fi to take seriously" reflex by showing the boring alternative is also wild. Good framing for getting skeptics to engage.

- "Forecasting Transformative AI: What's the Burden of Proof?" (2021), https://www.cold-takes.com/forecasting-transformative-ai-whats-the-burden-of-proof/
  Tackles the meta-question of how confident we should be about transformative AI given there is no settled "field of AI forecasting" or expert consensus. He argues the available estimates (including biological-anchors methods) make something like PASTA more likely than not this century, and that absence of consensus is not a reason to dismiss it. Sets up how to reason under deep uncertainty.

## Hooks for the brain
- Note the career signal: the author of the "most important century" thesis stopped blogging and went inside a frontier lab to work on safety policy. That move is itself a data point about where serious futurists think leverage now lies.
- "This Can't Go On" is the single most-quotable intuition-reset in the whole futures canon: one chart that reframes normal growth as a historical anomaly.
- PASTA (AI that automates R&D) is a cleaner, more economic framing than "AGI" and maps directly to the recursive-self-improvement debates Alexander and the AI 2027 team obsess over.
- Post idea: "Call to Vigilance" versus "call to action" is a distinctive emotional stance (intensity and humility, not excitement or fear) that contrasts well with both doomers and accelerationists.
