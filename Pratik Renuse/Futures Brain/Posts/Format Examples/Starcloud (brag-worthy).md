Did you know there's already a company training AI in space?

Not as an experiment. In orbit, for real. It's called Starcloud, and Nvidia backs it.

Why go up there? Cost. The logic is simple:

- In the right orbit the sun barely sets, so solar panels make up to 8x more power than on the ground.
- Cooling is free. Space is freezing, and a vacuum carries heat away on its own, no fans, no water. A big AI data center on Earth can use millions of litres of water a year just to stay cool.
- In December 2025, Starcloud trained the first AI model ever run in orbit. Its next satellite carries one of Nvidia's newest chips.

The bet they're making: by the 2030s, the cheapest place to run a giant AI model might not be on Earth at all.
