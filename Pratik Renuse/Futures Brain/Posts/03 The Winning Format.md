# 03 The Winning Format (the proven structure)

This is the structure that finally worked. Build EVERY post on it so we stop reinventing the wheel. It fuses the 7 Laws ([[Posts/00 The Master Template]]) with the [[Posts/02 Robin Sharma Voice]]. Call it "The Overturned Law": you take something the reader assumes has always been true, prove it, then show the future breaking it.

The two gold-standard examples are [[Posts/The End of Discovery by Luck (2026-07-02)]] (biology) and [[Posts/The Robot in Your Kitchen (2026-07-02)]] (robots). Read them to feel the rhythm.

---

## The 7 movements (in order)

1. **THE BOLD CLAIM (1 line).** A contrarian one-liner that overturns something the reader thinks is normal. Must stop the scroll alone.
   - "Every medicine you have ever taken was found by luck."
   - "In our one human lifetime, there will be more robots on Earth than cars."

2. **THE PROOF (1 short beat).** Immediately back the bold claim with ONE concrete, verifiable example or hard number, so it lands as fact, not opinion. This is the beat we kept forgetting. A strong counter-claim needs a receipt.
   - Penicillin: Fleming left a dish out, went on holiday, came back to mold that killed the bacteria by accident. Saved hundreds of millions of lives.
   - "Right now there are maybe 15K humanoid robots in the entire world."

3. **THE OLD LAW (1 to 2 lines).** State the age-old rule in plain words, the thing that "never changed."
   - "That is how we fought disease for the whole of human history."
   - "For a hundred thousand years, if something physical needed doing, a human had to do it."

4. **THE BREAK + THE HARD NUMBER (the core).** "That era just ended." Then the staggering, dated, sourced evidence of the shift, ideally a jaw-dropping contrast of two numbers.
   - AlphaFold mapped 200 million proteins in 2 years; humans cracked 180,000 since the 1960s. A thousandfold.
   - A humanoid is a smartphone with arms: 35 grand last year, less than a used car by 2030, cheaper every year.

5. **THE TURN (1 quotable line).** The reframe. Usually read to write, or think to do, or scarce to free.
   - "It no longer just reads biology. It writes it."
   - "Your hands were the one tool that could never be mass produced. That is the law about to break."

6. **THE FUTURE PUNCH (1 beat, dated + attributed).** An impressive 10/20/30-year stat with a named source. This is the theme (the future) made concrete.
   - "Demis Hassabis, who won a Nobel for this, believes AI could help end most disease within ten to twenty years."
   - "Morgan Stanley expects one billion of these machines at work by 2050."

7. **THE CLOSE (1 line, declarative, NEVER a question).** A short, personal, futuristic wow about the reader's own life or their children. It should feel like a door closing.
   - "Your grandchildren may never hear the words there is nothing more we can do."
   - "By 2060, most of the world's robots will not be on factory floors at all. They will be home. Standing in the corner of yours."

---

## The rules that ride on top
- **Length ~900 to 1,400 characters.** Shorter is usually better. Never let it drag (the robot draft that ran ~1,800 lost the reader in the middle). If a beat isn't pulling to the next, cut it.
- **Voice = [[Posts/02 Robin Sharma Voice]]:** the wave (short punches relieved by one long line), anaphora, rule of three, direct "you", one signature word max.
- **Every claim traceable to the brain's curve notes.** A strong claim gets a proof (movement 2). No naked assertions.
- **Formatting:** staircase layout, one thought per line, blank lines between. The "." pause lines between movements (Pratik's style) are welcome. No em dashes. No emojis. No hashtags unless asked.
- **Never end on a question. Never reuse an opener, stat, or close from a recent post. Rotate human elements** ([[Posts/01 Human Elements Bank]]).

## Self-check before shipping
- [ ] Movement 1 overturns a "normal" assumption in one line
- [ ] Movement 2 proves it with a real example or number (receipt for the counter-claim)
- [ ] There is a jaw-dropping number contrast in the core
- [ ] There is one attributed future stat (10/20/30 yrs)
- [ ] It closes on a declarative, personal, futuristic wow, not a question
- [ ] Under ~1,400 chars, no drag, no em dashes, no emojis
- [ ] Every fact traces to a curve note / signal in the brain
