Window: July 28 to August 7, 2026. The brain was updated only where the week actually moved something. Raw pull: [[Latest Pull 2026-08-07]].

## Headline
The week three long-running watch items all resolved on the same three days, and a fourth thing appeared that the thesis did not have a category for. Oklo's Groves went critical on August 5, ten months from groundbreaking. Unitree priced at $9.04 billion on August 6 with DeepSeek on the strategic book. Astera Labs' August 4 earnings answered the six-week, 40% drawdown this brain had been tracking since late June: record revenue up 104%, and a Q3 guide 30% above consensus. And on August 3, a two-year-old London company called OLIX raised $312 million to build an AI chip that deliberately uses neither high-bandwidth memory nor advanced packaging, because those are the two things nobody can get. That is a motion this thesis had not seen before: not down the stack, not sideways across it, but around it. Underneath all of that, the roster moved harder than it has in months. Jeff Dean left Google after 27 years to found Discovery Loop, and Demis Hassabis stepped back from CEO of Google DeepMind to Chair, in the same announcement.
