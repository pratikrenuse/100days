Window: August 1 to August 11, 2026, with emphasis on August 4 to 11 since the last run closed on the 7th. The brain was updated only where the week actually moved something. Raw pull: [[Latest Pull 2026-08-11]].

## Headline
The chokepoint fell out of the semiconductor industry. For a year this brain has watched the binding constraint slide from packaging to memory to interconnect, and every one of those could eventually be relieved by spending enough money. This week it landed on **indium phosphide**, the crystal every AI data-centre laser is grown on, and indium is a by-product of zinc smelting, so the supply is set by how much zinc the world wants rather than by how many lasers AI needs. Lumentum's CEO said the gap is now worse than DRAM and NAND. Nokia bought a fab on August 5 to make the stuff. [[AXT]], the substrate grower, went from $3.6M of quarterly indium phosphide revenue a year ago to a record $30.7M. One layer over, the same shape showed up in ceramic capacitors, where buyers are paying two to three times market price for a part worth a fraction of a cent. Meanwhile Nvidia stopped guaranteeing power projects and started buying them, taking about 20% of [[Lancium]], and [[Eaton]] told its earnings call the US data-centre order book is now **307 gigawatts, roughly fifteen years of building at 2025 rates**.
