## Headline
The week the AI buildout started being financed like a power plant. Nvidia is reported in talks to guarantee roughly $250B so OpenAI can lease a 10 GW campus at Piketon, Ohio, built and owned by SoftBank's SB Energy on a decommissioned uranium-enrichment site, with 9.2 GW of its own gas generation attached because the grid cannot deliver. Two substitutions in one deal: the tenant borrows on the chip vendor's credit, and the campus builds its own power. Underneath it, the tripwire this brain has been watching for a month came back negative: GE Vernova's Q2 (July 22) raised the year-end gas backlog-plus-slots guide from 110 GW to 125 GW on record $176B backlog. The four advanced reactors that reached criticality under the DOE Reactor Pilot Program earlier this month (logged in the 07-14 run, Aalo-X being the fourth on July 4) softened nothing. Five new hidden players entered the brain: SB Energy, Sargent and Lundy, Etched, Cerebras and Humanoid.
