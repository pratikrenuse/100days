# Helion

#player
**Proves:** [[Energy is the Ceiling]] (the longer bet that lifts the ceiling)
**Awareness:** Building (fusion is hyped; Helion's specifics, and how close the permitting is, are not widely understood)

## One line
A fusion startup taking the unusual direct-electricity path, now holding the operating licenses for its first plant and reporting real fusion on its prototype.

## The chokepoint it owns
Not a chokepoint owner like SK Hynix or GE Vernova. Helion is the opposite bet: if fusion delivers firm, clean power this decade, the gas-turbine and grid chokepoints that cap AI today matter far less. It is a wager on lifting the ceiling rather than owning it.

## Key facts (dated)
- Polaris prototype reached a 150-million-degree-C plasma and demonstrated measurable deuterium-tritium fusion. (June 2026, ANS / The Fusion Report)
- Secured operating licenses for its first power plant, Orion, now under construction in Malaga, Washington. (June 2026)
- Peer marker: Commonwealth Fusion filed a grid-interconnection application for its ARC plant with PJM, an industry first, and published papers asserting ARC is net-electricity-positive; CFS closed a Series C on June 4, 2026.
- Has a signed power offtake agreement with Microsoft (2023), one of the first commercial fusion contracts.

## Why it matters for the future
The tell this week was paperwork, not physics. Licensing and grid-interconnection filings are how you know a technology has shifted from "possible" to "someone is treating it as a business." If even one of these plants puts electrons on a grid this decade, the [[Energy is the Ceiling]] thesis gets a new ending.

## Awareness watch
Still obscure enough to hook. The dated catalyst to watch is first electricity delivered to a grid, or a second major offtake deal. If fusion becomes a daily headline, retire as a fresh hook.
