# Agility Robotics

#player
**Proves:** [[The Physical World Gets Automated]] (the US answer to Unitree: a public price on humanoid labour)
**Awareness:** Building (robotics circles know Digit; the general public does not know a US humanoid maker is about to have a stock ticker)

## One line
The Oregon company whose warehouse humanoid Digit is about to make it the only US-listed pure-play humanoid stock, with real orders instead of demos.

## The chokepoint it owns
Deployment head start. Digit has been doing paid work in warehouses (GXO, Schaeffler) longer than any Western rival, and Agility owns its own factory. In a race where the winner is decided by cost curves and reliability data, being first to scaled, boring, repetitive paid work is the moat.

## Key facts (dated)
- June 24, 2026 (broke wide July 5 to 8): announced merger with Churchill Capital Corp XI at a ~$2.5B valuation, raising $620M+ gross (~$420M SPAC trust plus a $200M PIPE led by Foxconn), the largest capital raise in humanoid robotics to date. Nasdaq ticker AGLT, closing expected before end of 2026. (Agility / SEC Form 425; TechCrunch July 5; Yahoo Finance July 8)
- $300M+ in multi-year orders for Digit v5, billed as the first AI-enabled "cooperatively safe" humanoid (built to work near people, not behind fences). (June 24, 2026, Agility)
- Salem, Oregon factory (RoboFab) designed to produce up to 10,000 units per year. (June 24, 2026, Agility)
- Backers and partners: DCVC, Nvidia, Amazon, SoftBank Vision Fund 2, Foxconn, Schaeffler, Playground Global. (June 2026)
- CEO Peggy Johnson (TechCrunch interview, July 5, 2026): explicitly not promising home robots any time soon; the business is warehouses and logistics first. A notable anti-hype stance in a hype-heavy sector.

## Why it matters for the future
Unitree gives China's humanoid industry a public price; Agility gives the US one. Two listings, two continents, weeks apart, means the sector graduates from venture stories to quarterly numbers, and capital starts flooding the layer below (actuators, gears, magnets, batteries). The 10 to 20 year read: physical labour gets a ticker before it gets a face, and the first companies to publish real cost-per-hour-of-work numbers set the benchmark every robot after them must beat.

## Awareness watch
Building. Mainstreaming triggers: the AGLT listing day, the first public quarterly report, or a household-name customer announcement. Once "the American robot stock" is a retail phrase, retire as a hook and drop to the component layer (Harmonic Drive, actuators, magnets), same rule as [[Unitree]].
