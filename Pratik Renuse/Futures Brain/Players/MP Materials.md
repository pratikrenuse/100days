## One line
The owner of America's only operating rare-earth mine (Mountain Pass, California) and its first scaled magnet plant, now a named target in the US-China rare-earth standoff.

## The chokepoint it owns
Rare-earth refining and, increasingly, sintered neodymium magnets. The magnets that turn current into motion sit inside almost everything that matters for the next decade: humanoid robot joints, EV motors, wind turbines, drones, precision-guided weapons. China refines ~90% of rare earths and makes the overwhelming majority of finished magnets. MP is the one Western name trying to close that loop end to end, mine to magnet.

## Key facts (dated)
- China's Ministry of Commerce added 10 US firms, including MP Materials and USA Rare Earth, to its export-control list. Entity-specific, not a new blanket ban. (June 22, 2026, StockTitan)
- MP is suing USA Rare Earth over alleged theft of "grain boundary diffusion" magnet formulations via a former employee; the rival filed its denial in the Texas Business Court this month. (June 2026, Mining.com)
- MP expects first magnet sales from its Independence facility (Texas) in H2 2026, its move up the value chain from oxide to finished magnet. (2026)
- Holds long-term supply deals with the US Department of Defense and Apple; signed a rare-earth refinery development deal in Saudi Arabia. (2026)
- China has recorded no terbium or dysprosium oxide shipments to Japan since November 2025, a sign of how tight the heavy rare-earth squeeze is. (2026)
- **Status check, reviewed August 11, 2026.** The April 2025 regime licensing dysprosium, terbium and the magnets containing them was **never suspended and remains fully in force**, so most permanent-magnet shipments still need a licence. The statutory target under the dual-use regulations is **45 working days, about nine weeks**, extendable with no fixed deadline where a security review applies; in practice, industry reporting shows heavy rare-earth approvals running **60 to 120 days or more**. New non-China magnet manufacturing capacity begins coming online in **summer 2026**, which is the first live test of this brain's "ex-China refining actually commissions" tripwire, though self-sufficiency remains years away. A **November 2026 enforcement date** applies to the extraterritorial provisions and is the next hard date to watch, alongside MP's own H2 2026 first magnet sales from Independence. (CSIS / IEA / China Briefing / trade compliance reporting, reviewed August 11, 2026)

## Why it matters for the future
If physical inputs are the real leverage once cognition is cheap, the magnet is one of the highest-leverage atoms in the building. Being named on China's export-control list is a backhanded confirmation that MP is strategic. The 10 to 20 year question is whether the West can stand up non-China refining and magnet capacity before the squeeze bites, given a new separation line takes 5 to 7 years.
