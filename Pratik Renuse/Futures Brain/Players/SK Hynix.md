## One line
A Korean memory maker that quietly became one of the two or three companies deciding how fast AI is allowed to grow.

## The chokepoint it owns
High-bandwidth memory (HBM), the stacked memory bolted next to an AI processor. A GPU starves without it. Only three firms can make it (SK Hynix, Samsung, Micron); SK Hynix makes about half the world's supply and is the one Nvidia leans on most.

## Key facts (dated)
- HBM is hard: 8 to 12 memory dies stacked, wired through the silicon, jammed millimetres from the processor; yields are low. (2026)
- HBM sold out across all three makers through 2026, reserved beyond. (2026)
- AI data centres consume ~70% of all memory chips produced globally. (2026 estimate)
- SK Hynix chairman: the wafer crunch could last until 2030. Samsung: shortages at least through 2027. (2026)
- SK Hynix stock at record highs on HBM demand. (2026)
- Shipped 12-layer HBM4E samples to major customers: 48GB, 16 Gbps/pin, ~20% better power efficiency and ~17% better heat resistance than HBM4. (June 18, 2026, SK hynix Newsroom)
- Filed for a US listing: ~$29B (45.45 trillion won) via ADRs on Nasdaq under 'SKHY', priced ~255,000 won (~$166), first trade targeted July 10 (tentative). Underwriters BofA, Citi, Goldman, JP Morgan. (June 24, 2026, Bloomberg / CNBC / SEC F-1)
- Debut confirmed for July 10, 2026, the largest ADR listing in market history (past Alibaba's $21.8B in 2014). Proceeds earmarked for a Yongin fab and advanced packaging capacity. Market cap ~$1.2 trillion, passing Samsung Electronics as Korea's most valuable listed company. (June 30 to July 6, 2026, Roic News / CNBC)
- The debut happened: priced $149 per ADR, raised $26.5B (largest US share sale ever by a foreign company, past Alibaba's $25B), ~7x oversubscribed, opened $170, closed day one at $168.01, up 13%. Provisional ticker SKHYV, permanent SKHY from July 13. Chairman to CNBC: "demand is enormous." (July 10, 2026, Bloomberg / CNBC / Quartz)
- Chairman Chey Tae-won: plan to roughly double output over five years; ~$67B capex 2024 to 2028. (June 2026)
- Korean shares fell ~12.5% on June 23 on a report Nvidia might cut Rubin output and SK Hynix might slow HBM4 expansion. Treat as rumor: Nvidia confirmed Vera Rubin in full production on June 1. (June 23, 2026)

## Why it matters for the future
Memory bandwidth, not raw compute, is the real wall. The maker of the best memory sets the speed limit on AI. The pattern repeats: the winner is the unglamorous supplier one floor below the conversation.

## Awareness watch
The June 24, 2026 US listing is the mainstreaming trigger: HBM and SK Hynix are now financial-press talking points, so retire as a fresh hook. Move one rung down for new hooks: packaging (TSMC CoWoS, gap narrowing 20% to 10% by end-2026 per TrendForce June 15) and the wiring between chips (see new [[Astera Labs]] note).
