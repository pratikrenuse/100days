#player
**Proves:** [[Energy is the Ceiling]]
**Awareness:** Building (energy investors know it; the wider audience does not)

## One line
The "boring" power business spun out of General Electric in 2024 that now sells the one thing every AI company is desperate for and cannot get: dispatchable electricity.

## The chokepoint it owns
Heavy-duty gas turbines, the machine that actually burns the gas powering 2026 data centres. The turbine, not the reactor, is the near-term bottleneck, and it is sold out.

## Key facts (dated)
- Q2 2026 (July 22, 2026): orders $24.2B, up 88% year over year. Total backlog a record $176B, up $13.0B sequentially across equipment and services. Gas Power equipment backlog plus slot reservation agreements went from 100 GW to 116 GW, with guidance raised to at least 125 GW by year-end 2026 (previously at least 110 GW). Revenue $11.1B, up 22%. Free cash flow $5.1B in the quarter, more than all of 2025. 2026 guidance raised to $45.5B to $46.5B revenue and $11.5B to $12.5B free cash flow. Management says backlog is on track for $200B in 2027. (GE Vernova press release and 8-K, July 22, 2026)
- Gas turbines sold out to 2029, with orders out to 2031; backlog ~100 GW (Q1 2026, up from 83 GW end-2025). Company guided to at least 110 GW of combined backlog and slot reservations by year-end 2026, since raised to 125 GW. (week of June 26, 2026, matterfact / Utility Dive / Industrial Info)
- New-bid turbine pricing running ~10% to 20% higher than prior-quarter backlog pricing. (June 2026)
- In Q1 2026, data centres drove ~$2.4B of equipment orders, more than all of 2025.
- Some turbine orders priced up to ~300% higher over a three-year window. (earlier 2026)
- Spun out of GE in April 2024 (carries the century-plus GE power heritage).

## Why it matters for the future
If electricity is the ceiling on AI, the makers and movers of power spend the next decade as the most strategic, least-discussed companies around. Peer set to track: Siemens Energy (record ~EUR 136B backlog), Mitsubishi Power, transformer makers.
