## One line
Jeff Dean left Google after twenty seven years to build a company whose product is not a model or a chip. It is the scientific method, running on its own.

## The chokepoint it owns
The proposal step. Robot labs already exist and can run experiments faster than humans can watch them. What has never been automated is the part before the experiment: deciding what to try next, reading the last result properly, and choosing the follow-up. Every "AI for science" result to date has had a person at that junction. Discovery Loop is built to remove them, and to run propose, run, evaluate and iterate thousands of times over. If that closes, the rate of discovery stops being bounded by how many capable researchers exist.

## Why it matters for the future
Note the ordering, because it is the whole story. They are not starting with cancer or batteries. They are starting by pointing the machine at machine learning research itself, which is the one field where the experiment is cheap, the feedback is immediate and the output is a better version of the machine. That is a compounding loop before it is a science company. The 10 to 20 year read: if the loop closes even weakly, the constraint on discovery moves off human researchers and onto the physical world, which means laboratory throughput, reagent supply and, eventually, electricity. Every thesis in this brain converges on that point. The honest counter is that this could spend two years improving its own models and never reach a wet lab, which would make it a compute story wearing a science costume.
