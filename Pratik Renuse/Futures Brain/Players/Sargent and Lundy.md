# Sargent and Lundy

#player
**Proves:** [[Energy is the Ceiling]]
**Awareness:** Obscure (a private Chicago engineering firm that has designed a large share of America's power plants for over a century, with essentially zero public profile)

## One line
The engineering firm that designs power plants nobody attributes to it, now drawing a nuclear hull that will take whichever reactor the buyer eventually picks.

## The chokepoint it owns
Design and licensing know-how, which is the part of nuclear that cannot be bought with capital or hurried with software. A reactor company can raise a billion dollars and still wait years for a design that a regulator will accept. Sargent and Lundy sells that step. The floating-platform deal extends the chokepoint one level higher: a standardised hull that accepts multiple reactor designs means the customer no longer has to pick a winner among reactor vendors before ordering the plant. That decouples the schedule of the building from the schedule of the technology inside it.

## Key facts (dated)
- Signed an MOU with Samsung Heavy Industries in Washington DC on July 21, 2026 to jointly develop the world's first reactor-agnostic floating small modular reactor platform. (World Nuclear News, Korea JoongAng Daily, Seoul Economic Daily, Splash247, July 21 to 23, 2026)
- Division of labour: Samsung Heavy brings offshore EPC and shipbuilding capability; Sargent and Lundy brings nuclear plant design and licensing. Scope covers development, licensing support, construction planning and maritime deployment, with explicit focus on the US market. (July 21, 2026)
- The compartmental hull is designed to accept multiple SMR designs rather than one vendor's, letting a buyer defer the reactor choice. (July 21 to 23, 2026)
- The move takes Samsung Heavy's floating SMR programme out of the concept-design phase it completed in 2025. (July 2026)
- Context: as of May 2026, thirteen announced offshore nuclear projects have committed more than 9.8 GW specifically to AI data-center infrastructure. (TechTimes, July 23, 2026)

## Why it matters for the future
Nuclear's problem was never physics, it was queueing: land, permits, grid interconnection, and a design a regulator has seen before. A floating plant sidesteps three of those, and a reactor-agnostic hull sidesteps the fourth by letting the vessel be built while the reactor market is still sorting itself out. If that works, the unit of nuclear deployment stops being a construction project and becomes a product with a delivery date, built in a shipyard on a shipyard's schedule. The 10 to 20 year read: the first gigawatt of AI power that arrives faster than the grid may well arrive by sea, and the firm that captures it is not the reactor brand on the outside, it is whoever owns the licensable design of the box.

## Awareness watch
Obscure and likely to stay there; this is a private engineering firm with no ticker and no consumer story. Bump only if a named reactor vendor and a named buyer attach to a specific hull, or if a US regulator opens a licensing pathway for floating plants.
