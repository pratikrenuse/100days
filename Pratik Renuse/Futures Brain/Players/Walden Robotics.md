#player
**Proves:** [[The Physical World Gets Automated]]
**Awareness:** Obscure (one week out of stealth; a single Bloomberg story and trade coverage, no retail awareness at all)

## One line
The Toyota Research Institute spinout that left stealth as a billion-dollar company on day one, with a robot already working shifts in a Toyota plant.

## The chokepoint it owns
Deployment credibility plus the deepest industrial backer bench in humanoids. Most humanoid startups raise on demos; Walden emerged with the customer, the factory, and the supply chain already attached. Its backers are its distribution: Toyota (factories to work in), Nvidia (compute and robot brains), Boeing (aerospace assembly), Samsung Ventures, and CoreWeave (training infrastructure). It is the clearest US answer yet to China's volume lead: skip the demo phase, launch inside the production line.

## Key facts (dated)
- Left stealth July 15, 2026 with ~$300M in seed funding at a $1.1B valuation, one of the largest seed rounds on record. (Bloomberg, July 15, 2026)
- Led by Russ Tedrake, the MIT roboticist who ran Toyota Research Institute's robotics work; the company is a TRI spinout. (Bloomberg, July 15, 2026)
- Backers: Toyota, Nvidia, Boeing, Samsung Ventures, CoreWeave. (Bloomberg, July 15, 2026)
- A Walden robot is already working shifts at a North American Toyota plant, per the launch coverage. (Bloomberg, July 15, 2026; RoboZaps, July 19, 2026)
- Context: it launched into the biggest humanoid capital month ever, days after Agility's ~$2.5B SPAC (S-4 filed July 14) and LimX's second $200M round (July 14). (July 2026)

## Why it matters for the future
The humanoid race has split into two models: China scales volume and lists (Unitree, LimX), the US scales credibility and capital (Figure, Agility, now Walden). Walden is the purest bet that the winning robot company looks like an industrial supplier, born inside a carmaker's production system, not a viral demo channel. The 10 to 20 year read: if physical labour gets a software cost curve, the firms attached to real production lines from day one ride it first, and the backer list (car, chip, plane, cloud) reads like a map of who intends to own that curve.

## Awareness watch
Obscure and fresh. Watch for the first named product, the first non-Toyota customer, and any Series A valuation print. If Tedrake or the robot becomes a mainstream media fixture, bump to Building.
