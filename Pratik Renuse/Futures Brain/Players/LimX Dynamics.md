## Why it matters for the future
The EV playbook is repeating one industry over: first one listing opens the window (Unitree as the sector's Tesla-moment), then a queue of followers raises billions against the same story, and the capital flood lands in the shared supply chain of actuators, gears, and magnets. The 10 to 20 year read: most of the queue will not survive, but the financing wave they trigger builds the component base that makes cheap humanoids inevitable, exactly how China's EV shakeout left behind the world's densest battery and motor supply chain.
