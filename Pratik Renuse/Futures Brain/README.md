# Futures Brain

The engine behind the daily future-insight posts. Not a news folder. A map of where the world is heading in 10 to 20 years, and the specific, mostly unknown players who will quietly decide it.

## The one idea

Organise everything by **thesis**, not by topic. A topic is a pile of news. A thesis is a bet about the future, and a bet is something new information can confirm, sharpen, or break.

The filter that keeps this alive: a new item only earns a place here if it **moves a thesis** and can answer two questions, *what does this imply in 10 to 20 years*, and *which hidden player does it point to*. If it can't, it's noise. Drop it.

## How it's organised

- **Theses/** the spine. A few master arcs about the future, each a living note. This is the memory map.
- **Players/** the gold. The mostly unknown companies and people who own a chokepoint (think SK Hynix, not "memory"). Each links to the thesis it proves.
- **Sources/** the registry of future thinkers and feeds worth reading. Seeded with my picks, ready for yours.
- **Signals/** the inbox. Raw incoming items wait here until they get filed under a thesis or thrown out.
- **Posts/** output and the idea backlog. Published pieces plus the queue.
- **Style Guide.md** the locked voice, format, and rules every post must clear.

## How a post gets made

1. Scan Sources and Signals for the item that best advances a thesis.
2. Pull the **hidden player** it points to (the unknown name is the hook, the theme is just setup).
3. Write the **10 to 20 year implication** in the locked voice.
4. File the post, update the thesis note, and drop the awareness score on anything the crowd is starting to notice.

## The awareness rule (the edge)

Every thesis and player carries an **awareness score**: obscure, building, or mainstream. The engine fishes from the high-importance, low-awareness corner. The day the crowd catches on (memory stops being a secret), that node retires from the hook pool and we move to the next unknown. This is what keeps us early instead of trend-chasing.

## Scope

Frontier tech is the centre of gravity (AI, compute, energy, robotics, biology, space). Anything else can earn a place, as long as it genuinely reshapes the next 10 to 20 years.
