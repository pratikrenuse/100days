# Biology Becomes a Search Problem: The Long-Horizon Curve (deep research)

Built 2026-07-02 from a 2-agent sweep (AI drug discovery + protein design; DNA synthesis/storage + synbio). Supports [[Biology Becomes a Search Problem]]. Sources: Nature/Nature Medicine, DeepMind, Isomorphic, Twist, Atlas, McKinsey, market research. Speculation flagged.

**The one line:** AI has cracked reading and designing biology (structure prediction, de novo proteins), but the clinic and the write-cost of DNA are the walls. Discovery is getting fast; proof in humans, and cheap DNA writing, are not.

---

## AI DRUG DISCOVERY & PROTEIN DESIGN
**Dated milestones:** AlphaFold2 solves protein folding (2020) -> first AI-designed drug in trials, Exscientia (2020) -> AlphaFold DB hits 200M+ structures, a ~1,000x jump over the ~180,000 experimentally solved in 60 years (2022) -> RFdiffusion designs proteins from scratch (2023) -> AlphaFold3 adds DNA/RNA/ligands/antibodies (2024) -> Nobel Chemistry to Baker, Hassabis, Jumper (2024) -> Insilico's rentosertib posts the first Phase 2a clinical proof for a generative-AI drug, in Nature Medicine (June 2025) -> Insilico IPOs (Dec 2025) -> Isomorphic raises $2.1B, the largest AI-drug round ever (May 2026), first human trials targeted "by end of 2026."

- ~30 to 70 AI-discovered molecules are in trials. The strongest independent stat: AI-discovered drugs show ~80-90% Phase 1 success vs a ~40-65% historical baseline, BUT Phase 2 is only ~40% (same as everyone). Zero AI-designed drugs have been FDA approved yet.
- Speed: AI cuts discovery-to-candidate from ~4-5 years to as little as ~8-18 months; ~30-50% faster, up to ~50% cheaper (BCG). Baseline drug: ~$2.6B and 10-15 years.
- Market: ~$2-7B today -> ~$18-44B by 2035 (wide analyst spread). McKinsey: gen-AI could unlock $60-110B/yr for pharma.
- Bull: Hassabis says AI could help "solve all disease" in 10-20 years; a predictive "virtual cell" maybe within ~10.
- BEAR (important): ~90% clinical failure persists (Isomorphic's own CSO says so); the two biggest killers, target selection and human toxicity, are "not yet within AI's reach" (Derek Lowe). First AI drug (DSP-1181) was discontinued; BenevolentAI's failed Phase 2. Trial duration is fixed by human biology (~5 years minimum), so even perfect discovery can't compress it much.

## DNA SYNTHESIS ("writing DNA"), DATA STORAGE, SYNBIO
- Writing DNA cost: ~$20/base (1990) -> ~$0.20/bp (2000s) -> ~7 to 9 cents/bp today (Twist). KEY ASYMMETRY: reading DNA collapsed ~$300M/genome (2000) to ~$100-600 today; writing has fallen far slower. That gap gates everything.
- Enzymatic synthesis is the next paradigm (longer, cleaner strands): Ansa Biotechnologies shipped 50 kb sequence-perfect clonal DNA (Oct 2025); DNA Script sells a benchtop synthesizer. Still premium, not commodity.
- Twist Bioscience FY2025: record $376.6M (+20%), NGS prep now the biggest segment, gross margin ~51%, still lossmaking.
- DNA DATA STORAGE: density ~215,000 TB/gram (demonstrated) to ~455 EB/gram (theoretical); lasts thousands of years. BUT today it is ~$3,500 per MB and writes at ~2 KB/second, and needs ~100 MB/s to be viable (roughly 50,000x too slow). Atlas Data Storage spun out of Twist (May 2025), $155M seed, backers include Bezos Expeditions and In-Q-Tel (CIA-linked). DNA Data Storage Alliance = Illumina, Microsoft, Twist, Western Digital. It is a write-once, cold-archive bet, not a this-decade product.
- Synthetic biology market: ~$24-26B (2025) -> ~$100-240B by 2035 (17-26% CAGR, wide spread). Caution: the flagship pure-play Ginkgo Bioworks had to slash costs hard, a $100B+ market does not mean every company thrives.

## THE 10 / 20 / 30 SHAPE
- **~10 yrs (2035):** structure prediction + de novo design are already normal; AI molecules keep entering the clinic; the real threshold, an FDA-approved AI-designed drug, is plausibly late-2020s to 2030s. Synbio becomes a solid $100-240B market. DNA storage reaches a first niche archival use (speculative).
- **~20 yrs (2045):** if enzymatic write-cost/speed break through, DNA storage could serve broad enterprise archives; engineered cells for materials/food move from niche to meaningful. Contingent on breakthroughs not yet demonstrated.
- **~30 yrs (2055):** the "read + write DNA at commodity cost" endpoint, designer organisms, on-demand biomanufacturing, DNA as a real data tier. Entirely dependent on the write curve breaking, which by 2026 it has NOT.
