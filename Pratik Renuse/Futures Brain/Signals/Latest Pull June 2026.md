Touches the **compute and resources become geopolitics** thesis directly, and supports **atoms over algorithms**.
- **Energy and physical infrastructure are the real ceiling.** Gigawatt datacenters (OpenAI's Michigan Stargate, xAI Colossus), HBM supply limits, and China's high-voltage transmission dominance all frame buildout, not model cleverness, as the constraint. Squarely the **energy is the ceiling** thesis, reinforcing **atoms over algorithms**.
- **The bottleneck is sliding down the stack toward agents, orchestration, and cost.** Computer-use in Gemini Flash, Codex-as-workspace, orchestration models, Nvidia extending from chips into the agent stack, and Uber capping per-developer token spend all show the frontier moving from raw model IQ to agent reliability, integration, and unit economics. Maps to **the bottleneck slides down the stack**.
- **Biology and science are turning into search and reasoning problems.** GPT-5 cracking an immunology mystery, near-autonomous AI chemists, LifeSciBench, GPT-Rosalind, DeepMind's Co-Scientist rejuvenating cells, and scaling laws for protein folding all point the same way. Direct support for **biology becomes a search problem**.
- **The physical and institutional world is next to be automated, and safety is openly behind.** AI moving into military advising, UK housing-planning, home/apartment training data, and robotics in Europe show automation reaching into atoms and institutions, while Import AI's "alignment is not on track," DeepMind's agent-security roadmap, and recursive-self-improvement chatter show the control problem lagging. Touches **the physical world gets automated**, with a clear undercurrent that governance trails capability.
