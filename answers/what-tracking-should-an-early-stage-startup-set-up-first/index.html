What tracking should an early-stage startup set up first?

By Pratik Renuse · Updated 23 November 2026

Short answer

Before chasing traffic, install the basics: Google Analytics with conversions, Search Console, Tag Manager and the pixel of every ad platform you will use. If you sell online, connect the shop to Analytics and Ads. Add retargeting lists and use annotations for every change. It takes a few hours and turns every visitor into something you can learn from.

The standard first step in my mentoring

When I mentored seed-stage startups in the Newchip accelerator in 2020 and 2021, tracking was the third call of the programme, right after introductions and scope. In the 2023 Techstars Berlin programme it came up again with a founder whose platform had little traffic and no reliable data. My advice was the same: set up the basic tracking elements before you try to grow traffic.

The basic stack

  • Google Analytics, with conversion goals for the actions that matter, and ecommerce tracking if you sell online
  • Google Search Console, to see what people search before they reach you
  • Google Tag Manager, so you can add events without waiting for a developer
  • The Meta pixel, and the tags of any other ad platform you plan to use
  • Retargeting lists, created now so they fill up before you need them
  • For Shopify and similar shops: link the store to Analytics and Google Ads so sales are attributed

Fix the funnel steps

Ask your developer to track each step of the sign-up or checkout funnel. Without that, you see people arrive and some buy, but not where the rest leave.

Annotate everything

Always use annotations in Google Analytics: a campaign launch, a new landing page, a price change. Without them, a spike or a drop three months later is a mystery.

Sanity check the data

Look for numbers that cannot be true. I once found a payment gateway counted as a referral source, which made referrals look like a top channel. One filter fixed it.

Learning it yourself

A founder told me analytics felt beyond them. I told them it is like simple Excel. Two or three hours of YouTube basics is enough to read the essentials, and you should be able to read your own numbers.
