Questions founders ask me

How do I grow organic traffic when my startup blog gets very few visitors?

By Pratik Renuse · Updated 23 November 2026

Short answer

Start from the pages Google already likes. Find the pages that got the most traffic last week, rewrite and extend them, and share new articles once or twice a week on social and with your target community to earn the first backlinks. Make sure Search Console and Analytics are set up first, so you can see what worked.

The founder's problem

In the 2023 Techstars Berlin programme I mentored a founder who had taken over a software platform for therapists. The site had a blog, little traffic and no clear tracking. The question was where to start without hiring an SEO agency.

Step 1: set up the basics

Before writing anything, make sure Google Analytics, Search Console and Tag Manager are set up properly. Without them you cannot tell which change helped.

Step 2: find what already works

Open Analytics and look at the pages that got the most traffic in the last week. These pages already rank for something. They are your fastest wins.

  • Rewrite them so they answer the search intent better
  • Extend them with the questions your customers actually ask
  • Add a clear next step for the reader, such as a demo or a free trial

Improving a page that Google already shows is faster than waiting for a new page to be discovered.

Step 3: publish on a steady rhythm

Share new articles once or twice a week on your social platforms. A steady rhythm beats bursts of ten posts followed by silence.

Step 4: earn backlinks from your audience

Share your articles with the people you serve. For this founder that meant therapists. Some will link to a useful article from their own sites, which gives you the first backlinks without any outreach campaign.

Step 5: watch people, not just numbers

With little traffic, numbers swing wildly. At this stage you need more qualitative data than quantitative. Session recordings show whether readers scroll, click or leave.

Be patient

SEO is the long game. Expect three to six months before the effort shows. That is why I start with existing pages: they give early proof while the slower work builds.
