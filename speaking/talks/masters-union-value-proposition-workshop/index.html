Workshop

Value proposition workshop: build a product customers want

Event: Masters' Union founder workshopsHost: Masters' UnionWhere: Gurugram, IndiaWhen: 2023Audience: Student founders
The session

A founder workshop I run at Masters' Union, built on my own deck, Value proposition and customer interviews. Teams learn the lean feedback loop and the Jobs to be Done framework, then spend 30 minutes filling in the value proposition canvas for their own startup. The aim is to stop building on guesses about what customers want.

The problem it solves

Most student startups begin with a product idea and look for customers afterwards. The result is a feature list that nobody asked for. This workshop flips the order: start from the customer's job and pains, then check whether the product actually serves them.

Takeaways stated at the start

  • How to use the lean feedback loop: build a small thing, measure the response, learn, repeat
  • How to use the Jobs to be Done framework to understand why a customer would hire your product

The content

  • The value proposition canvas, the well known tool that sets the customer profile next to the value map
  • Writing a problem hypothesis you can test
  • Jobs to be Done: the functional, emotional and social job behind a purchase
  • Customer interviews that test the hypothesis instead of pitching the product

The exercise, 30 minutes

Each team makes a copy of the value proposition canvas and fills it in for their own startup. Then they duplicate the value map and fill in the product, the gains it creates and the pains it relieves. Teams compare the two sides and mark where the fit is weak. Those weak spots become the questions for their next customer interviews.

What I see most often

The most common gap I see in founder sessions, from Masters' Union to Techstars, is that customer interviews have not been done properly, so the pains column is full of the founder's assumptions. Teams that have spoken to five real customers fill it in with quotes. Teams that have not fill it with adjectives. That difference is the lesson.

I used the same tools with climate founders in the Seedstars CTCN programme in 2021, where I shared Isaac Jeffries' guide to the canvas and Brian Rhea's Jobs to be Done interview guide.
