Playbook

Set up a customer referral programme

By Pratik Renuse · Updated 23 November 2026

For: Subscription and fintech startups with happy early customersTime: Two weeks to set up, then monthly reviewTools: Referral links or codes, Email platform, CRM to track redemptions
In short

Give both the referrer and the new customer a reward, and make the reward something tied to your product rather than plain cash. Ask at the moment customers feel the value, make the link easy to share, and track redemptions from day one. At Bueno I learned that cash rewards did not move property owners much. The real barriers were timing and friction.

The steps

  1. Set the goal

    Name the target the programme serves. At Bueno the referral programme was part of the plan to reach our first thousand customers.

  2. Reward both sides

    Offer something to the person who refers and to the person who joins. Two sided rewards feel fair and get shared more.

  3. Tie rewards to the product

    Match the reward to what people value in your product. At Bueno we offered a free electricity overpayment check with a discount, or a free non-resident tax guide.

  4. Ask at the right moment

    Ask when the customer has just felt the value, for example after their first bill is sorted or their tax is filed, not at sign up.

  5. Remove friction

    Make the link easy to find in emails and the account, and the sign up for the friend as short as possible.

  6. Track and review

    Count links shared, redemptions and paying referrals every month. If uptake is low, look at timing and friction before raising the reward.

Common mistakes

  • Raising cash rewards when the real problem is timing
  • Asking for referrals before customers see value
  • Rewards nobody on the other side wants
  • No tracking, so nobody knows if it works

Where this comes from

I set up Bueno's referral programme with two sided, product linked rewards and reviewed what moved owners and what did not. See more.
