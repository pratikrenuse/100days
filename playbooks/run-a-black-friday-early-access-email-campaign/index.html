Playbook

Run a Black Friday early access email campaign

By Pratik Renuse · Updated 23 November 2026

For: Premium D2C brands with an email list that want Black Friday sales without looking cheapTime: One day to write, two days to runTools: An email platform such as Klaviyo, Founder quotes and customer or athlete proof, Translations reviewed by native speakers
In short

Day one, send a heads-up from the founders: tomorrow you get exclusive access before the public launch, limited to a fixed number of orders. Use the body to remind people why the product is worth it: quality, who uses it, who it is for, guarantees. Day two, send the access link. Then open the offer on the site. I ran this for VU, the Swiss goggle brand, in English and German.

The steps

  1. Set a real limit

    Choose a limit you will honour, such as the first 50 orders, and decide whether the discount is ever public. Fake scarcity damages a premium brand.

  2. Write the heads-up email

    From the founders, by first name. One line on the offer and the limit, then the reasons to want the product.

  3. Lead with quality

    One concrete material or engineering fact. For VU, neodymium magnets, an aerospace grade material and the strongest type of magnet.

  4. Add proof

    A quote from a credible user, a line from a respected review, an award.

  5. Say who it is for

    Not for everyone: for enthusiasts and professionals who want quality, fog free rides and a good fit.

  6. Close with risk reducers

    Lifetime guarantee and a 30 day full refund, then the sign off.

  7. Translate properly

    Write each language version with a native speaker. VU's German email used du and the tone of German ski communities.

  8. Send the access email and go public

    Day two, the access link. Then the public offer, so list members always feel first.

Common mistakes

  • Leading with the discount instead of the product
  • A limit that is not real
  • Machine translated emails for other markets
  • Sending the public offer the same day, which removes the reason to be on the list
